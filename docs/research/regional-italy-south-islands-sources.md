## Original cases

The Fiano case supplies common fruit and contrasting cellar treatments, with a stated customer preference. Its action tests that preference rather than ranking every steel or wood wine.

The Taurasi/Vulture case holds grape and vintage broadly similar but changes vineyard and maturation. Learners identify why this permits a useful comparison while retaining confounding variables; tannin alone does not prove origin.

The Etna case supplies documented extra costs and similar sensory evaluations. Cost recovery and demand are assessed separately; no real bottle price, market statistic or quality premium is asserted.

The Cannonau case supplies equal sugar with different acidity and phenolic readiness. Learners obtain missing measurements and assess block-specific decisions; waiting can alter more than one maturity measure. No universal harvest date or official viticultural recipe is supplied.
