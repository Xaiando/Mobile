## Primary evidence and boundaries

- [IVBAM Wine-Growing Region](https://vinhomadeira.com/o-vinho-madeira/regiao): poios are terraces; levadas distribute irrigation water; latada is horizontal training, with espalier discussed for gentler land. The page describes demanding hand harvesting across steep fragmented holdings. Labour, access, water availability and machine feasibility are parcel-specific checks; no universal rate or quality premium follows.
- [IVBAM Verdelho](https://vinhomadeira.com/o-vinho-madeira/castas/verdelho): off-dry association and a relative body/acidity comparison with Sercial.
- [IVBAM Boal](https://vinhomadeira.com/o-vinho-madeira/castas/boal): semi-sweet, medium-bodied, rich style with acidity/sweetness balance. Its comparison with Verdelho is indicative.

The variety pages' approximate growing elevations are descriptive. No elevation cutoff or residual-sugar band is taught as a legal requirement. A compatible sensory profile does not establish grape identity, a vineyard altitude, exact sugar or a quality score. No numeric planting or sugar rule has been added.
