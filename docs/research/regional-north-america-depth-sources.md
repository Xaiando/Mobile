## Independently checked course scope

The [WSET Diploma specification, August 2025](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/17609/wset_l4wines_specification_en_august-2025.pdf), printed page 12, names California, Oregon, Washington and New York for the USA and Ontario/British Columbia for Canada. The selected US content stays within those states. All 44 points map to **WSET L4 core, depth 3**; the 16 case points are L4 only.

The [WSET L3 specification, May 2022 issue 2](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf), printed pages 12–14, names Santa Cruz Mountains, Monterey, Santa Barbara, Lodi, Willamette, Columbia/Yakima and Finger Lakes, plus Niagara Peninsula/Okanagan and Canadian Vidal/Riesling. Lower mapping is editorial: 14 foundations are L3 core at depth 2 (Santa Barbara, Santa Lucia Highlands, Santa Cruz Mountains, Lodi, Red Mountain/Yakima context, Oregon production and Niagara bench physics). The other 14 are **secondary depth 2**: Amador, Walla Walla profile detail, Long Island and the eight further Canadian points. The Okanagan pair is additional local grape/site enrichment within a named broad L3 region, rather than a claim that these exact examples are separately required.

The [CMS Europe 2026/27 syllabus](https://courtofmastersommeliers.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Syllabus-202627-1.pdf), physical PDF page 20, was read with its column layout preserved. **Canada districts, varieties and Icewine, smaller California districts including San Joaquin/Sierra Foothills, and New York appear under Advanced**, not Certified. Therefore all Canadian and Long Island foundations, together with Lodi and Amador, are **CMS Certified secondary depth 2**: 16 points. The remaining 12 are core depth 2 support for principal California, Washington and Oregon comparisons. Mapping does not copy Advanced requirements into the Certified required scope. The 28 foundations all have both lower-track mappings; these are study judgements, not examining-body endorsement.
