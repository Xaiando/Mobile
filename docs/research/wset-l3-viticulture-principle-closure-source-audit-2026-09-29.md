The distractors deliberately test common overclaims: soil or stone directly determining flavour, site labels guaranteeing quality, a regional climate eliminating frost or drought, canopy work being universally prescribed, and varietal adaptation fixing harvest date or wine style. The source-backed key is conditional where the underlying evidence is conditional. All 40 have four distinct options; key positions are balanced 10/10/10/10, and no key is the uniquely longest option.

Coverage was measured on the release date. On the isolated base, WSET Level 3 viticulture had 184 of 342 core items with useful practice; this batch adds 40 to 224 of 342. Core viticulture principle gaps fall from 118 to 78 here; integrating the separately authored, non-overlapping 22 general-viticulture choices would bring that specific gap count to 56. The other viticulture gaps, especially unserved multi-action cases, need an appropriate reasoning format rather than arbitrary one-key choices.

Validation: dataset lint, aggregate authored-choice ingestion/regression, bundled coverage test, coverage baseline refresh and static analysis. Existing facts remain marked unverified pending qualified subject-matter review.
