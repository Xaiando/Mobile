# WSET geography interactions: registered coverage and next work

Read-only audit, 29 September 2026, of curriculum release `0.24.6`. Counts come from the per-track `items` in `build/coverage-0246-final.json`; registered facts come from the 151 includes in `assets/curriculum/curriculum.yaml`, and geometry from `assets/geography/manifest.yaml`. These are **app coverage measures**, not WSET assessment results. This audit did not run Flutter/Dart or inspect learner records.

## What the specifications require

- [WSET Level 1 Wines, Issue 1.2 (2022), LO2](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11682/wset_l1wines_spec_en_jun2022_issue12.pdf), p. 5, names examples such as Chablis and Burgundy for wine–grape/style recognition. It does not prescribe locating them on a map.
- [WSET Level 2 Wines, Issue 2.1 (April 2026), LO3](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/19132/wset_l2wines_specification_en_april2026_issue21.pdf), pp. 10–11, lists grape-linked GIs and Burgundy Premier Cru/Grand Cru labelling terms. Its criterion is to describe, compare and understand the specified wines/terms; a map-tap format is an app choice.
- [WSET Level 3 Wines, Issue 2 (May 2022), LO2](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf), pp. 11–14, expressly asks learners to **identify and locate** principal regions and explain style, quality and price. Its Burgundy list includes Bourgogne Côte d'Or, both Hautes Côtes and Bourgogne Côte Chalonnaise; its New World list includes Sonoma County, Coquimbo, Patagonia, Hemel-en-Aarde Wards, Barossa and Hunter Valley. It specifies knowledge, not this app's question format.
- [WSET Level 4 Diploma, Issue 1.4 (August 2025), D3](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/17609/wset_l4wines_specification_en_august-2025.pdf), p. 13, names principal regions and requires analysis of production, law, business, style, quality and price. It names China at country level and broad regions including Spain's Valencia/Murcia/Castilla-La Mancha/La Rioja/Navarra, South Africa's Olifants River and South Eastern Australia. It does not require a country-click exercise or make the app's `core` label official.

## Registered geometry and served questions

The registered curriculum has 1,515 `LOCATED_IN` items on 1,514 distinct subjects. **All 1,514 subjects have a geometry entry** in `assets/geography/manifest.yaml` (1,525 entries on 1,522 nodes). The generic `LOCATED_IN` map templates in `assets/curriculum/templates/map.yaml:10–12` provide `map_locate`, `map_identify` and `map_pair`; the measured release shows `map_locate` served for every WSET core location item below. Geometry presence alone does not prove any particular question is eligible or served, so the served counts are taken from the report.

L4 inherits the cumulative L3 mappings (`assets/curriculum/track_scope.yaml:882–883`); its equal location count does not mean L4-specific regional analysis is complete. The L3 Burgundy selector is at `track_scope.yaml:201`; New World selectors start at `:516` (USA), `:556` (Chile), `:576` (Argentina), `:594` (South Africa), `:610` (Australia) and `:632` (New Zealand). L2 required-origin selectors begin at `:3558`.

The named Burgundy/Chablis targets checked against L3 Range 1 have location items and geometry: `ki_wset_geo_bourgogne_cote_d_or_location` and the two Hautes Côtes/Côte Chalonnaise items in `assets/curriculum/areas/wset_required_geography.yaml:451–494`, with the Côte d'Or polygon at `assets/geography/manifest.yaml:1162`; Chablis, Petit Chablis, Chablis Premier Cru and Grand Cru location items are in `assets/curriculum/areas/france_atlas.yaml:2831–2862`, with area entries at `manifest.yaml:1324–1351,2386`. The Chablis subareas extend app practice beyond the single Chablis name in L3 Range 1; their presence should not be mistaken for a WSET mandate to locate every climat. Sample New World targets also have geometry, including Sonoma County (`manifest.yaml:14158`), Coquimbo (`:3925,13996`) and Patagonia (`:9487`). No missing registered map target emerged from this check.

**Geometry fidelity differs from question availability.** `ml_wset_required_gazetteer` in `manifest.yaml:468–480` is a point layer explicitly attributed as *orientation references*, not official wine boundaries or centroids. Sonoma County's entry at `:14158` is such a point. Bourgogne Côte d'Or and the Chablis entries use the French appellation area layer (`manifest.yaml:118–130`). A successful locate question on a marker establishes orientation practice, not boundary recognition.

## Highest-value interaction gaps

1. **Give map-only core locations another defensible family.** `lib/core/coverage/coverage_model.dart:186–191` calls an item useful only with an objective format and at least two families. `map_locate`, `map_identify` and `map_pair` are all spatial; 20 L2 and 54 L3/L4 core location items are therefore map-served but not useful by this app metric. Examples are `ki_wset_geo_sonoma_county_location` (`wset_required_geography.yaml:401`) and `ki_wset_geo_bourgogne_cote_d_or_location`, `ki_wset_geo_bourgogne_hautes_cotes_de_nuits_location`, `ki_wset_geo_bourgogne_hautes_cotes_de_beaune_location`, `ki_wset_geo_bourgogne_cote_chalonnaise_location` (`:451–494`). Add reviewed, unambiguous regional-hierarchy or location choices rather than assuming a generic MCQ always has unique distractors. This is an **app practice-breadth gap**, not absent Level 3 map coverage.
2. **Prioritize grape–origin and label meaning before finer geometry.** Official L2 LO3 lists Merlot–Stellenbosch and Chardonnay–Western Cape/Chablis. `ki_wset_origin_stellenbosch_merlot` and `ki_wset_origin_western_cape_chardonnay` (`assets/curriculum/areas/wset_grape_origin_contexts.yaml:245,257`) serve only recall (`flashcard`, `typed`) in L2/L3; `ki_chablis_grape` (`assets/curriculum/areas/france.yaml:111,451,459`) serves only recognition (`mcq`) at L2. Burgundy `ki_wset_eu_burgundy_premier_cru` and `ki_wset_eu_burgundy_grand_cru` (`assets/curriculum/areas/wset_regional_europe.yaml:960,969`) serve recall only in L2–L4. Add source-specific second-family application/recognition questions that teach the association or label meaning; a location map does not teach it by itself.
3. **Review L4 named-region importance.** `assets/curriculum/areas/diploma_regions.yaml:99–179,190–266` has map-served items for seven D3-named broad regions, yet the measured L4 track carries them as `secondary` through L3 mappings: `ki_diploma_valencian_community_location`, `ki_diploma_region_murcia_location`, `ki_diploma_castilla_la_mancha_location`, `ki_diploma_la_rioja_region_location`, `ki_diploma_navarra_region_location`, `ki_diploma_olifants_river_wine_location`, `ki_diploma_south_eastern_australia_location`. Review direct L4 `core` mappings if the app intends named D3 region orientation to count as core. D3's larger need remains analytical comparison, not a higher map-point count.
4. **Treat China as optional atlas context.** `n_geo_china` has a country polygon (`manifest.yaml:559`) and the `wset_l4.world.china` objective (`track_scope.yaml:1415`); its child `ki_cn_atlas_ningxia_location` and other regional items (`assets/curriculum/areas/china_atlas.yaml:93–135`) have served map practice. There is no direct `LOCATED_IN` item for `n_geo_china`, hence no country-level “Find China” prompt. This is a low-priority optional app interaction, **not** an official prescribed format or a reason to promote China into general-track core. Keep the existing L3/CMS and L4/D3 atlas treatment optional.

The next release should verify any added item against its own primary geographic/legal source, remeasure per-track *served* formats, and keep marker-based orientation separate from boundary-accurate mapping.
