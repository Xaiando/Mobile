## Editorial decisions and boundaries

- Port timing changes retained grape sugar. Ruby and Tawny are contrasted as maturation objectives, with separate bottle-ageing guidance for Vintage and some LBV. The English IVDP overview has an older category list and a misleading “only Port that ages in bottle” phrase; neither is repeated as an exclusive claim. No exhaustive current category list or legal ageing minimum is authored.
- Sherry facts distinguish flor metabolism from alcoholic fermentation. Flor uses oxygen and ethanol, produces acetaldehyde and consumes glycerol; the qualified body explanation is a mechanism rather than a chemical tasting diagnosis. The original weak-flor case keeps an air space while investigating nutrients and cellar conditions. It does not prescribe filling every butt or imply oxygen alone restores flor.
- The Sherry Consejo process pages describe traditional fortification. This file does not assert that every possible present-day Sherry must receive spirit or set a legal alcohol threshold. Oloroso richness is distinguished from sweetness. Cream is given as a **common** dry-plus-sweet blending route; the source itself also allows other sweetening materials, so the row never says that Pedro Ximénez is compulsory.
- IVBAM's English page has a mistranslated canteiro caption suggesting bottle storage; its process paragraph clearly specifies casks. Only the cask explanation is used. University of Madeira documentation supplies cellar-location and evaporation management. No universal quality ranking between estufagem and canteiro, artificial “caramelisation at a precise temperature”, or immediate readiness claim is made.
- Roussillon's public press pack supplies mutage, liquid versus skin-contact production and young versus oxidative VDN maturation. It is a technical source for these mechanisms, not authority for an unverified 2026 appellation limit. The fresh Muscat case leaves room for intentionally mature Muscat styles.
- Chambers and Pfeiffer practices remain explicitly producer-specific. Neutral spirit, old large casks and parcel bench trials are documented examples rather than mandatory procedures for every Rutherglen producer. Classification depends on richness, complexity and intensity as well as age; no average-age number or a single mandatory solera design is taught.
