# Executive Summary compared with the current app

Checked on **27 September 2026**, against all six pages of the supplied **Executive Summary.pdf** and the local working tree's curriculum release **0.11.0**.

The PDF substantially understates the implemented engineering and learning system. Its strongest remaining finding is the need for complete, qualified-review content. The current app has a working scheduler, question engine, maps, tasting, journal, learner progress, data controls and CI configuration. It is still an incomplete WSET Level 4 Diploma study companion.

This comparison concerns the **local app**, including the latest uncommitted continuation. It does not establish that GitHub, an installed device build or a published product contains those changes. The PDF's action points and roadmap are assessed as proposals, not instructions to change the app.

## Important corrections to the PDF's baseline

- The code is **Flutter/Dart, Riverpod and Drift/SQLite**, rather than the PDF's speculative JavaScript/React Native/Vercel architecture. Questions are generated locally from authored facts and templates; runtime AI generation is not part of this app.
- There are **1,744 canonical knowledge items**, generating **9,583 question instances and 31 exercise pools**. Generated question instances are not independently authored facts. The PDF's 46-item observation describes an early seed, not the current release.
- **1,424 items are location facts: 81.7% of the fact count.** The authored domains contain 1,645 geography items, 79 viticulture items and 20 winemaking items. There are no business-domain items. Volume therefore does not establish balanced Diploma coverage or a percentage of the syllabus completed.
- **All 1,744 items remain unverified.** A cited primary source and a passing validator do not substitute for qualified review. Lint also reports 1,719 uncurated relation dates and 111 structural relations needing curator review.
- The agreed whole-companion target is **WSET Level 4 Diploma**, with CMS Certified support. The PDF's assumption that all CMS levels and separate wine/spirits/sake qualifications are mandatory launch scope is not established. CMS Certified beverage/service requirements still need their own authored content; they should not be dismissed merely because the principal target is Diploma in Wines.
- The PDF mixes Cellar/Cork Dork branding, trial/subscription prices and external marketing counts into its discussion of this repository. Its citation tokens do not provide usable source URLs. Those external product assertions were not used as this app's requirements or completion evidence.

## Remaining Diploma content

The [unit audit](wset-level-4-gap-audit.md) remains the appropriate content baseline:

| Unit | Current support | Missing work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | Foundational climate, soil, grape, hazard and selected production/legal facts | Coherent vineyard/winery explanations, decision cases, faults and effects on style, quality and cost |
| D2 | No authored business-domain facts | Markets, costs, distribution, commercial structures and marketing analysis |
| D3 | Extensive reference geography and selected grape/wine-law facts | Regional style, production, quality and commercial comparisons; deeper physical geography and analytical tasting |
| D4 | Sparkling-region references and selected legal/method facts | Systematic methods, styles, economics, regional comparisons and tasting |
| D5 | Fortified-region references and selected legal/style foundations | Complete styles, maturation/blending choices, business comparisons and tasting |
| D6 | No authored research-training material or assignment workspace | Source evaluation, evidence organisation, argument development and original writing support |

Short written answers currently use learner self-checking against factual key points. They do not assess essay quality or Diploma arguments. Higher-order reasoning, service/pairing cases, label interpretation, numeric exercises and certification rehearsal presets remain backlog work. Geography still needs hierarchy sequences, neighbours, ordering, physical-geography/deduction drills and broader grape-growing clues.

The PDF's Barbaresco wording concern deserves qualified review: the authored assertion correctly identifies exclusively Nebbiolo, while the generic principal-grape template asks about a principal variety. Replacing that template everywhere with "required grape" would incorrectly change the meaning of other specifications. Wording should follow each fact's legal distinction, using original questions rather than copied examination wording.

## Licensing correction in the PDF

Page 5 describes OSM data as CC-BY-SA. OpenStreetMap's official notice distinguishes its **ODbL data licence** from **CC BY-SA documentation**. [Official copyright and licence notice](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), checked 27 September 2026. This app's current licensed geometry pipeline does not bundle OSM data; its source rules favour verified openly licensed snapshots and disclosed reference points. The PDF's suggestion to draw unavailable legal regions manually conflicts with those source/provenance rules.

## Current validation and release limits

The [continuation handoff](codex-handoff.md) records 741 unique Flutter cases covered across the first full run and focused rechecks, clean analysis, 20 passing geography pipeline tests and a byte-identical offline geometry rebuild. The first full run passed 718 of 740 cases; an attribution expectation and 21 progress timing failures were corrected. Then 104 feature/progress/geometry tests and 20 explicit progress tests passed, including a new refresh regression. The complete suite was not repeated after that fix.

The verified web output is `build/new-world-web`; all 68 curriculum/geography/progress asset files and the SQLite/Drift runtime files match their inputs. A successful web build does not establish full offline browser reopening. Newest local changes have not been committed/pushed, checked by remote CI, packaged for native installation or published to stores.

## How the PDF's roadmap should change

The proposed eight-week estimate is not grounded in the current repository, qualified-review throughput or the Diploma content gap. Several scheduled engineering tasks already exist, while content authoring/review cannot be assumed to fit its final two weeks.

The dependency order supported by this audit is:

1. Define and author the missing Diploma and CMS Certified content, with qualified review of the existing material; curate dates and unresolved source qualifications.
2. Add analytical/written/tasting and advanced map exercises around that content. Expand progress/analytics without treating available-material mastery as full qualification readiness.
3. Complete device QA, a final uninterrupted test run, newest remote CI and the chosen native/store release gates.
4. Resolve the business model and web-offline release target before scheduling accounts, subscriptions, telemetry or browser-shell caching. These are product choices, not work authorized merely by the PDF.

The current app is therefore **well beyond the technical prototype described by the PDF, but still incomplete as a full Diploma learning product**. The principal remaining investment is accurate, balanced, reviewed study material and the exercises needed to practise it.
