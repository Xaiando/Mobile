# WSET Level 3 geography: remaining principle practice — 29 September 2026

The [WSET Level 3 Award in Wines specification, Issue 2 (2022)](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf) defines the track's study scope. It is not the factual answer key. This batch adds 40 original, item-specific `authored_choice` comparisons to previously unserved WSET_L3 core geography `PRINCIPLE_EXPLANATION` assertions. These questions concern site conditions, grape identity and roles, regional style, or protected-origin interpretation. They do not replace the atlas: existing map questions and geometry are unchanged.

The before/after coverage reports for the same 2026-09-29 dataset show geography core useful practice rising **942 → 982 of 1,060** and the whole WSET_L3 track rising **1,585 → 1,625 of 2,232 core facts**. Because these assertions are shared by tracks, the CMS Certified geography count also rises **745 → 761 of 776** and WSET_L4 geography **948 → 988 of 1,110**; WSET_L1/L2 do not change. This is the app's served-format metric, not independent proof of exam readiness, content correctness, a learner's mastery, or an official WSET award. Every new assertion remains `unverified` in the bundle pending qualified review.

The 40 keyed answer positions are 10/10/10/10, with four distinct options per item. None of the keys is the unique shortest or longest answer. An editorial pass replaced generic absolute-word distractors with concrete alternative grapes, regions and production routes. In the draft, 88 of 120 incorrect answers had `always`/`never`-style cues; the revised bank has 14 of 120, versus one keyed answer. These mechanical checks are useful but do not establish psychometric difficulty. A wine educator should assess ambiguity and factual currency before marking these items verified.

The Austrian Wine PDF and several producer/consortium pages were intermittently unavailable to direct automated opening; official-domain indexed excerpts or alternative official pages confirmed the bounded parts used. The broad Greece classification page was blocked by a challenge, so the three specific Wines of Greece origin pages were checked for the regional pairing. The Muscadet choice combines two citations attached to the same assertion: its `sourceCitationId` points to the grape account, while the separate InterLoire citation supports climate. No question claims a compulsory bottle style from regional averages. The linked Castilla y León specification is a dated regulatory text; current legal use should be checked during expert review.

## Remaining WSET_L3 geography core gaps

The same coverage report leaves **78** geography core facts without the app's second served family:

| Relation | Count | What remains |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `LOCATED_IN` | 35 | Spatial map service exists, but no separate meaningful family is yet counted. Preserve the clickable map questions. |
| `PRINCIPLE_EXPLANATION` | 8 | Further evidence or question design is needed; IDs below. |
| `CASE_ACTION` / `CASE_LIMITATION` / `CASE_REASON` / `CASE_TRADEOFF` | 11 / 10 / 8 / 3 | Existing scenario/recall content remains; 32 cases need separate graded application practice. |
| `AWARDED_BY` / `PRIVATE_CLASSIFICATION_OF` / `RESERVED_FOR_REGION` | 1 / 1 / 1 | VDP/Grosses Gewächs classification and Valpolicella Ripasso protected-origin distinctions need carefully bounded questions. |

The eight unserved principles are `ki_reg_fe_beaujolais_parcels`, `ki_reg_fs_anjou_oceanic`, `ki_reg_fs_saumur_season`, `ki_reg_isi_salice_styles`, `ki_wset_role_castilla_styles`, `ki_wset_role_dornfelder_vine`, `ki_wset_role_friuli_cellar_cost`, and `ki_wset_role_saumur_champigny_cellar`. The first four had inaccessible or insufficiently specific source views during this batch; the latter four involve more contextual winemaking or cost decisions that did not support a clear source-bounded single best answer within the 40-item limit. They are explicitly left open rather than rewarded with ambiguous generic choices.
