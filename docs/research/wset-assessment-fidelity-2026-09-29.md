# Level 3 practice mix and paired tasting evidence

29 September 2026. The [WSET Level 3 Award in Wines specification, Issue 2](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf), pp. 25–26, assigns written questions 1–2 to still-wine factors and regional application, question 3 to those topics with customer advice, and question 4 to sparkling/fortified wine with customer advice. It requires a 30-minute blind tasting of one white and one red still wine. WSET sets and marks those examinations; this app provides original, self-reviewed practice and does not assign an official mark.

The original Level 3 rehearsal bank now has version `2026.09.29-original.5`. Its four written prompts are drawn from a fixed study mix: two still-wine/vineyard, one service, and one sparkling/fortified. Every current prompt has one group, and starting a rehearsal fails if any group lacks current mapped prompts. Saved version `.4` and older attempts remain readable because the added group and blueprint fields are optional in snapshots. The mix ensures those broad subjects occur in every practice, but the current prompts and criteria do not reproduce WSET's exact multi-part 25-mark written structure or 70/20/10 mark distribution.

A completed timed pair now counts toward the Level 3 **app practice milestone** only when both wines have their required observations/evidence and their recorded colours are one white and one red. The unrestricted pair recorder still saves other combinations. The setup asks for two still wines, and the finished screen explains whether a pair counts. The app cannot verify the identity or still-wine status of a physical bottle and does not grade sensory accuracy; educators and the official WSET assessment remain necessary for those judgments.
