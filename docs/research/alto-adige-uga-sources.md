# Alto Adige additional geographical units — first five markers

Authored 27 September 2026 on `grok/alto-adige-ugas`. This does not finish the list in the decree. All new items stay `unverified`.

## Legal list

The consolidated disciplinare attached to the decree of 8 October 2024, published in the Gazzetta Ufficiale on 17 October 2024 (24A05379), article 7.2, lets Alto Adige / Südtirol / Südtiroler be followed by named additional geographical units. Each name is tied there to one or more municipalities. Article 2.6 repeats the same names when it limits the grapes. The markers below use article 7.2 for the place and do not copy a syllabus.

The press count of 86 units is not the legal text used here. A unit is mapped only when GeoNames gives that exact name, or an alternate name, inside the municipality article 7.2 names.

## Rejected this pass

- **Girlan-Gschleier, Penon-Kofl, Penon-Hofstatt, Gries-Moritzing and Salurn-Pfatten.** Separate legal units. They do not inherit the neighbouring marker.
- **Goldrain.** GeoNames 7290533 is Coldrano / Goldrain in Laces, admin3 021037, not Cortaccia.
- **Pardell.** GeoNames 8966474 is in admin3 021033, Funes-Villnöss. Article 7.2 places Pardell in Chiusa, admin3 021022.
- **Eggen.** San Nicolò d'Ega is admin3 021059, not Cortaccia.
- **Graun.** Curon Venosta is not the Graun in Cortaccia.
- **Missiano, Monticolo, Mazzone, Gleno and Sirmiano.** They sit in Appiano, Appiano, Egna, Montagna and Nalles, but the dump does not give the German unit name as the name or an alternate. They are not treated as Missian, Montiggl, Mazon, Glen or Sirmian.
- **Zona Artigianale Kalditsch.** An industrial-area name is not the Kalditsch unit.
- **Municipal seats named like a whole commune** (Ritten/Renon, Karneid/Cornedo, and the town of Kaltern, Tramin, Eppan, Montan or Neumarkt) were not reused for a smaller unit.

The other article 7.2 names had no same-name populated place or peak in the named municipality. They stay unmapped.
