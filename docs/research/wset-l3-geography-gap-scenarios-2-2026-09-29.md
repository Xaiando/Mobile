# WSET Level 3 geography practice, second batch — 29 September 2026

The [WSET Level 3 Award in Wines specification, Issue 2 (2022)](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf), Unit 1 Learning Outcome 2, sets the study scope. It is not a factual answer source. These 40 original scenarios use distinct, previously unserved WSET_L3 core geography `PRINCIPLE_EXPLANATION` items on top of commit `b6a64fb`: 39 are directly mapped Level 3 core and one US origin-scale item is inherited Level 2 core. All 40 use existing item-linked primary citations, drawn from **34 distinct URLs**. They do not add knowledge assertions, mappings, map geometry, or progress requirements. They do not alter the 54 `LOCATED_IN` items that already report spatial service.

Each item has four specific alternatives and an explanation. The keyed positions are balanced 10/10/10/10; answers are never uniquely shortest or longest. These checks reduce mechanical clues but do not establish psychometric quality or guarantee that every distractor is equally difficult. The items ask for bounded site, grape, origin, or sourcing inferences and avoid claiming that a regional name alone proves a bottle's quality or price.

Argentina's linked law view is explicitly the original 1999 text, so the question stays with the protected-category distinction corroborated by the INV department, rather than interpreting every current legal detail. IPONZ states that “live” includes pending statuses. Legal applications, source wording and distractor clarity need manual wine-educator review. **All items remain expert-unverified.**
