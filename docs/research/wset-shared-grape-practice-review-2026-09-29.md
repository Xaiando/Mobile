# Review of 20 shared Level 2 and Level 3 grape choices

29 September 2026. These 20 choices are registered in `assets/curriculum/templates/wset_shared_grape_choice.yaml` for existing Level 2 and Level 3 core facts. They were compared independently with each item's current `assertion_text` and attached citation before registration. The facts remain `unverified` pending qualified review.

Source access: the Wine Australia Chardonnay, Cabernet Sauvignon and Pinot Noir pages returned HTTP 200 through a direct HTML fetch and contained the passages used below; the web reader timed out for those three pages, although its search index also showed their official content. The Wine Australia Sémillon page, CMS Europe PDF, WSET Level 2 session-plan PDF, Soave regulations, Plantgrape pages, Italian consortium pages, Pinotage Association page and Wines of Argentina page opened in the web reader. No secondary source was used to justify a key. Source links and the exact locators are also retained in `source-index.json` and `source-review-audit.md`.

| `ki_wset_grape_chardonnay_profile` | [Wine Australia Chardonnay](https://www.wineaustralia.com/market-insights/regions-and-varieties/chardonnay), styles: cooler climate tends toward higher acid, while warmer climate tends toward fuller body. **Revised** to test that exact two-site contrast from the graded assertion. The former key additionally required lighter body at the cooler site, which the source supports but the assertion did not state directly. Only one of the four new comparisons has both directions right. |
| `ki_wset_grape_cabernet_sauvignon_profile` | [Wine Australia Cabernet Sauvignon](https://www.wineaustralia.com/market-insights/regions-and-varieties/cabernet-sauvignon), Coonawarra: firm tannin without inherent hardness or astringency accompanies the named black fruits. **Keep**; the other textures contradict that bounded profile. |
| `ki_wset_grape_pinot_noir_profile` | [Wine Australia Pinot Noir](https://www.wineaustralia.com/market-insights/regions-and-varieties/pinot-noir), styles: cherry/strawberry and light-to-medium body. **Keep**; the dark-fruit/full-body alternatives are not its stated typical style. The prompt specifies still wine. |
| `ki_wset_grape_nebbiolo_profile` | [CMS Europe markers PDF, p. 8](https://courtofmastersommeliers.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Grape-Markers-25.2.pdf): medium ruby-garnet can coexist with medium-to-high acid and high tannin. **Revised** the key from categorical “high acidity” to the source's “medium-to-high acidity” and made the low-tannin alternative parallel. | `ki_wset_grape_carmenere_profile` | [CMS Europe markers PDF, p. 8](https://courtofmastersommeliers.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Grape-Markers-25.2.pdf): ripe dark fruit, pepper/green pyrazine and medium tannin in the Chile example. **Keep**; “common” limits the inference, and no other complete combination fits. |
| `ki_wset_grape_pinot_noir_variation` | [Wine Australia Pinot Noir](https://www.wineaustralia.com/market-insights/regions-and-varieties/pinot-noir), maturation: excess new oak can obscure fruit. **Keep**; older/neutral vessel alternatives do not describe the cited excess. |
| `ki_wset_grape_garganega_variation` | [Soave consortium regulations](https://www.ilsoave.com/en/regulations/), Recioto DOCG: grape drying is specified and the wine is sweet. **Keep**; drying precedes pressing, while the other listed steps are not the Recioto-specific route. The explanation correctly distinguishes drying from the subsequent fermentation outcome. |
| `ki_wset_structure_grenache_structure` | [Plantgrape Grenache](https://www.plantgrape.fr/fr/varietes/varietes-a-fruits/120), technological potential: very high sugar accumulation and generally low acidity. **Keep**; the four factorial options have one source-supported pair. |
| `ki_wset_structure_tempranillo_structure` | [Plantgrape Tempranillo](https://www.plantgrape.fr/fr/varietes/varietes-a-fruits/269), technological potential: acid shortage in hot zones. **Revised** the key from “hot inland” to “hot growing zones”, the scope directly stated by Plantgrape. | `ki_wset_structure_barbera_structure` | [Barbera d'Asti consortium press kit, p. 5](https://www.viniastimonferrato.it/wp-content/uploads/Press-Kit-2019.pdf): high fixed acid, abundant anthocyanin and low grape tannin. **Keep**; the four combinations yield one source-supported key. This describes grape tannin, not every finished wine's extracted or barrel tannin. |
| `ki_wset_structure_sangiovese_structure` | [Consorzio Vino Chianti Sangiovese](https://www.consorziovinochianti.it/il-sangiovese-e-la-sua-corte/): marked acidity and a tannic framework. **Keep**; “evident tannin” is the closest supported pairing; slight-tannin options are not the consortium's description. |
| `ki_wset_structure_pinotage_structure` | [Pinotage Association vinification](https://pinotage.co.za/about/vinification/), pressing/skin contact: frequent early pump-overs or punch-downs are linked to less and softer tannin. **Revised** to ask for that actual directional result. The previous vineyard/berry-colour distractors were too easy; the new four tannin combinations are parallel and have one supported key. |
| `ki_wset_structure_carmenere_structure` | [Plantgrape Carménère](https://www.plantgrape.fr/fr/varietes/varietes-a-fruits/55), technological potential: vegetal character can dominate without good maturity. **Keep**; oak and bottle-age notes are independent processes, not the stated under-ripeness risk. |
| `ki_wset_structure_malbec_structure` | [Wines of Argentina Malbec descriptors](https://blog.winesofargentina.com/destacadas/ten-key-descriptors-for-todays-malbec/): cool sites are associated with floral notes; elevated cool sites with freshness, while warm sites show jam/spice/balsamic tendencies. **Keep**; “otherwise comparable” and “may” avoid a guaranteed outcome. |
| `ki_wset_structure_chenin_blanc_structure` | [Plantgrape Chenin](https://www.plantgrape.fr/fr/varietes/varietes-a-fruits/68), technological potential: dry, sparkling and sweet styles are listed. **Keep**; prompt asks for the documented combination and no other whole option is in that list. |
| `ki_wset_structure_semillon_structure` | [Wine Australia Sémillon](https://www.wineaustralia.com/market-insights/regions-and-varieties/semillon), Hunter Valley: early dry stainless-steel fermentation makes a light, lemony, high-acid young wine. **Keep**; honey is a later development, and the other alternatives introduce unsupported oak/weight/acidity. |
| `ki_wset_structure_viognier_structure` | [Plantgrape Viognier](https://www.plantgrape.fr/fr/varietes/varietes-a-fruits/288), technological potential: high sugar accumulation, broad/unctuous body and somewhat limited acid under favourable conditions. **Keep**; key is a tendency at suitable ripeness, not a required specification. |
| `ki_wset_structure_syrah_vineyard` | [Plantgrape Syrah](https://www.plantgrape.fr/fr/varietes/varietes-a-fruits/267), cultivation: the brief optimum includes sugar, acid, berry/bunch condition and berry tasting. **Revised** distractors so all four sets include sugar and acidity; only the key includes the additional physical and sensory checks recommended by Plantgrape. |
| `ki_wset_structure_cabernet_sauvignon_ageing` | [WSET Level 2 session plans, pp. 46–47](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/13022/wset_l2wines_sessionplans_en_may2023_issue2.pdf): acidity, tannin and concentrated fruit support long ageing. **Keep**; given both examples already have tannin/colour, fruit concentration plus acid is the relevant extra evidence. This does not guarantee improvement for an individual bottle. |
| `ki_wset_structure_merlot_ageing` | [WSET Level 2 session plans, p. 46](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/13022/wset_l2wines_sessionplans_en_may2023_issue2.pdf): lighter Merlot needs little extended maturation; very good/outstanding fuller Merlot may age. **Keep**; the specified concentrated/balanced/structured candidate is stronger than a light ready-now wine, soft tannin alone or deep colour alone. |
