# D4/D5 original written rehearsal extension

30 September 2026. Written bank 1.1.0 appends three original prompts and twelve learner self-review criteria to each of D4 and D5. The D1/D2 units retain their exact prior parsed content, question IDs and 90/60-minute deadlines.

The [August 2025 WSET Diploma specification, Issue 1.4](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/17609/wset_l4wines_specification_en_august-2025.pdf), printed pages 15 and 17, publishes a 90-minute unit assessment covering written responses and three-wine tasting. It does not publish a 45-minute writing allocation. Our 45-minute presets are app-authored writing-only practice, labelled that way on their screens and entry buttons. Existing physical flights remain separate. These fictional prompts, reflective criteria and saved participation are not WSET exam questions, a copied tasting grid, official marking or proof of unit completion.
