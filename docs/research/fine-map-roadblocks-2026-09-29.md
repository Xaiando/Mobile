# Fine-map roadblocks checked 29 September 2026

The three remaining Vino Nobile di Montepulciano Pievi and Paardeberg South remain **unmapped**. Their legal names and parent regions are established, but this review found no independently reusable, georeferenced interior marker or ward polygon suitable for the companion's existing CC0/CC-BY atlas snapshots. No marker was moved, guessed, or copied from a visual map.

## Sant'Ilario, Cerliana and Valardegna

[Italy's 5 February 2025 product specification, Annex A](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/gu/2025/02/05/29/sg/pdf) defines each unit in words (printed pages 13–14). Sant'Ilario starts at the Fosso Salcheto/railway junction and follows the Montepulciano municipal border, Torrente Parce, Fosso Scarpaia, and Fosso Marmo. Cerliana runs from Bivio di Nottola via SP 326, via di Montenero, Fosso Salcheto, Antica Chiusina, and other roads/cadastral-sheet edges. Valardegna runs from Bivio di San Biagio along the town edge, Antica Chiusina, Fosso Marmo, Fontelellera/Gonzeto roads, and SS 146. The [Consorzio's Pieve guide](https://www.consorziovinonobile.it/pieve/) describes Cerliana northeast of town, Sant'Ilario east/southeast, and Valardegna against the town's southeast side. Its press-kit diagram is useful for orientation but is not georeferenced, reusable geometry.

The [Regione Toscana CTR10K toponym download](https://dati.toscana.it/dataset/top10k) was checked rather than relying on its catalog license label. The downloaded ZIP (10,501,528 bytes; SHA-256 df515c9fdb74ddff0e1eb69505a96c5116eff033acae52a75fd9afdf998daebd) contains licenza_toponomastica_ctr10k.pdf, which expressly licenses its EPSG:3003 shapefile **CC BY-SA 3.0**. In Montepulciano it records Ciarlana hamlet/farm labels, Torrente Ciarliana and P. Vallardegna/Villardegna: older spelling variants, not the current legal Pieve names. It has no Sant'Ilario point there; the technical-map label at one historical Podere S. Ilario site is P. Sant'Agnese. None of its coordinates was copied into the atlas.

The [Regione Toscana RETORE download](https://geoblog.regione.toscana.it/-/ho-bisogno-dei-toponimi-di-una-zona-dove-posso-trovarli-) is more informative historically. The ZIP (84,576,276 bytes; SHA-256 74450d920046533e7715abf92370eace6739f59bb9647ccaedc81ab53d3f06a2) contains licenza_ccbysa.pdf, expressly **CC BY-SA 4.0**. In its Montepulciano records, Cerliana appears as historic/cadastral toponym RTR0520150133981_133981 and as variants Ciarliana/Ciarlana; Podere S. Ilario appears in RTR0520150178886_178886, while another historical IGM record (RTR0520150131273_131273) is now labelled P. Sant'Agnese in the technical map; Vallardegna/Villardegna appears in RTR0520150130148_130148 and RTR0520150129962_129962. These are point toponyms with name changes, not legal Pieve centroids or verified interior points. The ShareAlike license differs from the atlas's accepted coordinate licenses, so their coordinates were not imported. The [GeoNames Italy dump](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/IT.zip) has no Cerliana/Ciarliana/Vallardegna/Villardegna or Sant'Ilario record in Siena; its other Sant'Ilario records refer to different provinces. A producer address at Via Ciarliana also cannot establish which side of a Pieve boundary the address occupies: the [Consorzio lists La Ciarliana in both the Ciarliana and Ascianello areas](https://www.consorziovinonobile.it/la-ciarliana/).

**Next bounded action:** obtain from the Consorzio, Comune di Montepulciano, or another official publisher either a georeferenced Pieve layer with an atlas-compatible reuse license or independently licensed, precise named-place points that can be checked against the legal boundary description. Verify each proposed point lies inside its own unit and is distinct from sibling targets before authoring. If only ShareAlike geometry is available, first resolve whether a separate ShareAlike geographic database can legally coexist with the atlas; do not silently merge the data.

## Paardeberg South

The [SAWIS February 2026 production-area register](https://www.sawis.co.za/cert/download/Production_areas_-_Eng_%26_Afr_-_Feb26.pdf) lists Paardeberg South as a Swartland ward. [Board Notice 65 of 2018](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201805/41632bn65.pdf) says the proposed ward lies northwest of Voor-Paardeberg and west of Paardeberg and is defined on farm boundaries. It gives no coordinates or farm list. The [SAWIS production-area map portal](https://www.sawis.co.za/cert/productionareas.php) requests a login for the detailed boundaries. A [Wine Certification Authority proposed Moordkuil demarcation PDF](https://wineauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/103_Demarcations_-_Moordkuil_Proposed_Demarcation.pdf) labels Paardeberg South for context but does not supply a licensed georeferenced ward polygon or qualifying point. The applicant Sadie Family's general farm address does not itself prove that farm lies within this particular ward.

The earlier [GeoNames candidate review](../../tool/geography/new_world_oceania_africa_sources.md) remains valid: Staart van Paardeberg is on the wrong side of the mountain relative to the notice; Slent identifies itself as Voor-Paardeberg. Neither is a substitute for Paardeberg South. Public producer and route descriptions found in this pass describe the broad Paardeberg or Swartland landscape, not a specifically bounded South-ward farm.

**Next bounded action:** acquire a dated SAWIS/Wine Certification Authority ward boundary, farm list, or first-party confirmed in-ward property with independently reusable georeferenced coordinates. Check the candidate against the notice's northwest/west relationship and against the existing Paardeberg and Voor-Paardeberg markers. Until then the inventory should retain 101 of 102 wards, with Paardeberg South explicitly unknown.
