# WSET Level 2 viticulture choice audit — 29 September 2026

The [WSET Level 2 Award in Wines specification, Issue 2.1 (2026)](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/19132/wset_l2wines_specification_en_april2026_issue21.pdf), Learning Outcome 1, sets the study scope; it is not used as factual evidence for an answer. Its topics include grape parts and vine needs, ripening, environmental influences, growing choices and selected label language. The new questions are original app practice, not WSET exam items.

At PR31 head `74accc0`, coverage measured **113 WSET_L2 core viticulture items, 68 with useful practice**. Of 45 without useful practice, nine are `HAS_BERRY_COLOUR` identification facts. This batch supplies an item-specific, four-option authored choice for each of the remaining **36 existing `PRINCIPLE_EXPLANATION` items**. No knowledge item, assertion, certification mapping, progress requirement, map or examination claim is added. The 36 choices use 28 existing item-linked source records and one new AWRI source linked to the hand-selection item. The keyed answer is the graded assertion, not an adjacent regional fact. There are nine keyed answers in each option position, four distinct options per question, and no key that is the uniquely longest or shortest option. These mechanical checks do not establish item difficulty or eliminate all ambiguity.
