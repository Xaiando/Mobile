# Business and wine-service foundations — 27 September 2026

This continuation adds **26 business principles, 14 service principles and four original decision cases**. Each case has four separately cited items: action, reason, trade-off and limitation. The resulting 56 items use 100 new nodes, 111 explicit track mappings and 13 primary institutional or producer sources. All remain **unverified** pending qualified content review.

The authored files are [principles_business_service.yaml](../../assets/curriculum/areas/principles_business_service.yaml) and [business_service_cases.yaml](../../assets/curriculum/templates/business_service_cases.yaml). Shared principle/decision-case definitions and release integration belong to the root continuation. No database schema change is required.

The business material begins C5/C7 and DIP-2; it does not complete the Diploma business curriculum. It distinguishes costs, contribution, gross margin and markup, cash timing, inventory, customer research, pricing and exchange-rate exposure. There are no current prices, tax rates, legal requirements or personalised financial recommendations. Wine-service material covers heat/light exposure, controlled assessment, glassware and conditional handling of sediment and older bottles. It makes no universal food-pairing claim or exact service-temperature prescription.

## Track depth and case assumptions

Every business item maps explicitly to `WSET_L4`; Diploma-only marketing and exchange-rate concepts do not acquire automatic CMS or Level 3 parity. Selected cellar/accounting concepts also map to CMS Certified. Five wine-cost/price foundations map to Level 3 where they support understanding price. Service items map to CMS Certified and Level 3; seven elementary storage/assessment items also map to Level 2. Level 4 inherits those foundation mappings with their original depth. Four-point cases require depth 3; principle recall requires depth 2.

The discount case uses invented prices of 30 and a variable cost of 18 currency units per bottle. A 20% discount gives a price of 24, so contribution falls from 12 to 6: double the volume is required for the same contribution. These figures illustrate unchanged costs, spare capacity and unknown demand; the app does not machine-grade a numeric answer or forecast the sales response.

The cellar case separates profitable recorded sales from available cash and dated bills. The exporter case assumes a fixed foreign-currency invoice due in three months, home-currency costs and no offsetting foreign costs. The older-wine case expressly assumes fragility and visible sediment. Their actions are defensible under those conditions; they do not establish a unique choice for every situation.

Each scenario template is scoped to its own case node using `scope_node_ids`, with all four relation types named in `key_points`. Existing short-answer practice checks two to four authored points according to the learner's studied material. The learner marks covered points; the app does not claim professional marking, official exam scores or validation of physical service technique. Every item also retains flashcard/typed recall with short answer names and authored aliases. MCQs are disabled to avoid pretending that other plausible decisions are necessarily wrong.
