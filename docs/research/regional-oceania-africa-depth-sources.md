It contains 62 nodes, 44 relations, 44 normalized-distinct recall aliases, 100 canonical certification mappings, 15 new source records and 64 item-citation links to 20 sources. ## Editorial study-level mapping

All 44 points map to WSET Level 4 core at depth 3. The [pinned August 2025 Diploma specification](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/17609/wset_l4wines_specification_en_august-2025.pdf), printed pages 12–13, includes the relevant Australian state, New Zealand island and Western Cape regional context and environmental, production, blending and business comparison. The selected content is an original regional application of that framework, not a claim of official endorsement or completion of D3.

All 28 foundations also map to both lower tracks at depth 2. The [pinned May 2022 Level 3 specification](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf), printed pages 12 and 14, names Hunter Valley, McLaren Vale, Adelaide Hills, Nelson, Canterbury, Gisborne, Constantia, Paarl and Robertson, with grape scope used to distinguish supplementary details. Nineteen foundations are core and nine secondary. The [pinned CMS Europe 2026/27 syllabus](https://courtofmastersommeliers.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Syllabus-202627-1.pdf), PDF pages 18–19 and 22, covers these regional climate, grape, wine and production contexts; 20 foundations are core and eight secondary. All 16 case points are Diploma-only.

The eight secondary foundations shared by Level 3 and Certified are `ki_reg_oa_mclaren_whole_berries`, `ki_reg_oa_mclaren_gentle_cap`, `ki_reg_oa_mclaren_ceramic`, `ki_reg_oa_adelaide_m3_sources`, `ki_reg_oa_adelaide_m3_process`, `ki_reg_oa_constantia_assembly`, `ki_reg_oa_pinotage_sort_cap` and `ki_reg_oa_pinotage_oak_comparison`. They are named producer/vintage or narrowly specific production examples: useful enrichment, without making exact recipes mandatory lower-track outcomes. `ki_reg_oa_gisborne_patutahi` is also Level 3 secondary because its Gewurztraminer association extends the pinned New Zealand grape table; Certified retains this regional grape association as core. No foundation lacks a lower-track mapping. These importance assignments are editorial scope judgements, not an examining body's approval.

## Primary evidence actually read

All assertions cite primary regional-body, producer, research/extension or government material with item-level section locators. Regional descriptions support bounded site, grape and style observations; their promotional language does not establish universal quality, higher price, disease immunity or a soil-mineral flavour mechanism. Named production records support an example rather than all wines bearing the same origin. Original comparative inferences and missing-evidence boundaries are identified in locators and assertions.

## Access, vintage and inference limits

The regional, producer and scope pages were actually read through live primary browser text and PDFs. WOSA's correct full Winelands page failed in the browser but direct live HTTP 200 recovered and exposed the complete Paarl and Robertson paragraphs, which were read. An unused guessed parent route failed and is not cited. The cash-flow browser fetch intermittently failed; direct live HTTP 200 recovered its complete current sections. Ohio browser access returned 403, but its exact primary PDF had been recovered by direct HTTP 200 and all three pages actually read in this agent's immediately preceding regional review; the same canonical source is reused here.

The 2025 Limestone Hill primary PDF returns HTTP 200 but is an image-only page with no browser-extractable text. Its original embedded page image was extracted to an ignored build preview and visually read in full, including Climate, Irrigation and Vinification. The current shop page's vintage label had changed despite its older URL path and is not cited. A Primo sheet labelled 2019 contained a conflicting 2020 harvest description; it was discarded. The two coherent 2018 technical sheets support the expressly dated Fairview comparison. No historic cellar record is asserted to be a present compulsory practice. Yangarra's own ceramic wording takes precedence over an embedded critic's different vessel wording.

The four cases are original conditional still-wine scenarios with complete identical premises in the case node and template. Hunter's no-oak records support correcting an oak inference; changed vintage/parcel and incomplete storage records prevent an isolated ageing or quality experiment. The McLaren trial supplies a controlled split and an actual panel result, but does not prove a universal gentle-cap recipe or lower cost at commercial scale. Canterbury supplies documented sweetness and equal listed price; demand, stock quantity and payment timing remain missing. Robertson supplies repeated water/soil observations, two different block states, a hypothetical permitted allocation and stated early electricity cost; it supplies neither a dose nor harvest-ready fruit. These premises independently support action, reason, tradeoff and limitation without definite blind origins, universal quality/price ranks or reduced-crop superiority.
