# WSET Level 2/3 causal reasoning, 29 September 2026

The 0.24.13 curriculum adds two original two-step reasoning exercises, each mapped as core to WSET Levels 2, 3 and 4. They are scheduled only after the supporting fact has been studied. Each final answer has three distinct, directly contradicted alternatives with item-level source locators; generic relation distractors remain disabled. The hypothetical premises control major confounders and do not imply universal yields, final harvest weight or a fixed alcohol percentage.

The two exercised conclusions raise core reasoning from 0 to 2 in both Level 2 and Level 3; Level 4 rises from 8 to 10. The other eight authored items are supports or contradiction evidence, not separate reasoning targets. All ten remain `unverified` pending qualified wine-education review. This is an initial causal-practice slice, not completion of the broad reasoning-coverage threshold.

The existing Level 2 fermentation fact `ki_wset_found_fermentation` and related Level 3 facts `ki_win_grape_sugars` and `ki_win_alcoholic_products` are general `PRINCIPLE_EXPLANATION` recall. They do not state a matched-sugar comparison or form the required ordered `CAUSES_STATE → LEADS_TO` path. Existing frost facts such as `ki_vit_radiation_inversion` concern the conditions for frost protection, not a controlled fruiting-shoot and cluster-loss comparison. The reasoning renderer rejects principle-explanation stars and requires direct `CONTRADICTS` evidence for each alternative, so these conditional paths need their own support, conclusion and negative-evidence items. The four new core items are the two supports at depth 3 and the two final reasoning targets at depth 4. The six contradiction items are secondary at depth 2.
