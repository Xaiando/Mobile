## Scope and limitations

All 44 facts map directly to WSET Level 4 as core depth 3. The 26 general foundation points also map to WSET Level 3 and CMS Europe Certified as core depth 2. The two dated Bordeaux 2024 examples and all case points are Level 4 only. Track choices are editorial support, not examination-body approval. Root integration routes the full module explicitly to D3 despite mixed geography, viticulture, winemaking and business domains.

Comparisons apply public regional observations to original given conditions. Actions are defensible options, not unique best choices; samples and cuvée information remain necessary. MCQ is disabled because producer, site, vintage and objectives admit plausible alternatives. Answer names and aliases retain enough context for typed recall. Each case node name includes the complete scenario prompt so conditions also remain visible in generic flashcard and typed recall. These pools use learner self-checking and do not automatically assess prose or reproduce Diploma marking.

No fossil-shell-to-minerality mechanism is claimed. A soil name does not prove a flavour, grape or origin. Climate is qualified by local conditions, and bank, latitude, vessel or labour cost does not guarantee quality, drinking readiness or selling price. The Chablis page's implication that Meursault producers always use new oak is not adopted. The Rhône page's overbroad claim that Syrah is the only red variety in all Côtes du Rhône crus is not adopted. Its claim that all northern work is manual is narrowed to actual steep terrace constraints. Outdated prospective classification statements from the BIVB Mâconnais page are unused. The unresolved 2026 Rhône PDF was not needed or added.

This is a bounded French comparison addition. It does not complete France, D3, analytical tasting, legal registers, wine-market coverage or the Diploma. Further regional comparison work, qualified review and the rest of the study companion remain open. No WSET/CMS exam, proprietary grid, source PDF, map artwork or copied question is bundled.
