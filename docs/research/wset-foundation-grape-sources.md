# WSET beginner foundations and grape profiles: source ledger

Authored 27 September 2026. All new assertions are original paraphrases marked `unverified`; a qualified reviewer must check factual and pedagogical accuracy. The public WSET specifications establish study scope, not the correctness of a regional wine claim. This pack does not award a qualification or establish official exam readiness.

## Scope and assessment distinction

- WSET_L1: [June 2022 issue 1.2](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11682/wset_l1wines_spec_en_jun2022_issue12.pdf).
- WSET_L2: [2026 Issue 2.1](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/19132/wset_l2wines_specification_en_april2026_issue21.pdf).

Level 1 has a theory multiple-choice exam; its tasting teaching is not a practical tasting exam. Level 2 likewise assesses theory with multiple-choice questions. These original flashcard and explanation lessons are learning practice, not copied WSET exam or SAT material. Regional profiles and the broader vineyard, production, service, sparkling and fortified requirements must be checked in the integrated coverage matrix. Exact item evidence is in [the evidence JSON](wset-foundation-grape-evidence.json).

## Source interpretation

University extension, university ampelography, public regional boards and wine-country boards supply factual references. WSET knowledge-centre articles are cited as public educational explanations, not legal authority. Berry colours use ampelography or official variety registers; no final wine colour is inferred from berry colour alone. Descriptors are conditional tendencies and named regional examples, not fixed guarantees for every wine.

The public CMS deductive markers PDF contains an apparent copied Gamay description under its Corvina/Amarone section. That section was excluded: Corvina/Valpolicella/Amarone claims instead cite the Valpolicella consortium. CMS markers were used only for Carménère, Nebbiolo and Sangiovese. The Primitivo synonym follows UC Davis DNA identification and allows clonal differences. The proposed VIVC pages could not be read and were not cited. Some university sites and regional pages were accessible via indexed full primary-source text despite direct-fetch restrictions; locators identify the passage for review.
