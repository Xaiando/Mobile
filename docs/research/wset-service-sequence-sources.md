# Level 3 service-sequence sources

27 September 2026. The public [Level 3 specification](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf), Unit 1 LO5 Range 1, requires sequence of service. Existing lessons taught separate opening/glass/temperature operations but did not combine them or explain a multiple-wine order. Two original unverified points close that gap.

- **src_wset_sseq_bordeaux**, new: [CIVB How to serve a wine properly](https://www.bordeaux.com/en/wine-tasting-basics/serve/), accessed 27 September 2026. Supports moving from lighter to more powerful wines with intensity and texture considered, flexible colour order and careful handling of fragile bottles.
- **src_wset_srv_cms**, reused: [Court of Master Sommeliers Europe Service Standards 24.1](https://courtofmastersommeliers.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Service-Standards-24.1.pdf), PDF pages 4-5. Supports bottle identity confirmation, tasting pour, approval and full servings. Its formal table conventions are not imposed on every venue; the original app explanation covers the transferable sequence rather than reproducing the standard.
- **src_wset_sfault_common_faults**, reused from companion fault closure: [WSET public wine-fault advice](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2023/august/24/common-wine-faults-and-how-to-spot-them).
- Existing glassware sources support clean suitable glassware and leaving serving space.

Exact repairs are in `wset-service-sequence-evidence.json`. Root owns registration and the final requirement catalog.
