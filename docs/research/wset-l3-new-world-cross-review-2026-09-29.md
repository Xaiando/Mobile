## Repairs before integration

1. **`ki_wset_nw_south_africa_wo_levels` — name the legal class precisely.** The keyed `Unit > region > district > ward` uses bare “unit,” while the [linked WOSA Wine of Origin page](https://www.wosa.co.za/The-Industry/Wines-Of-Origin/Wine-Of-Origin-scheme/) also discusses smaller *estate* and *single-vineyard production units*. Its “geographical units / regions / districts / wards” paragraphs support the scale order, with a caveat that wards need not sit within a district. Replace the stem with **“In South Africa's Wine of Origin scheme, which ordering runs from broader to smaller named origin-area classes?”** and options (same key index 2): `Region > geographical unit > district > ward`; `Geographical unit > district > region > ward`; **`Geographical unit > region > district > ward`**; `Geographical unit > region > ward > district`. Explain that this orders classes by scale, not mandatory nesting of every ward.

2. **`ki_reg_sh_swartland_shade` — ambiguous “low foliage.”** The [linked WOSA Swartland Shiraz account](https://www.wosa.co.za/WOSA-News/Blogs/Cape-Chatter/Swartland-Shiraz/) says a bush-vine canopy acts like an umbrella over fruit. In the current options, “Low foliage shades fruit” can mean either foliage low to the ground (possibly true) or sparse foliage (false), competing with the keyed leafy-canopy option. Keep index 2 but replace all four options with: `Leaf removal exposes fruit to summer sun`; `Leaf removal shades fruit from summer sun`; **`A leafy bush-vine canopy shades fruit from summer sun`**; `A leafy bush-vine canopy exposes fruit to summer sun`.

3. **`ki_reg_am_patagonia_latitude` — avoid a second defensible geographic trend.** The [linked Patagonia climate account](https://patagonia.gob.ar/wp2019/en/organice-su-viaje/climate/) states average temperature declines southward but also distinguishes cold mountain conditions from milder coastal sites. “Generally cooler farther west” could be read as true of the Andes, even though the southward choice is the intended key. Replace the stem with **“Which two broad temperature factors does Patagonia Argentina describe?”** and options (key index 2): `Warmer southward; elevation has no effect`; `Warmer southward; elevation also matters`; **`Cooler southward; elevation also matters`**; `Cooler southward; elevation has no effect`. This follows the item's latitude-plus-altitude assertion without a competing westward claim.

4. **`ki_reg_sh_stellenbosch_aspect` — separate exposure from drainage.** The [linked Stellenbosch Cabernet brochure, “Major Factors,” PDF pp. 6 and 13–14](https://stellenboschcabernet.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/SC-E-brochure-4-1.pdf) attributes sun and sea-breeze exposure to aspect/slope, but drainage and nutrition to slope/**soil**. The current key, “Slope and aspect alter sun, breezes and drainage,” can imply that aspect directly determines drainage. Preserve the stem and index 0 with these options: **`Aspect/slope affect sun and breezes; slope/soil affect drainage`**; `Aspect/slope leave sun and breezes unchanged; slope/soil affect drainage`; `Aspect/slope affect sun and breezes; slope/soil leave drainage unchanged`; `Aspect/slope leave sun and breezes unchanged; slope/soil leave drainage unchanged`. Replace the explanation with **“The brochure links aspect and slope to sun and sea-breeze exposure, and slope and soil to drainage and nutrition.”**

5. **`ki_reg_sh_chenin_fresh` — make the explanation support the precise keyed aroma pair.** The current key is correct, but the explanation only says “fresh” versus “rich.” The [linked Chenin Blanc Association style indicator](https://www.chenin.co.za/styleindicator), opening paragraphs, specifically names fresh green apple/lime and rich baked pineapple/marmalade. Keep the choices and key; replace the explanation with **“The Chenin Blanc Association contrasts a fresh style with green apple and lime against a rich style with baked pineapple and marmalade.”**
