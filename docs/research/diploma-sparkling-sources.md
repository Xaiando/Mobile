## Scope and authoring choices

Thirty principles map to WSET Level 3 at depth 2. Seventeen foundations also map to Level 2 at depth 2, and 29 principles map to CMS Certified at depth 2. Eight advanced production/economic points and all 20 case facts map directly to WSET Level 4 at depth 3. Level 4 inherits the earlier foundations through the existing track hierarchy. These are editorial study selections, not endorsements from an examining body. The three business-domain principles concern automated riddling and purchased/headspace gas; the remaining 55 items use the winemaking domain.

All items disable MCQ because qualified process choices admit defensible alternatives. Brief answer nodes and useful synonyms support typed cited recall, while assertions carry the important conditions. Scenarios offer four action/reason/tradeoff/limitation facts; presentation can initially use fewer facts under the existing new-item budget.

The content distinguishes bottle, tank, transfer, ancestral and force-carbonation mechanisms. It covers base-wine composition, pressing, blending, optional MLF, lees/autolysis, riddling, disgorgement, dosage, gas solubility, closures, gushing, counter-pressure and selected operating costs. It does not equate a method with a universal quality rank, require all ancestral wine to retain sediment, confuse dosage with tirage, promise indefinite oxidation protection from lees, or treat a stable base wine as automatically representative after more fermentation alcohol.

The Wine Australia process overview is used for method descriptions, without its broad implication that all sparkling wine receives two fermentations. The older consumer guide was not used as a complete contemporary market census. The producer examples are kept to the described processes; Wolf Blass's general three-variety wording and statements implying continuous riddling during maturation are not adopted. Comité Champagne's pages are technical context here: legal grape lists, minimum ageing, sugar labels, bottling dates and press-yield limits are not authored from these pages. The AWRI engineering discussion establishes mechanisms and costs, not a retrofit recipe. Closure material is independently grounded in PE.DI first-party descriptions, its 9 July 2026 research-partner report, and the original Pons-Mercadé et al. study. The latter reports observations on Cava lees, not a universal maximum ageing period. Manufacturer claims are used for described mechanisms, without a product recommendation, legal pressure threshold or prediction for every wine.

No proprietary WSET/CMS question, syllabus sentence, marking scheme, source PDF, diagram or artwork is bundled. These original facts are a first sparkling-production extension, not complete D4 regional, commercial, tasting or expert-reviewed readiness.
