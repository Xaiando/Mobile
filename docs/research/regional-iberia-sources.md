# Iberian still-wine comparisons: source and review notes

Curated 27 September 2026. This module adds 14 paired regional principles (28 explanation points) and four original conditional cases (16 action/reason/tradeoff/limitation points). Spain has eight principles and two cases, Portugal six principles and two cases. There are 44 cited knowledge items, 62 nodes, 44 aliases, 74 item citations and 100 certification mappings. Domain allocation is geography 25, viticulture 6, winemaking 6 and business 7.

All 44 items await qualified review (`verification_status: unverified`). MCQs are disabled because qualified site explanations and case decisions admit defensible alternatives. All items map to WSET Level 4 at depth 3/core; the 28 foundations also map to WSET Level 3 and CMS Certified at depth 2/core. There are no lower-track exclusions. These are editorial study mappings, not claims of official qualification equivalence, complete examination coverage or learner certification.

The case premises are identical in each case node name and its fixed question template, so an individual flashcard or typed rubric point retains the hypothetical conditions. Dates record curation, not the date a regional condition began. No geometry, place nodes, geographic containment claims, exhaustive permitted-grape sets or new fortified/sparkling facts are introduced. ## Business evidence and original inference

Three existing canonical source IDs are reused rather than duplicated:

- `src_biz_au_pricing`: Australian Government [Choose a pricing strategy](https://business.gov.au/products-and-services/choose-a-pricing-strategy), supporting actual costs, competing offers, perceived value and market testing in the Priorat price and Baga inventory cases.
- `src_biz_victoria_pricing`: Business Victoria [Calculate your breakeven point, margin and markup](https://business.vic.gov.au/business-information/finance/pricing-for-profit/calculate-your-breakeven-point-margin-and-markup), supporting required-price arithmetic, separate from sensory quality or willingness to pay.
- `src_biz_au_cashflow`: Australian Government [Guide to managing cash flow](https://business.gov.au/guide/guide-to-managing-cash-flow), especially payment timing, inventory management and supplier terms. The Baga case's cash tied up in storage follows its explicit pay-now/sell-later premise; the source does not predict Portuguese wine sales or maturation.

The four cases are original teaching scenarios, not copied examinations or accounts of real producers. Comparative actions, tradeoffs, residual confounding and quality/price limitations are editorial reasoning from cited mechanisms and stated hypothetical conditions. Regional trade references are useful primary sources for local conditions and established grape/style associations, but promotional rankings, categorical quality guarantees, health claims and direct soil-to-flavour assertions have been excluded.
