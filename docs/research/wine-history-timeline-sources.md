# Chronological wine-history source audit

The additional pack is `assets/curriculum/areas/wine_history_timeline.yaml`: 23 explanation items, three four-part cases (35 items total), 20 source records and 70 secondary CMS Certified/Level 4 mappings. It supplements the 15 registered history items and the 57-item expanded course. None of its items is mapped to WSET Levels 1–3. Every item remains `unverified`; authored citations are not a qualified fact check.

The claims below were checked against the linked publisher pages on 28 September 2026. A museum object is evidence that a vessel existed by its catalogued date. A trade association's history is an account of its own industry's past. A law compilation has a date and jurisdiction. None of these alone establishes an invention date, a universal practice, or current law outside its named jurisdiction.

| Era or topic | Primary or institutional source | Narrow claim used | Limit retained |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Greek wine service | [Metropolitan Museum krater, object 31.11.11](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/253349) | Attic column-krater dated about 550 BCE; its catalog describes wine diluted with water in a krater at a Greek symposium | One vessel documents a pre-Roman service practice, not its invention date or how every Greek drank. |
| Roman transport | [Metropolitan Museum amphora, object 74.51.355](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/239940) | Late first to mid-second century CE Roman amphora type often used for wine export | One artifact is not every Roman vessel or wine style. |
| Medieval Burgundy | [UNESCO Burgundy Climats](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1425), [Burgundy Climats historic timeline](https://www.climats-bourgogne.com/en/historic-timeline_50.html) | Monastic and ducal influence on named parcels; twelfth-century Cistercian enclosed-vineyard and cellar model | No single founding year for the present château or direct equivalence to today's AOC boundaries. |
| 1152 Aquitaine | [Official Bordeaux wine-council account](https://www.bordeaux.com/fr/Journal/Education/10-anecdotes-sur-les-vins-de-Bordeaux-pour-l-apero) | Eleanor and Henry's union linked to Bordeaux's outward wine history | No shipping volume or claim that the marriage created the vineyard. |
| 1395 Burgundy rule and 1789 aftermath | [Burgundy Climats historic timeline](https://www.climats-bourgogne.com/en/historic-timeline_50.html) | Philip the Bold's Gamay restriction on his lands; redistribution of clergy and noble vineyards after the Revolution | The restriction is not a present ban on Gamay, and ownership change did not erase all parcels. |
| Glass and cork | [Corning bottle circa 1650–1660](https://glasscollection.cmog.org/objects/10921/wine-bottle-with-seal), [Corning corked bottle dated 1730](https://glasscollection.cmog.org/objects/12994/bottle) | Surviving English glass wine bottle, then a cork/foil/label/liquid example | Neither object dates the invention of bottles, corks or the disappearance of casks. |
| Cape foundation | [Wines of South Africa: Three Centuries of Cape Wine](https://www.wosa.co.za/the-industry/history/three-centuries-of-cape-wine/) | Dutch East India Company station in 1652; diary account of Cape-grape wine on 2 February 1659 | The diary entry is not the first wine ever made in Africa. |
| Cape origin scheme | [Wines of South Africa: Wine of Origin](https://www.wosa.co.za/The-Industry/Wines-Of-Origin/Introduction/) | System formulated in 1972 and officially instituted in 1973 | Distinguish a legal scheme from the 1659 production milestone. |
| Australia plantings | [Wine Australia: New South Wales](https://www.wineaustralia.com/market-insights/regions-and-varieties/new-south-wales-wines), [Wine Australia old-vines educator guide](https://www.wineaustralia.com/getmedia/df3ca7e9-0743-4036-abf5-be7c129a6589/AWD-Old-Vines-Educator-Guide.pdf?ext=.pdf) | First Fleet vines planted at Sydney Cove in 1788; Busby's later collection arrived in 1832 and circulated more widely | A planting is not a successful vintage; the two events are distinct. |
| Argentina colonial and nineteenth-century | [Argentine government wine history](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/pais/vino), [Argentine government Malbec history](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/malbec-tu-casa-siempre-fue-la-argentina-0) | Colonial-era production and spread by 1598; Mendoza's 17 April 1853 school proposal and Pouget's later European introductions | Malbec did not begin all Argentine viticulture. The 1853 date is the proposal date, not necessarily a harvest. |
| Pasteur and wine microbiology | [Institut Pasteur early years](https://www.pasteur.fr/en/about-us/early-years-1847-1862), [Institut Pasteur wine-disease account](https://www.pasteur.fr/en/whats-new/latest-news/features/louis-pasteur-universal-legacy) | 1857–1862 living-ferment work; Napoleon III's 1863 request; wine-preservation patent of 11 April 1865 | Pasteur did not invent fermentation, and pasteurization is rarely used for wine today because it can affect taste. |
| US AVAs | [TTB chronological AVA list](https://www.ttb.gov/regulated-commodities/beverage-alcohol/wine/ava-establishment-dates) | Augusta, Missouri listed 20 June 1980; Napa Valley 28 January 1981 | An AVA is an origin designation, not a tasting result or universal quality rank. |
| Australian GI law | [Wine Australia Act 2013, 21 February 2025 compilation](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A02362/2025-02-21/2025-02-21/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html) | Part VIB establishes and empowers a GI committee; sections 40N, 40P and 40Q | The short title does not date the origin of every Australian wine region. Recheck the current consolidation before treating it as current legal advice. |
| Chile 2024 announcement | [Chile agriculture-policy office](https://www.odepa.gob.cl/publicaciones/noticias/agro-en-la-prensa/ministro-valenzuela-anuncia-incorporacion-de-nuevas-variedades-y-nuevas-denominaciones-de-origen-para-el-vino-chileno) | Officials announced additions to decree 464 in July 2024 | Announcement is not a transcription of the amended decree or proof that a bottle exists. |
| Present day | [OIV agroecology resolution 680-2024](https://www.oiv.int/node/3804), [OIV state of the sector in 2025, published 12 May 2026](https://www.oiv.int/node/4879) | International recommendations name biodiversity, soil health and climate adaptation; OIV describes 2025 production, demand and trade pressures | Recommendations are not universal practice; a 2025 report is not a forecast for every future vintage. |

The companion's three cases ask learners to distinguish a Greek symposium krater from a later Roman wine-transport amphora, separate Roman and English artifact dates from invention myths, and keep the 1976 tasting apart from national origin-name systems. `assets/curriculum/templates/wine_history_course_cases.yaml` registers these three cases alongside eight scoped cases from the expanded course, for eleven short-answer templates. They use learner self-review, not automatic prose grading. The nine rows in `assets/curriculum/candidates/history_course_questions.yaml` remain editorial handoff data, not ingested templates.

The source-backed timeline is intentionally selective. It does not claim a complete history of every wine region, culture, technology or law through 2026. It adds a documented Greek wine-service milestone before the Roman material and addresses the identified medieval, packaging, Cape, Australian, Argentine, fermentation-science and present-day gaps without filling unsupported periods with invented dates.
