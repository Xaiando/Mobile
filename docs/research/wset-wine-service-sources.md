# Wine service and food interactions: Levels 1–3

Authored 27 September 2026 as part of the WSET completion ledger. This batch adds 72 original lessons in 24 teaching groups, including three conditional written cases. All remain `unverified`; primary-source checking is not qualified expert verification. Grok owns history and non-wine beverages separately.

## Teaching and level decisions

- Level 1 introduces storage, style-sensitive temperatures, glass hygiene, controlled still/sparkling opening, preservation, common faults, preference-led recommendations, food sweetness/acidity/salt/fat/chilli and responsible consumption. Levels 2–4 inherit these lessons.
- Level 1 also includes umami and flavour-intensity interactions. Explicit lower-level mappings added after independent scope review preserve the existing lessons. Level 2 adds sediment-aware decanting.
- Level 3 adds three original recommendation/service cases with four criteria each: action, mechanism, tradeoff and limitation. They use supplied observations and acknowledge alternatives. The existing self-assessment practice displays source-backed points; it does not mark arbitrary prose objectively.
- Generic principle recall serves explanatory groups. No automated generic MCQ distractors are generated for conditional recommendations. The separate original rehearsal bank supplies objective questions where one answer is defensible.
- Temperature ranges are approximate style guidelines, not legal limits. The Champagne body recommends 8–10°C; the Court publishes style-dependent ranges. Neither is presented as one mandatory temperature for every sparkling wine.
- Prescription-like shelf lives, product marketing promises, a guaranteed healthy drinking limit and rigid food/grape pairings are excluded. Opening fragments are distinct from chemical cork taint; normal tartrates/sediment are distinct from spoilage. Oxidative styles require context.

## Primary sources

Scope follows the current public [Level 1](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11682/wset_l1wines_spec_en_jun2022_issue12.pdf), [Level 2 Issue 2.1](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/19132/wset_l2wines_specification_en_april2026_issue21.pdf) and [Level 3 Issue 2](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf) specifications. These establish the teaching remit, not the factual citations for wine mechanisms. Each lesson carries its own factual source and locator in `wset_wine_service.yaml`.

The temperature/storage article supplies cool, steady, dark storage and the fuller red/white examples. The [Court’s public service standards](https://courtofmastersommeliers.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Service-Standards-24.1.pdf) supplies practical wine opening, clean glassware and light/sweet style temperatures; gendered service customs and its other-beverage sections are not imported. [Comité Champagne](https://www.champagne.fr/en/champagne-tasting/preparations-for-drinking-champagne) supplies controlled sparkling opening, glass choice and a proper sparkling stopper.

WSET’s public [tool guide](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2025/five-wine-tools-every-enthusiast-should-know), [decanting guide](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2024/july/21/frequently-asked-questions-about-serving-and-decanting-wine) and [preservation guide](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2023/march/07/the-best-ways-to-preserve-wine-after-opening) support concise operational lessons. Consumer gadget claims are not adopted as guaranteed usable life. Actual equipment must suit still or sparkling wine.

Separate public WSET articles support [individual preference and perceptual interactions](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2023/july/13/four-rules-to-masterful-food-and-wine-pairing), [umami and intensity](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2020/june/02/how-to-pair-wine-with-your-favourite-takeaway-meals), [sweetness and acidity](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2020/december/21/winter-wine-and-food-matching) and [spice and wine structure](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2026/how-to-pair-drinks-with-spice). The app examples and decision prompts are original applications, rather than copied suggested dishes.

[AWRI’s faults reference](https://www.awri.com.au/industry_support/winemaking_resources/sensory_assessment/recognition-of-wine-faults-and-taints/wine_faults/) supports contextual sensory fault recognition. [WHO’s alcohol factsheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/alcohol) and [WHO Europe’s cancer-risk explanation](https://www.who.int/europe/news/item/04-01-2023-no-level-of-alcohol-consumption-is-safe-for-our-health) support responsible consumption, driving/pregnancy and the absence of a risk-free health claim. The latter has a September 2026 correction; the corrected page was checked. No local age limit, permitted pour or personal medical instruction is invented.

## Review gates

Meaningful checks must demonstrate that these lessons are reachable at the intended cumulative level, each group is served with explanatory practice, case criteria share their subject and citations, and ambiguous conditional alternatives cannot become objective generic MCQs. Review also checks the mechanism and qualifications in each fact rather than accepting a source solely because it is primary. Final completion requires the wider requirement matrix, not this batch count alone.
