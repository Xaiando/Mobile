# WSET Level 3 viticulture application: first 40 general principles

Curated 29 September 2026 against the [WSET Level 3 Award in Wines specification, Issue 2](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf). Unit 1, Learning Outcome 1 requires explanation of how natural and human factors in the vineyard influence wine style, quality and price. Its range includes vine structure and growth cycle, sunlight and water, canopy management, harvest timing, frost, sunburn, phylloxera and fungal disease. The forty choices in `templates/wset_l3_viticulture_application_choice.yaml` serve forty existing WSET Level 3 **core** viticulture points (`ki_vit_*` without cases). They add no new claim or certification mapping. Each point remains `verification_status: unverified`; a qualified reviewer still needs to judge accuracy, emphasis and distractor quality.

The choices use only the source already cited by their individual knowledge item. The primary references and the bounded mechanism taken from each are:

Choices are original, site-conditional practice scenarios, not WSET examination questions. Four response positions appear ten times each. The canonical knowledge item, citation and track scope remain unchanged, so secondary Level 2 atlas/principle access does not become Level 2 core coverage. The baseline records useful practice, not knowledge mastery or official certification. The remaining WSET Level 3 core viticulture gaps, especially regional and case-rubric points, require further authored work.

At this branch's release baseline, useful WSET Level 3 **core** viticulture practice rises from **97/340 to 137/340**, leaving **203** core viticulture items without useful practice. Overall Level 3 core useful practice rises from **1,219/2,228 to 1,259/2,228**. Level 4 gains the same forty because those existing points are also core there. Level 1, Level 2 and CMS Certified core useful counts do not change. These are app coverage measurements rather than an examination-readiness estimate.
