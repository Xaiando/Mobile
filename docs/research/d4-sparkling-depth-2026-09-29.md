# D4 sparkling regional depth: source and scope audit

Researched 29 September 2026. The official [WSET Level 4 Diploma in Wines specification, Issue 1.4 (2025)](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/17609/wset_l4wines_specification_en_august-2025.pdf), especially D4 pp. 14–15 and assessment p. 26, sets scope only. These are original app questions, not WSET questions or marking guidance. D4 includes a closed-book open-response examination and a blind tasting of three sparkling wines; this content batch cannot assess either performance.

The batch adds **24 new regional/production principle assertions**, each with a separately authored four-option question, plus **4 complete-premise written decisions** with 4 individually cited criteria each. That is **28 distinct prompts and 40 new knowledge items**, all WSET Level 4 D4-only, core depth 3 and `unverified`. Four cases cover Champagne reserve allocation, 2025-harvest Cava Guarda Superior sourcing, Rive versus broad Prosecco buying, and English frost/reserve supply planning. Answers ask for an action, mechanism, commercial tradeoff and missing evidence. The scenarios, budgets, buyers and producer choices are hypothetical applications; citations support the mechanism or rule, not the fictional outcome.

## Boundaries and validation

Method, label, variety, origin and ageing provide hypotheses for style, input costs and stock time. They do not establish a tasting score or retail price for an untested bottle. Case price and stock tradeoffs are explicitly reasoned applications of the cited constraints; actual margins, supply and sensory results are missing by design. The D4 tasting objective remains planned, and `curriculumComplete` remains false. No map asset or geographic boundary is added. The 2025 Cava organic requirement and current Franciacorta/VDP rules should be rechecked at qualified expert review before publication as verified instruction.

Local validation: curriculum lint has zero errors (three existing-style curation warnings); the focused D4 test checks citations, L4-only scope, rubric completeness, ingestion and D4 progress; the combined authored-choice, production-curriculum, progress and bundled-coverage suite passed 44/44. The integrated release is `0.24.19`; its measured WSET Level 4 baseline is 4,012 mapped items, 2,738 core items and 1,332 core items with useful practice. These app counts do not certify Diploma completion.
