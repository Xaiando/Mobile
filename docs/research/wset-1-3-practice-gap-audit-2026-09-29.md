## Geography: map coverage versus analytical coverage

* The app's exact requirement dimensions ask for `map_locate` on 247 L2 and 461 L3 occurrences, resolving to **99 and 313 distinct item IDs**. All 99/313 had spatial serving in the item-level report, and the 0.24.6 baseline's spatial totals are unchanged (L2 115, L3 1,673). `assets/curriculum/templates/map.yaml` already offers locate, identify and pair formats; `assets/geography/manifest.yaml:468–491` adds cited orientation points, explicitly not legal wine boundaries. More pins should therefore follow a specific learner need, not be treated as the main remaining geography fix. WSET's specifications list origins and wine styles but do not require this app's map format.
* Of L2's **110** unserved geography core IDs, **79** are explanatory `production_principle` items without spatial practice and **20** are already **spatial-only** location items. Of L3's **322**, **226** are explanatory principles, **32** are case roles, and **54** are spatial-only locations. Most of the gain lies in source-bounded environment/grape/production/label/style comparisons. Map-only items need a meaningful second family if the app keeps its two-family policy; another map variant alone will not change the useful count.
* `assets/curriculum/areas/wset_required_geography.yaml` contains the remaining point/origin candidates; `assets/curriculum/areas/wset_regional_europe.yaml`, `wset_regional_new_world.yaml`, and the regional comparison packs contain the larger non-spatial application pool. The new 35-choice Level 2 grape expansion, 35-choice Level 2 remaining pack and 35-choice Level 3 New World expansion are **already registered**. Avoid recasting them as future work.

For multi-action `CASE_*` assertions, use a bounded premise/criterion-led self-review or a properly modelled reasoning exercise rather than a misleading one-key MCQ.
