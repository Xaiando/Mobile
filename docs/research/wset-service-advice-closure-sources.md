# Level 3 advice closure sources

27 September 2026. The critic found omitted contexts in the otherwise broad service pack: bitterness present in food, wine complexity alongside fruitiness, and occasion/individual sensitivities when recommending. Three original, unverified Level 3 explanations close these contexts without universal pairing rules.

Sources are reused, with no duplicate URL rows:

- **src_wset_taste_tannin**: [Wine Australia Tannin](https://www.wineaustralia.com/growing-making/vineyard-management/tannin), living educational reference. Distinguishes tannin-related bitterness and drying astringency.

- **src_wset_sfault_l3_spec**: [public Level 3 specification](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf), May 2022 Issue 2, Unit 1 LO5 Ranges 1 and 3. This row lives in the companion fault closure; both packs must register together.
- **src_wset_srv_food**: [WSET Four rules to masterful food and wine pairing](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2023/july/13/four-rules-to-masterful-food-and-wine-pairing), 13 July 2023. Supports differences between drinkers and the role of perception. The new recommendations and examples are original conditional applications.
- **src_wset_srv_sweet**: [WSET Winter wine and food matching](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2020/december/21/winter-wine-and-food-matching), 21 December 2020. Intense dishes can support complementary or contrasting choices.
- **src_wset_close_l2_session**: [WSET public Level 2 session plans](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/13022/wset_l2wines_sessionplans_en_may2023_issue2.pdf), May 2023 Issue 2. The public bitterness section supports individual sensory differences; no classroom exercise or official answer is copied.

The precise repairs are in `wset-service-advice-closure-evidence.json`. No official completion status or health advice is implied.
