# WSET Level 3 winemaking principle practice, 29 September 2026

The app's item-level coverage report at integration commit `737df85` found **209** WSET_L3 core winemaking items without useful practice: **141** `PRINCIPLE_EXPLANATION` items and **68** `CASE_*` roles (16 actions, 17 reasons, 18 tradeoffs, 17 limitations). Every gap had recall practice only. None was a spatial-only item. These are app coverage counts, not a claim about official exam completion; the mapped scope follows the [WSET Level 3 specification](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf).

`wset_l3_winemaking_principle_closure_40.yaml` adds 40 distinct, item-specific, scored choices to principles already supported by flashcard and typed recall: 25 fortified, 10 sparkling, two fault mechanisms, two cap operations and one Burgundy Climat production comparison. The options test mechanism, sequence, process or qualified inference without turning producer examples into universal requirements. Every choice cites a source already linked to its knowledge item; the [IVDP Port overview](https://www.ivdp.pt/en/wines/port-wines/introduction/), [Sherry Consejo ageing guidance](https://www.sherry.wine/sherry-wine/production/ageing), [Troon ancestral-method notes](https://troonvineyard.squarespace.com/pet-tannat), [Wolf Blass sparkling process](https://www.wolfblass.com/en-au/about/our-craft/sparkling.html) and [Chambers technical sheet](https://www.chambersrosewood.com.au/s/Tech-Sheets-Rutherglen-Muscat-750ml.pdf) are representative direct primary references. The Chambers citation now points to the producer's accessible PDF rather than a Wine Australia-hosted endpoint that returned HTTP 403 during this audit.

After the batch, L3 winemaking core useful practice is **282/451**, up from 242/451. **169** gaps remain: **101** principles plus the same **68** case roles. The parallel case-criteria work owns those roles; this batch contains no case IDs. The 40 new IDs also improve CMS, Level 2 and Diploma recognition where those tracks map them, but only three CMS core IDs were unserved. Existing typed and written practice remains in place. The new answers and distractors remain marked for qualified subject review.
