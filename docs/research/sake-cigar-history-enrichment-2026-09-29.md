# Optional sake, cigar and Amarna bridge — source audit

Release candidate 0.24.15 adds 19 unverified assertions and six focused exercises: three source-cited four-choice questions and three self-reviewed, four-role written cases. The sake and cigar material is mapped only as **secondary CMS study enrichment**. It is not mapped to any WSET Wines level and is not represented as a CMS Certified examination requirement. The history bridge follows the existing secondary history treatment on CMS and Diploma; WSET Levels 1–3 do not inherit it. These mappings make the material discoverable without claiming credential completion.

| Strand | Primary source and supported claim | Boundary retained in the lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sake and food | [Japan Sake and Shochu Makers Association, Basic Sake Food Pairings](https://japansake.or.jp/sake/en/basic/sake-food-pairing/) suggests matching flavour intensity or aroma, adjusting temperature and considering contrast. | A suggested pair is a starting trial for the stated dish and stocked sake, never a guaranteed match or a fixed rule based on a legal sake class. The guest may choose differently. The [CMS Europe 2026/27 syllabus](https://courtofmastersommeliers.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Syllabus-202627-1.pdf) puts sake pairing in Advanced, so this is user-requested enrichment rather than a Certified requirement. |
| Cigar identity | [Habanos' Authenticity Check](https://www.habanos.com/en/authenticity-check/) describes a code check for packaging claimed to be Habanos; its [introduction](https://www.habanos.com/en/introduction/) bounds the protected name. | A Habanos code is not a universal non-Cuban cigar identifier; a wrapper colour, band or shape cannot itself prove origin or current condition. |
| Cigar care and service | [Habanos' storage guidance](https://www.habanos.com/en/keeping-habanos/) recommends 16–18°C, 65–70% relative humidity and warns against a household fridge. Its [cutting and lighting guidance](https://www.habanos.com/en/choosing-cutting-lighting-and-smoking/) explains the cap and odourless flame. | These are manufacturer-specific care choices, conditional on guest consent and local/venue rules. The existing [WHO tobacco fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/tobacco) supports the explicit warning that tobacco use and second-hand smoke are harmful; care does not make smoking safe. |
| Egyptian bridge | [UCL Digital Egypt's Amarna label catalog](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/amarna/winelabels.html) describes inscriptions with year, vintner and vineyard; its [Amarna chronology](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/amarna/index.html) places Akhenaten around 1351–1334 BCE. The [British Museum Egypt and Sudan Newsletter 6](https://www.britishmuseum.org/sites/default/files/2020-06/British-Museum_Egypt_Sudan_Newsletter6_2019_0.pdf) describes fragment EA 59337 as a regnal-year-six Aten-estate wine label with a head vintner. | This is a surviving inscription and one inscribed fragment. The British Museum PDF was visible through its indexed museum excerpt but its direct fetch returned 403 during this audit; its exact page was therefore not asserted. The case contrasts existing cited Areni installation evidence with the Amarna inscription and the Met's circa 550 BCE Greek krater. It does not infer the first wine, first label, continuous practice or a modern appellation law. |

Every new item has an item-specific source link and locator, remains `unverified`, and uses `mcq_disabled` except for three explicitly authored alternatives. The written cases ask the learner to assess an action, reason, tradeoff and limit; a new learner initially sees a smaller pool by design and can encounter all four after studying the points. The remaining spirits, liqueur and Irish Whiskey currency findings in `history-beverage-remaining-audit-2026-09-29.md` are separate work and are not counted as closed here.
