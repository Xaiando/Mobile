# WSET Level 2 application practice, release 0.24.10

This release adds 30 item-specific four-option questions to existing Level 2 core facts. It covers service decisions, regionally important grapes, grape structure, vineyard effects, weather and climate, Asti production, and dry Oloroso. The questions exercise a bounded conclusion from a stated observation rather than treating a grape or GI name as a fixed tasting result. The topics were checked against the [WSET Level 2 Wines specification, 2026 Issue 2.1](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/19132/wset_l2wines_specification_en_april2026_issue21.pdf). The tartrate example is service enrichment supported by [WSET's decanting FAQ](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2024/july/21/frequently-asked-questions-about-serving-and-decanting-wine), not one of the specification's named faults.

The linked source for Garganega was repaired from a dead press-kit URL to the [Soave consortium's grape page](https://www.ilsoave.com/le-uve/), which gives the fruit, white-flower and almond profile. The water-concentration and frozen-pressing questions now cite [WSET's Level 2 educator plans, pp. 24–25](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/13022/wset_l2wines_sessionplans_en_may2023_issue2.pdf); the aroma-versus-sugar answer cites [WSET's sensory explanation](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2026/strawberry-pepper-and-petrol-why-wine-smells-the-way-it-does). The vintage-variation answer cites [Wine Australia's vineyard account](https://www.wineaustralia.com/growing-making/vineyard-management), which directly connects seasonal weather with yield and fruit-quality variation. Existing attached citations cover Corvina's role in Valpolicella as well as the young wine's style. The revision removed claims that a single cited page did not establish, including an Asti grape synonym and Cream Sherry's blending details.
