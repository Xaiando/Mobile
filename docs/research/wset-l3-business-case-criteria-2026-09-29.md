# WSET Level 3 four-criterion business cases — 29 September 2026

The [WSET Level 3 Award in Wines specification, Issue 2](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf) includes explanation of how vineyard, winery, law and commerce can affect wine style, quality and price. It also has short written answers. That is the scope for these study exercises, not a claim that the app supplies official examination marking or a credential.

## Coverage-policy audit and practice design

The prior release had **39 unserved core business `CASE_*` assertions** in 19 existing scenario subjects: 19 tradeoffs, eight actions, eight limitations and four reasons. Every one of those 19 subjects already had a complete four-role, source-cited written case (`CASE_ACTION`, `CASE_REASON`, `CASE_TRADEOFF`, `CASE_LIMITATION`) and all **76** role items were mapped as WSET_L3 core at depth 2. Its `short_answer` exercise lets a learner write a response and self-check the four key points. `flashcard` and `typed` are also recall. The [coverage rule](../../assets/curriculum/coverage_policy.yaml) counts useful practice only with an objective format and two format families, so another self-checked written prompt would not close the measured gap. The existing `reasoning` mode assesses an authored causal path, which these four decision roles do not establish.

The new `case_criteria` exercise presents the **whole existing premise**, shuffles four distinct response summaries already stored as the criterion objects alongside **two authored, case-specific false claims**, and asks the learner to select one defensible summary for each role. Each false claim has a short explanation tied to a contradiction or overreach in that case. The format grades all four item IDs separately, rejecting partial or duplicate assignments, then reveals each full assertion, its existing publisher citation(s), and the reasons the two extra claims fail. This is objective **structured scenario practice**, alongside the separate open-written case; it does not grade the prose of the learner's recommendation. The 19 subject IDs are explicit in one scoped template, and validation refuses missing roles, duplicate summaries, missing distractor explanations or uncited items. The planner offers a case only after the other three roles have been studied and only when all four are current and mapped at the required depth for the active track. A partial cross-track case cannot be scheduled or counted. The existing coverage thresholds and useful-practice rule are unchanged; policy records that no generic `case_criteria` generator applies to other relation types or unscoped cases.

## Existing primary-source ledger

No knowledge assertions, source-citation links or certification mappings change. The 76 case items already carry **35 distinct source URLs**, of which **17** occur on the 39 business-domain items. The new exercise fetches those item-specific citations into feedback. Illustrative buyer offers, costs, trial outcomes, deadlines and site observations are premises of the original cases, not market-wide claims from these sources. Publisher pages for the WSET specification, Australian Government cash flow/pricing, Business Victoria, Tokaj maturing and Wines of Portugal Baga were checked online during this batch. The remaining 30 URLs are pre-existing item citations and were audited for linkage and HTTPS, not revalidated as a complete contemporary legal or regional survey. The named sources are primary bodies, regulators, producer-origin material or extension publications as appropriate to the item; a producer example stays an example. The case explanations and role summaries remain **expert-unverified** and should receive wine-educator review for nuance and cue difficulty.

## Measured result and limit

On the clean `e67cb9e` base, WSET_L3 business core useful practice rises from **60 to 99 of 99**. The 39 business case gaps are all served with `short_answer` plus `case_criteria`; the other 37 co-role items in those complete cases also gain structured practice. WSET_L3 overall core useful practice rises from **1,576 to 1,652 of 2,232**, and WSET_L4 inherits the same 76 role-practice gains. This is a coverage-tool measure of available practice, not a finding of student mastery, exam equivalence, complete Level 3 theory or tasting readiness. The dataset version and baseline are provisional for integration.
