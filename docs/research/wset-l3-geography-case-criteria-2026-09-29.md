# WSET Level 3 regional case criteria — 29 September 2026

The [WSET Level 3 Award in Wines specification, Issue 2](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf), Unit 1 Learning Outcome 2, calls for explanation of how regional vineyard, winery, legal and commercial factors affect still-wine style, quality and price. The five applications here use already authored regional scenarios for bounded practice; they are not WSET questions or official marking criteria.

## Case inventory and practice boundary

On `0bffbad`, the WSET_L3 core geography domain contains **32 `CASE_*` role items** across 13 four-role cases. The local merge of the earlier business `case_criteria` commit serves 17 geography co-role items in eight of those cases. This batch scopes the other **five complete cases**: 20 cited role items, including the remaining **15 geography** items (five actions, three reasons, three tradeoffs and four limitations). Its ten new false criteria are specific to the supplied Valpolicella buying brief, Taurasi–Vulture paired tasting, two Rioja sites, Dão–Vinho Verde comparison, and Mosel–Rheingau Riesling purchase. They are accompanied by an explanation of why each claim fails. The format asks for four separate assignments: action, reason, tradeoff and limitation.

All 20 positive role summaries, full assertions, item citations, WSET_L3 core mappings and open-written `short_answer` prompts pre-existed. The five cases have **43 item-to-source links to 12 distinct HTTPS source URLs**; none is newly mapped or presented as expert-verified. Existing map-location exercises remain in the release. The case criteria exercise counts as structured scenario practice only when its complete four-role pool is available and mapped at the active track's required depth. It does not grade prose, infer a blind origin, turn a matched-pair observation into a regional quality hierarchy, or replace tasting instruction.
