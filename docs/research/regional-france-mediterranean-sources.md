## Case and citation checks

Each case node name is exactly its complete template prompt, including the supplied premises. The four role edges are action, reason, tradeoff and limitation; no case premise is hidden only in a template. Point aliases are distinct concise summaries, not additional answer options.

The Picpoul case requires representative sampling rather than treating equal sugar as equal readiness. The Faugères case explicitly plans for the next season after identified prior damage: it does not suggest pheromones cure larvae already damaging fruit. The Collioure case supplies a qualified site recommendation and estimated costs but no market or sensory premium evidence. The Provence split trial supplies acceptable balance in both samples and a specific customer colour preference; its tradeoff treats any flavour difference as requiring confirmation by tasting and does not grade all pale wines higher. The Australian pricing locators use the live “Cost-plus pricing” and “Set your prices” headings, checked through direct HTTP text when browser retrieval timed out.

Several CIVL English appellation pages currently repeat Cabardès body paragraphs under unrelated headings, including Pic Saint Loup and Terrasses du Larzac. Those body sections were excluded completely; direct appellation-syndicate material supports the authored comparisons. Some guessed or obsolete paths returned errors and are not cited. No copyrighted diagram, photo or map asset was copied.

Locators identify the actual passage and separate conditional editorial applications from regional source descriptions. No full current legal options list, soil-to-flavour explanation, guaranteed origin identification, automatic regional ranking or altitude-only coolness claim is introduced.
