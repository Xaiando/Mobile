# Diploma business practice closure — 29 September 2026

Release 0.24.48 adds practice for **existing** WSET Level 4 core items. It adds no knowledge assertion, citation, certification mapping, official mark or claim that D2 or the Diploma syllabus is complete. All 4,288 curriculum assertions remain `unverified` pending qualified review. The [WSET Level 4 Wines specification, Issue 1.4](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/17609/wset_l4wines_specification_en_august-2025.pdf) sets the Diploma study scope; the app's `core` and `useful_practice` labels are editorial coverage measures.

The baseline's business domain had **125/236** core items with useful practice. The new templates close its **111** gaps: 82 case-role assertions gain a 37-pool `case_criteria` exercise, and 29 principle assertions gain item-specific four-option `authored_choice` questions. The measured result is **236/236** business core useful practice, with **66 additional non-business co-roles** in the same complete cases gaining a structured exercise. The WSET Level 4 total rises from 2,279 to 2,456 core items with useful practice. These counts say that a question is available, not that a learner has mastered it or passed an official assessment.

The case cohort comprises seven business-only production, cash, currency, pricing and route-to-market cases; one Ribeira Sacra buying case; two D6 research-evidence cases; and 27 regional cases with business decision roles. Each pool contains the existing action, reason, trade-off and limitation assertions and two newly written, scenario-specific false claims with explanations. The three original cash, currency and discount subjects have short titles, so their prompts reproduce the full existing written-case premise. The format grades role assignments objectively and displays the existing assertion and linked publisher citation afterward. It complements the original open written response and does not grade that prose.

Every new principle choice records a `sourceCitationId` already linked to its own assertion. The case presenter draws each role's sources from its existing `knowledge_item_citations`; the new distractors are explicit contradictions or unsupported inferences from the supplied scenario. The regression test checks all 148 case-role links, all 29 choice links, complete four-role pools, full scenario overrides, served formats, and correct/false response grading. This is a structural and reasoning audit, not independent expert verification of each inherited source.

The remaining Diploma work is broader than this business coverage measure: sustained written D2 assessment, current market and legal evidence where a question needs it, the other Diploma units, and qualified content review still require separate work. No current price, tax rate, exchange rate or market share is invented here.
