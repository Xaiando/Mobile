# WSET Level 4 Diploma scope and gap audit

Scope checked on **26 September 2026**; implementation updated through **0.20.2 on 27 September 2026**. The companion now offers `WSET_L4` as a selectable study track, with existing WSET Level 3 material inherited through the certification chain. This activates study support; it does **not** establish complete Diploma preparation, assessment equivalence or qualification readiness. Release 0.20.1 retains the 0.20.0 inventory and adds the two [Italian factual corrections](fact-check-2026-09-27.md). The isolated 0.20.2 continuation adds four climate/weather reasoning exercises and 20 Diploma-only points; see its [report](diploma-climate-reasoning-continuation.md).

The [official qualification page](https://www.wsetglobal.com/qualifications/wset-level-4-diploma-in-wines/) currently links the [August 2025 specification, Issue 1.4](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/17609/wset_l4wines_specification_en_august-2025.pdf). The source is pinned in `assets/curriculum/track_scope.yaml`. The six workstreams below correspond to its six units; their labels, selectors and task breakdown are editorial. Printed pages 6–18 were used for this comparison. This audit does not reproduce the learning outcomes, sample examinations, tasting form or assignment titles.

## What activation changes

- The dynamic track picker obtains Diploma from the curriculum's `is_selectable` flag.
- Existing WSET Level 3 mappings supply the inherited geography, legal facts and permitted-grape practice. Their depth and importance are preserved; there is no mass copy of mappings and no automatic increase to Diploma-level depth.
- Diploma has its own official scope manifest. Regional selectors count support from authored facts, and dedicated Diploma tasks retain the unfinished work.
- The existing structured tasting grid remains the app's independently authored grid. It is not presented as WSET's Level 4 tasting form or as evidence of complete tasting preparation.

The coverage checker measures practice of **authored items**. An objective marked `represented` means at least one item matches its selector. An objective marked `planned` has a task but no matching item. Having no `missing` objectives means the scope has been accounted for; it cannot establish full syllabus coverage. Location-only selectors especially must not be treated as complete regional wine analysis.

## Completion evidence required

1. Check each required regional and product study group against primary facts, authored selectors and usable formats; maintain an explicit gap list even when every objective has a task.
2. Add Diploma-specific depth only where reviewed content and appropriate practice exist. Inherited Level 3 depth is preserved until then.
3. Develop original explanation, comparison, tasting and research support for the six workstreams; validate it with qualified reviewers. Generic short-answer pools and maps alone cannot demonstrate assessment parity.
4. Keep examining-body documents as scope sources only. Legal, geographical, production and business facts need their own primary citations; no copied examining-body tasting-grid artwork or examination questions.
5. Update this audit, the scope manifest, release coverage and rehearsal limitations together. Describe the current product as a Diploma study companion with documented gaps until the workstreams actually meet their acceptance criteria.
