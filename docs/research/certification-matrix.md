## WSET Level 3 preparation shape

Public qualification information supports three preparation needs:

1. theory recognition/recall;
2. short-written explanation/reasoning;
3. structured blind tasting of two wines.

The app should not declare WSET L3 parity while Q1/T1/T2 and the regional/principles content remain incomplete.

Source: [WSET Level 3 Award in Wines](https://www.wsetglobal.com/qualifications/wset-level-3-award-in-wines).

## CMS Europe Certified preparation shape

Public CMS Europe material supports three preparation needs:

1. mixed written theory;
2. two-wine deductive tasting;
3. practical service/recommendation knowledge.

The 2026/27 syllabus makes the non-wine beverage/business gap concrete: spirits, beer/cider, sake, aperitifs/liqueurs/bitters, classic cocktails/recommendations, bottle/event arithmetic and gross-profit concepts need explicit coverage rather than a generic “beverage basics” bucket.

Sources:
- [2026/27 Examination Syllabus](https://courtofmastersommeliers.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Syllabus-202627-1.pdf)
- [Certified Sommelier Certificate](https://courtofmastersommeliers.org/certified-sommelier-certificate-1-day/)
- [CMS Europe resources](https://courtofmastersommeliers.org/resources/)

## Future tracks

ASI, WSET L4, CMS Advanced/Master and SWE/CSW each require their own scope manifest when implemented. Never use one body's curriculum as a proxy for another.
