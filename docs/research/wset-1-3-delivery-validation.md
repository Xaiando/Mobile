# WSET Levels 1–3 study delivery

27 September 2026. Final release validation is in progress. This record supplements the granular [completion plan](wset-1-3-completion-plan.md) and its exact lesson evidence; it is not an accreditation statement.

## Study and learner progress

All five tracks are selectable: WSET Levels 1, 2, 3, Diploma and CMS Certified. Beginner tracks receive their authored foundations through cumulative mappings. Study searches assertions, names, aliases and accents, with a domain filter and retained item memory and sources. Selecting an unfinished requirement starts practice on its exact current item IDs through the ordinary study planner and shared FSRS state.

The published Level 1–3 requirement catalog separates actual grape/style/environment/production/label/quality-price teaching from geographic location. Optional atlas content has its own denominator. Learning a place pin cannot establish mastery of that region's explanatory material. Retired or unserved required lessons remain visible as unavailable; an empty scope cannot complete a milestone.

Memory mastery requires successful reviews on three distinct dates over at least seven days, a current estimated retrievability of at least 90%, and at least seven days of stability. A subsequent failed review or fading memory can remove mastery. The app study milestone additionally requires all reviewed required topics and the recorded level-specific practice activities. The learner's official examination result remains an independent self-reported field.

## Original practice and saved work

The rehearsal bank contains original questions and written cases, with level-specific topic blueprints and the public assessment durations. Answers, prompt versions, explanations and written criteria are saved as snapshots. Feedback is deferred until an attempt ends. Written work uses explicit self-review against explanatory criteria; prose receives no automatic official mark. Deadline enforcement is atomic and restart-safe. Participation and self-review do not write FSRS review events or official examination passes.

Three appended teaching grids and nine fictional tasting calibrations retain the two historical grids and their saved vocabulary. All required observations and evidence must be recorded before completing a calibration. Physical wine observations remain learner descriptions, with no invented objective correct answer. Level 3 also provides a timed two-wine practice with saved observations and conclusions. Physical single-wine practice is optional at Levels 1–2.

Malformed saved rows are handled individually, preserve their raw bytes for recovery, and earn no completion credit. Real database failures still propagate. Imported calibration reference vocabulary is checked before feedback or progress participation; valid retired case history remains readable but does not count as current study practice. Editing a physical tasting invalidates completed guided evidence until the learner completes the revised record. The existing backup and erase systems retain these records without a schema migration.

## Required geography and source preservation

The geography evidence ledger records 410 level/name requirements resolved to 312 canonical locations and 149 Level 2 grape/origin combinations. Required places have a generated framed location exercise. Country, AVA, point-marker and overlapping-area tap tests check actual answer grading. New markers are licensed reference coordinates, not fabricated wine boundaries. Twelve additional French appellation recipes use the existing licensed INAO source; a dissolved Natural Earth land layer supplies an accurate world frame.

The pre-authoring preservation audit retains all 2,896 original assertions, 3,371 nodes, 3,007 relations, both legacy tasting grids, the 704 Diploma D3 selectors, dependency lockfile, schema and pinned web database runtimes. Existing GeoJSON sources checked out with CRLF were normalized only where the resulting LF bytes exactly match their recorded upstream SHA-256. `.gitattributes` now preserves that canonical format. The French appellation layer is deliberately extended; the Vino Nobile marker layer has canonical builder property ordering with the same identities, names and coordinates.

China's eight optional atlas references remain available by the user's preference. Its 44 analytical lessons remain Diploma-only. Grok's wine-history and other-beverage work is a separate sommelier strand and does not count toward required Award in Wines completion.

## Review boundary

Independent research and critique compare the public specifications with actual assertion text and current certification mappings. Exact legal grape identity is separate from a style explanation. Sensory teaching describes typical or conditional tendencies rather than guaranteeing every bottle's characteristics. New factual items remain `unverified` with their original review status; internal scope and delivery review does not turn into qualified expert verification.

Final analysis, complete regressions, measured coverage ratchets, asset reproducibility and release/browser checks must close before publishing completion metadata. The ledger records those results and any repairs. Diploma's broader scope remains incomplete and its original unit selectors are preserved.
