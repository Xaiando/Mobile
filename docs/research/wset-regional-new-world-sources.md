# WSET New World regional lesson source ledger

Authored 27 September 2026. This pack contains original still-wine explanations for the enumerated Level 2 and Level 3 New World contexts. It does not reproduce assessment questions or claim official endorsement or a qualification. All new items remain unverified, with automatic MCQ generation disabled; original contextual questions require their own review.

## Scope and integration

- Level 2 scope: [official issue 2.1 specification](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/19132/wset_l2wines_specification_en_april2026_issue21.pdf), current qualification-page Issue 2.1 (2026). Principal-grape GI combinations are recorded individually in the evidence JSON (69 New World pairs).
- Level 3 scope: [official May 2022 issue 2 specification](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf), Unit 1 LO2 still wines. The 2016 cover on the PDF does not override its issue-2 publication page.
- Official specifications identify what to study. They are not used as unsupported factual wine sources. Sources below are regional bodies, technical wine agencies, government regulators and the Pinotage Association for its explicitly bounded competition rules.
- 84 new explanations: 39 explicitly Level 2 and 45 Level 3, all core at depth 2. 46 selected preexisting original explanations gain missing Level 2 core mappings; their higher-level mappings remain intact.
- Existing map/location facts are listed separately from explanations. Counties are not replaced by same-named AVAs. South African ward examples use three actual Hemel-en-Aarde wards. Barossa and Hunter Valley are zones, distinguished from their component regions.
- Ontario and BC are distinct VQA systems. British Columbia regulation consolidation was current to 22 September 2026; Chile Decreto 464 uses the consolidation of 14 July 2026. Cape Blend percentages are explicitly 2025 competition rules, not a claimed universal statutory rule.
- Production and quality/price require combination with the cited generic grape/cellar/commercial principles. Generic profiles are identified separately in JSON; an empty regional dimension is not silently counted as covered by a map pin. Sparkling, fortified, practical tasting and the rest of the curriculum are outside this batch.
- All transient planting percentages, promotional quality rankings, fixed retail prices, altitude-to-quality assumptions and soil-to-flavour guarantees are excluded. Dated examples remain dated.

## Lesson-by-lesson evidence

### ki_wset_nw_california_variation

Level: WSET_L2. Requirement group: us_california. Dimensions: environment, grapes, style. Named study context: California.

California includes warm inland and marine-cooled vineyards. Sonoma illustrates this range: Cabernet ripens in warmer areas, while Pinot Noir and Chardonnay suit cooler ones. A state label alone cannot fix body or ripeness.

- [src_reg_am_sonoma](https://sonomawinegrape.org/visit/sonoma-county-terroir/): Overview; climate and marine influence; Sonoma is the named example, not a description of every California vineyard.

### ki_wset_nw_napa_cabernet

Level: WSET_L2. Requirement group: us_napa. Dimensions: grapes, style, production. Named study context: Napa County; Napa Valley; Rutherford; Oakville; Calistoga; Los Carneros.

Napa Cabernet Sauvignon can show black cherry, plum and currant with dense youthful structure. Oak ageing can add spice; expression varies by site.

- [src_wset_nw_napa_wine](https://wine.napavintners.com/wine): Cabernet Sauvignon; no fixed ageing duration, universal oak use or quality guarantee.

### ki_wset_nw_napa_merlot

Level: WSET_L2. Requirement group: us_napa. Dimensions: grapes, style, production. Named study context: Napa County; Napa Valley; Rutherford; Oakville; Calistoga; Los Carneros.

Napa Merlot may be bottled alone or blended with Cabernet, contributing softer fruit and body. Ripe cherry and plum, texture and weight vary by site and cellar choices.

- [src_wset_nw_napa_wine](https://wine.napavintners.com/wine): Merlot.

### ki_wset_nw_napa_chardonnay

Level: WSET_L2. Requirement group: us_napa. Dimensions: grapes, style, production. Named study context: Napa County; Napa Valley; Rutherford; Oakville; Calistoga; Los Carneros.

Napa Chardonnay ranges from crisp to rich. Barrel fermentation, lees stirring and malolactic conversion can change texture and aromas; cooler Los Carneros supports slower ripening.

- [src_wset_nw_napa_wine](https://wine.napavintners.com/wine): Chardonnay; choices are conditional, not mandatory practices.

### ki_wset_nw_napa_sauvignon

Level: WSET_L2. Requirement group: us_napa. Dimensions: grapes, style, production, labels. Named study context: Napa County; Napa Valley; Rutherford; Oakville; Calistoga; Los Carneros.

Napa Sauvignon Blanc often uses neutral vessels to preserve herbal and fruit character with lively acidity. Some oak-fermented or aged examples have more texture and may be labelled Fumé Blanc.

- [src_wset_nw_napa_wine](https://wine.napavintners.com/wine): Sauvignon Blanc; Fumé Blanc is a style association, not an origin or universal oak rule.

### ki_wset_nw_oakville_setting

Level: WSET_L2. Requirement group: us_napa. Dimensions: environment, grapes. Named study context: Napa County; Napa Valley; Rutherford; Oakville; Calistoga; Los Carneros.

Oakville is moderately warm, with morning fog and cooling from San Pablo Bay. Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot are important grapes; vineyard position still matters.

- [src_reg_am_napa_regions](https://wine.napavintners.com/region): oakville subappellation profile.

### ki_wset_nw_rutherford_setting

Level: WSET_L2. Requirement group: us_napa. Dimensions: environment, grapes. Named study context: Napa County; Napa Valley; Rutherford; Oakville; Calistoga; Los Carneros.

Rutherford has a moderately warm growing setting, with less fog than Oakville. Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot are important; the regional name does not prescribe a wine flavour.

- [src_reg_am_napa_regions](https://wine.napavintners.com/region): rutherford subappellation profile.

### ki_wset_nw_calistoga_setting

Level: WSET_L2. Requirement group: us_napa. Dimensions: environment, grapes. Named study context: Napa County; Napa Valley; Rutherford; Oakville; Calistoga; Los Carneros.

Calistoga is among Napa’s warmer areas, with large daily temperature changes. Cabernet Sauvignon, Zinfandel and Syrah suit its warmer sites.

- [src_reg_am_napa_regions](https://wine.napavintners.com/region): calistoga subappellation profile.

### ki_wset_nw_stags_leap_setting

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: us_napa_subavas. Dimensions: environment, grapes. Named study context: Stags Leap District; Howell Mountain; Mount Veeder; Saint Helena.

Stags Leap District combines warmth from exposed slopes with afternoon bay breezes. Cabernet Sauvignon is important; individual wines still reflect site and production choices.

- [src_reg_am_napa_regions](https://wine.napavintners.com/region): Stags Leap District profile

### ki_wset_nw_howell_setting

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: us_napa_subavas. Dimensions: environment, grapes. Named study context: Stags Leap District; Howell Mountain; Mount Veeder; Saint Helena.

Howell Mountain’s higher vineyards sit above the valley fog and have relatively sunny conditions. Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot are important grape associations.

- [src_reg_am_napa_regions](https://wine.napavintners.com/region): Howell Mountain profile

### ki_wset_nw_veeder_setting

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: us_napa_subavas. Dimensions: environment, grapes. Named study context: Stags Leap District; Howell Mountain; Mount Veeder; Saint Helena.

Mount Veeder has a cool-to-moderate mountain setting, with many vineyards above valley fog. Its Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot plantings differ climatically from the warm valley floor.

- [src_reg_am_napa_regions](https://wine.napavintners.com/region): Mt. Veeder profile

### ki_wset_nw_saint_helena_setting

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: us_napa_subavas. Dimensions: environment, grapes. Named study context: Stags Leap District; Howell Mountain; Mount Veeder; Saint Helena.

Saint Helena is a warm northern Napa setting with less marine influence than southern sites. Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Zinfandel are important grape associations.

- [src_reg_am_napa_regions](https://wine.napavintners.com/region): St. Helena profile

### ki_wset_nw_sonoma_grape_sites

Level: WSET_L2. Requirement group: us_sonoma. Dimensions: grapes, environment. Named study context: Sonoma; Sonoma County; Russian River Valley; Alexander Valley; Dry Creek Valley; Sonoma Coast.

Russian River Valley and Sonoma Coast are known for Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Warmer Alexander Valley suits Cabernet; Bennett Valley supplies a cooler Sonoma Merlot example. These are associations, not exclusive planting rules.

- [src_reg_am_sonoma](https://sonomawinegrape.org/visit/sonoma-county-terroir/): Overview; Alexander Valley, Bennett Valley, Russian River Valley and Sonoma Coast.

### ki_wset_nw_dry_creek_zinfandel

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: us_sonoma. Dimensions: grapes, style. Named study context: Sonoma; Sonoma County; Russian River Valley; Alexander Valley; Dry Creek Valley; Sonoma Coast.

Dry Creek Valley is associated with Zinfandel and Sauvignon Blanc. Sonoma’s regional overview describes concentrated Zinfandel examples; actual tannin, ripeness and oak need assessment.

- [src_reg_am_sonoma](https://sonomawinegrape.org/visit/sonoma-county-terroir/): Dry Creek Valley and overview; no assertion that every wine is concentrated.

### ki_wset_nw_sonoma_coast_setting

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: us_sonoma. Dimensions: environment, grapes. Named study context: Sonoma; Sonoma County; Russian River Valley; Alexander Valley; Dry Creek Valley; Sonoma Coast.

Sonoma Coast has marine influence and varied elevations. Some vineyards receive sunshine above fog; Pinot Noir and Chardonnay remain important, without identical ripening at every site.

- [src_reg_am_sonoma](https://sonomawinegrape.org/visit/sonoma-county-terroir/): Sonoma Coast; avoid universal rainfall, altitude or above-fog statements.

### ki_wset_nw_santa_maria_grapes

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: us_central_coast. Dimensions: grapes, environment. Named study context: Santa Barbara County.

Santa Maria Valley’s ocean wind and fog provide a cool growing comparison within Santa Barbara County. Pinot Noir and Chardonnay are flagship grapes.

- [src_wset_nw_santa_maria](https://sbcountywines.com/portfolio_page/santa-maria-valley-2/): Santa Maria Valley profile.

### ki_wset_nw_mendocino_diversity

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: us_coast_depth. Dimensions: grapes, environment. Named study context: Mendocino County; Santa Cruz Mountains; Monterey; San Luis Obispo County; Paso Robles; Santa Maria Valley.

Mendocino County includes Pinot Noir and Chardonnay as well as Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Zinfandel and Syrah. Its different coastal and inland growing areas require site-specific comparison.

- [src_wset_nw_mendocino](https://mendowine.com/press-facts-2/): Regional press facts: grapes and wine-growing areas; county is distinct from Mendocino AVA.

### ki_wset_nw_san_luis_grapes

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: us_coast_depth. Dimensions: grapes, environment. Named study context: Mendocino County; Santa Cruz Mountains; Monterey; San Luis Obispo County; Paso Robles; Santa Maria Valley.

San Luis Obispo County combines cool coastal valleys and marine breezes with inland hills. Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Zinfandel and Rhône varieties illustrate the range.

- [src_wset_nw_slo](https://discovercaliforniawines.com/discover-california/central-coast/san-luis-obispo-county/): County overview; does not substitute SLO Coast AVA for the county.

### ki_wset_nw_lodi_style_choices

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: us_lodi. Dimensions: style, production, grapes. Named study context: Lodi.

Lodi Zinfandel includes lighter fruit-led wines and riper, fuller, oak-influenced examples. Vineyard selection and oak use help explain the range; an old-vine label alone cannot fix the style.

- [src_wset_nw_lodi_style](https://www.lodiwine.com/blog/How-to-delineate-the-proliferating-styles-of-today-s-Lodi-Zinfandels): Dated 2020 regional stylistic categories; no universal soil-to-flavour inference.

### ki_wset_nw_oregon_chardonnay_range

Level: WSET_L2. Requirement group: us_oregon. Dimensions: grapes, style, production. Named study context: Oregon; Willamette Valley.

Oregon Chardonnay ranges from fresh and tart to creamy examples. Its Willamette Valley association does not make oak or malolactic conversion compulsory.

- [src_wset_nw_oregon_grapes](https://www.oregonwine.org/discover/oregon-wine-varieties/): Chardonnay section; production mechanisms are also taught in the sourced Chardonnay variation lesson.

### ki_wset_nw_yakima_sites

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: us_washington. Dimensions: environment, grapes. Named study context: Washington; Columbia Valley; Yakima Valley.

Cooler Yakima sites support Chardonnay and Riesling; warmer areas support Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon and Syrah. River water and aquifers supply irrigation in this dry growing setting.

- [src_wset_nw_yakima](https://www.washingtonwine.org/resource/yakima-valley-ava/): Site variation and irrigation; do not apply one temperature to the whole valley.

### ki_wset_nw_finger_lakes_riesling

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: us_new_york. Dimensions: style, grapes. Named study context: New York; Finger Lakes.

Finger Lakes Riesling includes dry through sweet wines. Its cool-climate context and acidity are important; the region name alone does not identify residual sugar.

- [src_wset_nw_finger_riesling](https://newyorkwines.org/trade/new-york-the-mecca-of-american-riesling/): Riesling stylistic range; no quality ranking from promotional language.

### ki_wset_nw_usa_origin_scale

Level: WSET_L2. Requirement group: us_labels. Dimensions: labels. Named study context: USA; California; Napa County; Sonoma County.

A US state or county name identifies a political origin; an AVA identifies a delimited viticultural area. Napa County and Napa Valley are different origin designations, with different federal source requirements.

- [src_wset_nw_ttb_origin](https://www.ttb.gov/regulated-commodities/beverage-alcohol/wine/labeling-wine/wine-labeling-appellation-of-origin): Political appellations and American Viticultural Areas.

### ki_wset_nw_usa_ava_origin

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: us_labels. Dimensions: labels. Named study context: USA; California; Napa County; Sonoma County.

At least 85% of the grapes must come from the named AVA, and the wine must be fully finished within the state or states containing it, subject to the regulation’s exceptions.

- [src_wset_nw_ttb_origin](https://www.ttb.gov/regulated-commodities/beverage-alcohol/wine/labeling-wine/wine-labeling-appellation-of-origin): AVA source and finishing requirements; state law can add requirements.

### ki_wset_nw_ontario_icewine_selection

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: ca_ontario. Dimensions: production, grapes. Named study context: Canada; Ontario; Niagara Peninsula.

Ontario Icewine uses grapes frozen naturally on the vine and pressed while frozen, concentrating the juice. Vidal and Riesling are important white-grape examples; this is a specific concentrated-fruit process.

- [src_wset_nw_ontario_labels](https://vqaontario.ca/vqa-wine-program/wine-categories-and-labelling/): Wine categories: Icewine; natural freezing rather than artificial concentration.

### ki_wset_nw_ontario_icewine_style

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: ca_ontario. Dimensions: style, grapes. Named study context: Canada; Ontario; Niagara Peninsula.

Ontario’s Vidal and Riesling Icewines are sweet, concentrated wines with fruit aromas and balancing acidity. They differ from dry table wines; individual grapes and production affect the aroma profile.

- [src_wset_nw_ontario_icewine](https://winecountryontario.ca/icewine/): Icewine overview; no fixed serving temperature or sweetness inferred for all Canadian wines.

### ki_wset_nw_ontario_vqa_origins

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: ca_ontario. Dimensions: labels. Named study context: Canada; Ontario; Niagara Peninsula.

VQA Ontario wine uses Ontario-grown grapes. Niagara Peninsula labelling requires at least 85% Niagara Peninsula grapes, with the remainder from Ontario; named subappellations have stricter origin requirements.

- [src_wset_nw_ontario_labels](https://vqaontario.ca/vqa-wine-program/wine-categories-and-labelling/): Origin appellations and subappellations; Ontario rules must not be generalised to British Columbia.

### ki_wset_nw_bc_vqa_origin

Level: WSET_L3. Requirement group: ca_bc. Dimensions: labels. Named study context: British Columbia; Okanagan Valley.

BC VQA is governed by British Columbia’s regulation and requires British Columbia grape origin. Okanagan Valley is a British Columbia geographical indication; Ontario’s VQA origin percentages are a separate system.

- [src_nwa_ca_bc](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/168_2018/): Wines of Marked Quality Regulation: BC VQA, grapes of British Columbia and geographical indications.

### ki_wset_nw_central_valley_reds

Level: WSET_L2. Requirement group: cl_central. Dimensions: grapes, style, environment. Named study context: Central Valley; Maipo; Colchagua.

Central Valley includes Maipo and the Rapel zones Cachapoal and Colchagua, important for Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Carménère. Warm inland ripening and cooling influences vary, so the broad origin does not fix body or ripeness.

- [src_reg_am_chile](https://www.winesofchile.org/wine-regions-map/): Maipo, Cachapoal and Colchagua; political hierarchy separately supported by current Decreto 464.

### ki_wset_nw_central_valley_whites

Level: WSET_L2. Requirement group: cl_central. Dimensions: grapes, style. Named study context: Central Valley; Maipo; Colchagua.

Chardonnay and Sauvignon Blanc are Chilean white-wine references. Central Valley contains varied sites: assess fruit maturity and cellar choices rather than infer one acidity, oak level or aroma from this broad origin.
