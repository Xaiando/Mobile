## Findings

1. **P1 — The CMS Certified spirits objective is marked covered but omits named identifications and an Introductory distillation principle.** The selector assets/curriculum/track_scope.yaml:2586–2616 points to existing Scotch, tequila, grape-spirit, Calvados and Armagnac nodes. However, a search of all registered area assertions found no Islay identity, no Mezcal distinction, no Grappa identity, and no general fruit-eau-de-vie identification. assets/curriculum/areas/sommelier_practice.yaml:250–255 says only that other agave spirits are not tequila; it never teaches Mezcal. The same file at :268–273 explicitly says its EU annex does not define Grappa. assets/curriculum/areas/sommelier_beverages.yaml:112–135 teaches Scotch rules but not Islay. No registered explanation teaches even a nonprocedural distillation principle. The [CMS syllabus p. 24](https://courtofmastersommeliers.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Syllabus-202627-1.pdf) places principles of distillation in Introductory and specifically names Islay whisky, Marc/Grappa, Tequila & Mezcal, and fruit eaux-de-vie in Certified. Add concise primary-source-backed recognition/comparison items and a nonprocedural distillation explanation; link their nodes explicitly to cms_certified.spirits, then test that every named example has an actual assertion rather than merely a broad parent selector. Do not invent still-operating instructions.

2. **P2 — The Certified liqueur flavour-family inventory is represented by only citrus and herbal examples.** assets/curriculum/track_scope.yaml:2617–2627 marks the objective covered by n_cms_liqueur_extraction, n_cms_liqueur_case and EU category/aperitif nodes. assets/curriculum/areas/cms_spirits_liqueurs_closure.yaml:69–91 teaches Cointreau (citrus) and Green Chartreuse (herbal); assets/curriculum/areas/sommelier_beverage_basics.yaml:345–413 teaches legal liqueur, vermouth and bitter categories. There is no registered fruit, bean/nut, flower or unclassified liqueur example, and no comparison case that can recommend among those families. The [CMS syllabus p. 25](https://courtofmastersommeliers.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Syllabus-202627-1.pdf) explicitly lists those flavour families and ability to recommend at Certified. Add one source-scoped product/ingredient example per missing family, plus a guest-choice exercise that depends on actual stock. Avoid extrapolating a maker's recipe to a whole family.

3. **P2 user-scope gap, not a CMS Certified requirement — Sake has no food-pairing exercise.** assets/curriculum/track_scope.yaml:2651–2661 and assets/curriculum/areas/sommelier_beverages.yaml:202–234 teach polishing and special-designation classes; assets/curriculum/areas/sommelier_beverage_basics.yaml:120–134 and :273–305 teach parallel fermentation and service temperature. A search of the full registered curriculum found no sake/food pairing assertion or case. The project brief docs/research/history-beverage-scanner-completion-plan.md:6–8 and broader C7 scope docs/backlog.md:1071–1080 ask for sommelier sake basics including pairing. The [CMS syllabus p. 28](https://courtofmastersommeliers.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Syllabus-202627-1.pdf) puts production methods, label terms and types in the combined Introductory/Certified column; sake service and pairing are in **Advanced**, not Certified. Add a clearly labelled user-enrichment pairing case, if completing the broader user strand, using the [Japan Sake and Shochu Makers Association's basic intensity and aroma guidance](https://japansake.or.jp/sake/en/basic/sake-food-pairing/). Do not call its absence a Certified syllabus failure or prescribe one guaranteed match.

4. **P3 enrichment boundary — The cigar basics are specifically Habano basics.** assets/curriculum/areas/sommelier_beverages.yaml:238–261 covers the Habano protected name, leaf anatomy and tobacco harm; assets/curriculum/areas/sommelier_beverage_basics.yaml:138–152 and :309–341 covers Habanos parejo/figurado, ring gauge and factory/market vitolas. These are useful and [Habanos' own anatomy](https://www.habanos.com/en/the-anatomy-of-a-habano/) and [shape/size](https://www.habanos.com/en/principal-shapes-sizes/) pages support their attributed statements. They do not teach how to identify a non-Cuban cigar or provide broad cigar storage/service basics. If the user-facing strand is called general cigar basics, either label the lesson as Habano examples or add source-bounded general comparisons. This is **user enrichment**: the [CMS Europe syllabus](https://courtofmastersommeliers.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Syllabus-202627-1.pdf) has no named cigar criterion, and existing mappings are secondary. Keep the tobacco-harm statement; do not imply that a cigar shape makes use safe.

5. **P3 optional history bridge — The early timeline still jumps from Areni to a 550 BCE Greek object.** assets/curriculum/areas/wine_history_course.yaml:161–175 differentiates Georgian chemical residues from Areni-1's later installation; assets/curriculum/areas/wine_history_timeline.yaml:95–100 next gives a c. 550 BCE Greek krater. The course is expressly a selected essentials survey (docs/research/wine-history-course.md:5), so this is not an error or a WSET deficiency. If the intended “Areni to modern” story needs one intermediate moment, ancient Egyptian wine-jar labels would teach dated provenance without an unsupported first-wine claim. [UCL's Amarna record](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/amarna/winelabels.html) describes year/vintner/vineyard inscriptions; the [British Museum collection note](https://www.britishmuseum.org/sites/default/files/2020-06/British-Museum_Egypt_Sudan_Newsletter6_2019_0.pdf) gives object EA 59337. Register one narrowly worded period and comparison case only if this bridge is considered essential; preserve the existing archaeology/source-evidence distinctions.

6. **P2 release fact check — Irish Whiskey GI claims cite a historical technical-file copy during a known amendment process.** assets/curriculum/areas/cms_spirits_liqueurs_closure.yaml:41–64 (ki_cms_irish_origin, ki_cms_irish_pot_malt, ki_cms_irish_blend) are marked unverified; the source entry at :185–192 itself requests a pre-release currency check. [Ireland's competent-authority GI page](https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-agriculture-food-and-the-marine/publications/geographical-indications-spirit-drinks/) still links its technical file and explains that GI products must comply with the applicable specification, but that page showed a May 2025 update when checked. I did not establish whether a September 2026 proposal was adopted. Re-open the current competent-authority or EU register specification before calling these exact category/cask rules current, and retain unverified status until a qualified reviewer checks them. This is a source-currency limit, not evidence the current assertions are false.
