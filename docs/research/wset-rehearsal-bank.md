# Original WSET Level 1–3 rehearsal bank

Authored 27 September 2026. The bundled `assets/study/wset_rehearsal.json` contains **220 distinct original MCQs** and **12 original Level 3 extended prompts with 52 self-assessment criteria**. Four-option questions have one intended correct answer and independent distractors. The prompts and cases are app-authored; no official examination questions or model answers are reproduced. Written criteria describe evidence to check, without automatic prose marks or an official pass threshold.

Scope and practice timing use the verified public [Level 1 June 2022 Issue 1.2](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11682/wset_l1wines_spec_en_jun2022_issue12.pdf), [Level 2 2026 Issue 2.1](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/19132/wset_l2wines_specification_en_april2026_issue21.pdf) and [Level 3 May 2022 Issue 2](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf) specifications. These publications establish the study remit and format; canonical lesson citations establish the factual answers. This is original educational practice, not an official WSET question bank or assessment.

## Coverage and evidence

Every MCQ links a different canonical lesson item. Explanations reuse the existing cited lesson assertion, so the bank introduces no new factual claims or duplicate source-citation records. Written criteria may link multiple relevant items. Original `coverageTags` provide a machine-checkable editorial index; they are not memory credit or proof of official outcome completion.

- **Production and vine mechanisms:** skins and pulp, sugar fermentation, common white/red/rosé sequences, growth/ripening order, climate and acidity, fruit sampling, water/nutrients, canopy/disease, crop balance, harvest tradeoffs, pressing fractions, extraction, MLF, oak reuse, lees texture and protein stability.
- **All 30 Level 2 grape profiles:** Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Gris/Grigio, Riesling, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Pinot Noir, Syrah/Shiraz, Gamay, Grenache, Tempranillo, Nebbiolo, Barbera, Sangiovese, Corvina, Montepulciano, Zinfandel, Pinotage, Carménère, Malbec, Chenin Blanc, Sémillon, Viognier, Gewurztraminer, Verdicchio, Cortese, Garganega, Fiano, Albariño and Furmint. Each has a distinct profile question, and 12 have additional production/variation questions. The eight principal beginner varieties each have a separate Level 1 question.
- **Europe:** familiar wine identities, Bordeaux dry/sweet whites and estate/appellation distinction, Burgundy origin hierarchy, Montagny, Alsace late harvest/SGN, Loire rosé, Tavel, Côte-Rôtie, Pays d’Oc, Italian DOC/DOCG/Classico/Riserva, Delle Venezie/Gavi/Frascati, dried-grape Recioto versus Amarone, Vino Nobile’s town/grape distinction, Rioja maturation, Catalunya/Navarra/Toro, Portuguese Douro/Dão/Vinho Verde, German Prädikat versus sweetness and Mosel/Rheingau/Franken context, Austrian DAC and Welschriesling sweet-wine roles, sweet Tokaj berry selection, and Greece’s key Naoussa/Nemea/Santorini grape associations and Assyrtiko structure.
- **New World:** 25 regional questions covering the USA, Canada, Chile, Argentina, Australia, New Zealand and South Africa. They distinguish political origin from AVA, Ontario natural freezing and VQA origin, Chilean hierarchy, Australian GI scale and South African Wine of Origin. Growing/style questions include Napa/Carneros, Russian River, Willamette, Washington, Finger Lakes, Maipo/Casablanca, Mendoza/Andes/Patagonia, Barossa/Hunter/McLaren Vale/Margaret River/Coonawarra, Marlborough/Hawke’s Bay/Central Otago, Stellenbosch and South African Chenin. Twenty of these are mapped for Level 2; the remaining five retain Level 3 scope.
- **Sparkling and fortified:** traditional/tank/ancestral/carbonated methods, tirage versus dosage, base acidity/alcohol, autolysis, riddling/disgorgement, optional MLF, Port fortification and Ruby/Tawny/Vintage aims, Sherry flor/oxidative sequences and solera, PX drying, Cream blending, Rutherglen cask stocks and VDN mutage. Madeira remains optional atlas/Diploma study and is excluded from these required wine-level presets. Bank version `2026.09.27-original.4` replaces the two former Madeira questions with Sherry/Rutherglen questions and the optional Vienna question with a required Welschriesling role. VDN mutage and amber-wine skin contact retain Level 3 eligibility only; saved attempts retain their original versioned snapshots.
- **Service and tasting interpretation:** storage, closures, temperature, ice-water chilling, glass cleanliness, safe sparkling opening, opened-wine preservation, faults and intended styles, decanting, conditional food effects and customer preference. Tasting questions distinguish developed aroma, pleasant finish, intensity/body from quality, and concentration/structure in ageing reasoning. No unknown physical wine is objectively graded.

Level 3 still-wine questions span 15 country tags: France, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Germany, Austria, Hungary, Greece, USA, Canada, Chile, Argentina, Australia, New Zealand and South Africa. Tokaj extended work concerns suitable Aszú berry selection; the bank does not invent a mandatory dry-Tokaj Level 3 requirement. China and optional advanced atlas content do not enter this lower-level bank.

The extended prompts cover Chardonnay cellar choices, Bordeaux ripening/blending, sparkling methods, Port styles, Sherry ageing, vine resources/canopy/harvest, concentrated sweet-wine routes, Burgundy/Bordeaux label interpretation, chilli pairing, older-red service, Tokaj selection and confounded regional comparison. Each requires explanations and limitations rather than copying an isolated place name.

## Provenance and validation boundary

Supporting study packs are the installed foundation/grape/service/tasting and production principles, existing regional comparison items, and the separately frozen `wset_regional_europe.yaml` / `wset_regional_new_world.yaml`. The latter adds selected Level 2 mappings to existing original New World explanations; nothing was lowered solely to make a question eligible. Those authors supplied stable IDs and exact assertions before bank links were accepted. All lesson review statuses remain unchanged, including `unverified`; rehearsal publication does not constitute expert verification.

`test/core/rehearsal/wset_rehearsal_bank_test.dart` checks distinct prompts/options, citations for every link, the 30-variety/eight-beginner coverage, 15-country still-wine index and exact blueprints. Its integration case installs the real bundled curriculum, checks every linked item’s currentness and effective level membership, then samples nine full attempts (three per level), verifies counts/deadlines/topic totals and confirms there are no FSRS or official-pass writes. This agent ran only Python structural/content checks; root runs the Flutter SDK and formatters sequentially after freeze.

Question coverage is a varied rehearsal pool, not proof that every syllabus detail appears in each random attempt. The separate curriculum requirement ledger and study content determine app study coverage; physical tasting and official qualification results remain external. Historical practice saves its own versioned prompt/options/explanation/criteria snapshot, so later bank changes do not rewrite past answers or feedback.

Final scope boundary: transfer, ancestral and force-carbonation method explanations remain required at Level 3 and optional at Level 2. The two named-method MCQs are Level 3-only in bank version `2026.09.27-original.4`.
