# WSET Level 4 Diploma: remaining app gaps after D4/D5 physical flights

Read-only audit of the current checkout, 29 September 2026. The governing external source is WSET's [Level 4 Diploma in Wines specification, Issue 1.4 (2025)](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/17609/wset_l4wines_specification_en_august-2025.pdf), especially pp. 4–18 and 26–27; see also the [qualification page](https://www.wsetglobal.com/qualifications/wset-level-4-diploma-in-wines/). The six units, assessment formats, and Diploma award belong to WSET and its Approved Programme Providers. This app can offer study and record learner participation; its fact counts, self-review, and declarations are neither WSET requirements nor an official result.

## Current state and exact open selectors

* `assets/progress/wset_scope.json:44478` still says `WSET_L4.curriculumComplete: false` and records two explicit gaps: full six-unit coverage and sustained written/tasting/research work. Its D3/D4/D5/D6 unit groups have, respectively, 704/76/57/17 explicit `itemIds` (D1/D2 use broad domain groups); these are app-selected supporting items, not syllabus denominators or evidence of an assessed unit.
* `assets/curriculum/track_scope.yaml:873–2040` has 57 L4 editorial objectives. Fifty-five declare an item selector. The **only two with no `covers` selector** are `wset_l4.sparkling.tasting` at `:1873–1875` (tasks `DIP-4, T1, T2, Q8, S3`) and `wset_l4.fortified.tasting` at `:2009–2011` (tasks `DIP-5, T1, T2, Q8, S3`). `ObjectiveCoverage.status` in `lib/core/coverage/track_scope.dart:131–168` remains `planned` whenever no item matches and tasks exist. Thus these two item-coverage rows remain planned by design; physical flight records are a different evidence type. Selector presence alone does not prove comprehensive coverage of the objective.
* `assets/study/diploma_tasting_flights.json:1–43` now supplies a D4 and a D5 three-wine flight, prompting description, quality, ageing, style clues, uncertainty, cross-wine comparison, and self-review. `lib/core/diploma_tasting/diploma_tasting_flight.dart:272–388,606–626` requires three physically tasted wines with observations/evidence plus comparison and self-review; this flow is **untimed**. `lib/core/diploma_tasting/diploma_tasting_evidence.dart:5–55` counts saved submissions as participation, and `lib/features/progress/wset_progress_screen.dart:415–422` labels them self-reviewed practice, not a tasting score or exam pass. No learner data was audited, so no actual participation total is asserted.
* The D4 New World selectors at `track_scope.yaml:1764–1800` now have linked items from `assets/curriculum/areas/diploma_sparkling_new_world.yaml` (18 items). D6 evidence/argument selectors at `track_scope.yaml:2014–2040` now link to `assets/curriculum/areas/diploma_independent_research.yaml` (17 items). Historical notes in `docs/research/release-readiness-2026-09-28.md` and `docs/research/wset-level-4-gap-audit.md` that call those selectors empty predate these changes and must not be used as the current gap list.

## Assessment and teaching gaps, by unit

| Unit and WSET weight | Official assessment and required skill | Current app implication |
|---|---|---|
| D1 Wine Production, 20% | 90-minute open response. Explain and evaluate growing and winemaking options against style, quality, and price (spec pp. 6–9, 26). | Broad production facts and some cases, but no D1-specific sustained open-response rehearsal. A search of `assets/curriculum/areas/*.yaml` found no authored assertion using `regenerative`, `precision viticulture`, `dealcohol`, `HACCP`, `ISO`, or `traceability`, although these categories appear expressly in the current D1 range (spec pp. 7–9). This is a term-search lead, not proof that every concept is absent semantically. |
| D2 Wine Business, 10% | 60-minute open response on price drivers, business/channel choices, and marketing (spec pp. 10–11, 26). | Existing pricing, currency, producer and channel cases support study, but no D2-specific written rehearsal and weak useful-practice conversion in the checked-in baseline. Current law, import economics, exchange-rate and market cases need bounded evidence and explanation. |
| D3 Wines of the World, 50% | 3h20 open-response theory across two papers, plus two 90-minute blind six-wine papers (12 wines total); region-by-region production, law, commerce, and comparative quality/price analysis (spec pp. 12–13, 26). | The 704-item explicit group and maps can orient study but do not establish analytical breadth or 12-wine tasting. `assets/study/guided_tasting.json` and `assets/study/wset_rehearsal.json` contain only Levels 1–3; no D3 physical 12-wine sequence was found. |
| D4 Sparkling, 5%; D5 Fortified, 5% | Each combines 90-minute open response with blind tasting of three wines. LO 4.1/5.1 include regional styles, industry bodies/labels, price and business; LO 4.2/5.2 require accurate WSET Level 4 tasting (spec pp. 14–17, 26). | New real-wine flights address practice participation. They do not establish blind accuracy, timed performance or theory quality. The unit gap strings at `wset_scope.json:45220–45399` still call for broader styles, labels, commerce and tasting. The flight model records observations and narrative but has no structured wine-identity reveal or external marking (`diploma_tasting_flight.dart:193–269`). |
| D6 Independent Research, 10% | A WSET-issued 3,000-word research assignment with source evaluation, a referenced argument, and the brief's presentation requirements (spec p. 18, 26). | The 17 study items include research principles and fictional source packets. They prepare method, but there is no issued-title workflow, full-length submission, mentor review, or WSET assessment. Do not present app completion as D6 completion. |

WSET's recommended tasting samples are study recommendations, and exam wines may come from any producing area specified for a unit (spec pp. 20–26). A fixed app flight list should therefore be framed as practice examples, not an exhaustive or exam-predictive wine list.

The checked-in `assets/curriculum/coverage_baseline.json` is a **0.24.6 item-based snapshot**: 1,021 of 2,649 L4 core items have useful practice (38.5%) and only 8 have reasoning practice. Domain useful/core counts are business 0/229, geography 738/1110, service 57/110, tasting 88/178, viticulture 66/447, and winemaking 72/575. `assets/curriculum/coverage_policy.yaml` sets provisional app targets of 90% core useful practice and 50% core reasoning in viticulture, winemaking, service and tasting. Those are app policy thresholds, not WSET pass standards. Physical-flight participation is not an item-format conversion, so it does not silently increase these metrics.

## Highest-priority implementable tasks

1. **D3 analytical regional batches:** Prioritize representative principal regions across the official D3 list, adding source-cited causal comparisons of environment, vineyard/winery choice, law, route to market, style, quality and price. Couple each batch to original short open-response prompts and criterion-led self-review; measure coverage by required region/criterion, not raw map-point count. Anchors: `track_scope.yaml` D3 objectives around `:1510–1737`, `wset_scope.json` D3 group around `:44498–45219`.
2. **D3 physical tasting sequence:** Reuse the D4/D5 flight architecture for two distinct six-wine still-wine sessions with Level 4 SAT fields, evidence-based conclusions, comparison, optional reveal and self/mentor feedback. Preserve participation separately from correctness; do not infer a WSET pass. Official D3 has 12 blind wines; app sessions are preparation, not equivalent assessment.
3. **D1 targeted content plus decisions:** Audit the latest specification's explicit no/low-alcohol production, regenerative/precision vineyard approaches, hygiene/HACCP/ISO/traceability and packaging/transport choices. Add sourced cases asking learners to justify an option under constraints, with limitations and cost/style/quality consequences, not just definitions. Anchor: D1 range in spec pp. 7–9 and current production area packs.
4. **D1 and D2 written practice:** Build original 90-minute D1 and 60-minute D2 open-response rehearsal presets with saved prose and transparent criteria for learner self-review. Use no official mark or pass prediction. Existing `assets/study/wset_rehearsal.json` stops at Level 3.
5. **D2 applied business cases:** Add bounded source-backed supply/demand and currency calculations, producer and distributor trade-offs, regulatory/import costs and marketing choices. Explain assumptions and what would change the conclusion. The baseline's business 0/229 useful-core count makes this a large app-practice gain; first remeasure against the current checkout.
6. **D4/D5 theory depth:** Add current primary-source regional style, association/label and commercial comparisons for the official D4 and D5 lists; link every statement locally. Use open response to explain style, quality and price. The new D4 New World pack and existing fortified method cases are starting points, not closure of LO 4.1/5.1.
7. **D4/D5 optional blind rehearsal:** Keep the present untimed three-wine learning flow. Add an optional timed 90-minute combined theory/tasting practice and a post-submission identity/reveal field or mentor-reviewed note comparison. Any feedback should be explicitly formative, because WSET controls its assessment and wines.
8. **D6 research workspace:** Extend the 17 research-method items into a learner-owned brief/outline, source-appraisal log, claim-to-citation map, draft/revision checklist and export for tutor feedback. Use fictional practice briefs or the learner's own authorized topic; do not ship WSET's current assignment titles or imply WSET grading.
9. **Coverage semantics and documentation:** Keep the two tasting objectives' item-selector status honest. Add a separate `physical participation` signal beside objective item status, and update `docs/wset-learner-progress.md:53–57` plus the WSET_L4 unit descriptions to explain the D4/D5 flights. Avoid assigning an unrelated tasting fact merely to make a planned row appear represented.
10. **Editorial/metric gate:** Recompute current objective matches and useful/reasoning format counts after new content, review all new Diploma assertions against primary sources, and evaluate written/tasting practice separately from flashcard metrics. Preserve `curriculumComplete: false` until each six-unit objective is substantively supported and the app language accurately describes its limits; never interpret that flag as a WSET award.

This audit used repository files and the current WSET specification. It did not run Flutter/Dart, change registered content, or inspect learner records.
