## Editorial decisions

These are original explanations grounded in the cited mechanisms. Case premises are synthetic teaching situations, not claims that a particular commercial wine or winery used these methods. A proposed action is defensible under its stated conditions; it does not establish a uniquely optimal answer for every winery.

All items disable MCQ. Many options could be defensible under different unstated conditions, and the authoring does not invent three exclusive wrong actions. Short answer is self-checked through four individually cited items—action, mechanism, tradeoff and limitation. Each template scopes to exactly one case node so one case cannot accidentally draw another case’s rubric facts. Short answer does not provide official Diploma grading or machine evaluation of an essay.

All 42 principles map to WSET Level 3 at depth 2. Twenty-three foundational principles also map to WSET Level 2 at depth 2; 34 service-relevant principles map to CMS Certified at depth 2. The 24 case facts map directly to WSET Level 4 at depth 3. Level 4 also inherits the Level 3 foundations through the app’s track hierarchy. These editorial mappings are study selections, not examining-body endorsements.

`valid_from: 2026-09-26` records this content’s UTC curation boundary. The general mechanisms did not begin on that date. `last_verified_at` records the curator’s source check, while the independent expert-review status remains unverified.

Important qualifications stay in the assertions: machine harvesting can be suitable; skin extraction follows style; MLF is bacterial and is separate from alcoholic fermentation; diacetyl is conditional; oxygen has both useful and unwanted effects; sulfur dioxide does not inactivate Botrytis laccase; clear wine may be unstable or hard to filter; filtration can be followed by contamination; protein, tartrate and microbial stability are different targets.

This closes a first production-content gap, not the complete Diploma production unit. Further work includes alternative extraction technologies, systematic maturation economics, blending analysis, detailed sparkling and fortified methods, sweet-wine methods, packaging alternatives, faults and analytical exercises. No source PDF or source artwork is bundled; the app includes only original explanations and citation metadata.
