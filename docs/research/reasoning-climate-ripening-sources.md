The chain concerns direct conidial infection opportunity and risk; it does not equate humidity with wetness, assert a universal weather rule, diagnose noble rot or guarantee disease.

## Explicit wrong-option justifications

- Malate **more remaining** contradicts consumption of the substrate with replenishment matched. **Unchanged remaining** would require compensation excluded by the premise. **Necessary tartrate depletion** confuses different acids; the primary microvine comparison retained tartrate while malate changed.
- Botrytis **less risk through conducive longer wetness** reverses the controlled infection evidence. **Germination blocked by free water at a suitable temperature** conflicts with the laboratory germination result. **Certain infection of every berry** overstates the original mature-berry evidence and the scenario's risk-only conclusion.
