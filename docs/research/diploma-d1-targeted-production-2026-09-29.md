# D1 targeted production source audit

This integrated 0.24.16 batch addresses four named areas in the [WSET Level 4 Diploma in Wines specification, Issue 1.4 (2025)](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/17609/wset_l4wines_specification_en_august-2025.pdf): D1's regenerative and precision grape growing (printed p. 7), no/low-alcohol styles and alcohol reduction, hygiene/HACCP/ISO/traceability, and package/transport choices (printed pp. 8–9). WSET defines the study and assessment requirements. The app's 26 original assertions, five choices and four self-reviewed written cases are practice, not an assessed D1 result or an official WSET mark.

| Strand | Primary evidence | Bounded teaching claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regenerative vineyard decisions | [OIV-VITI 680-2024](https://www.oiv.int/node/3804) names cover, minimal disturbance, compost and lower persistent inputs as a regenerative purpose. [AWRI under-vine cover guidance](https://www.awri.com.au/industry_support/viticulture/weed-management/non-chemical-weed-management/sown-undervine-cover-crops/) documents erosion/infiltration benefits, vine competition, yield loss risk and site selection. | A dry slope calls for a measured cover trial, not a promise of carbon gain, superior grapes or a universal method. Cover species, seasonal mowing, available water, frost and labour can change the conclusion. Neither source confers a regenerative certificate. |
| Precision viticulture | [Wine Australia's mapping trial](https://www.wineaustralia.com/news/articles/new-trial-to-put-precision-site-mapping) describes vine/row maps, overlays and zone-specific management. [Bramley, Ouzman and Thornton's field study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/j.1755-0238.2011.00151.x) measures zone wines and a partial gross margin for selective harvest under its stated market assumptions. | Maps identify variation; ground checking is needed to find a cause. A variable-rate intervention or separated picking has value only if measured input savings or fruit/wine outcomes justify its extra cost. The study's profitable case cannot establish every vineyard's margin or a universal vigour-quality relationship. |
| No/low-alcohol production | [OIV-OENO 394A-2012](https://www.oiv.int/node/3274/download/pdf) lists partial vacuum evaporation, membrane techniques and distillation for sound wine under qualified oversight. The [OIV's 2025 technical account](https://oiv.int/press/12-months-12-resolutions-dealcoholisation-wines) discusses sensory balance, shelf stability and cost challenges. | The case asks for a supervised pilot and measurement. It does not promise a particular aroma profile, microbiological shelf life or legal product name from a process alone. Current destination law and final measured alcohol remain external checks. |
| Hygiene, HACCP, ISO and traceability | [OIV-OENO 630-2020](https://www.oiv.int/public/medias/7592/oiv-oeno-630-2020-en.pdf) distinguishes hygiene prerequisites, wine-specific hazard analysis, CCPs and traceability. The current [Codex CXC 1-1969 hygiene text](https://www.fao.org/fao-who-codexalimentarius/sh-proxy/pt/?lnk=1&url=https%253A%252F%252Fworkspace.fao.org%252Fsites%252Fcodex%252FStandards%252FCXC%2B1-1969%252FCXC_001e.pdf) describes validated limits, monitoring, correction, verification and records. [ISO's public 22000:2018 summary](https://www.iso.org/iso-22000-food-safety-management.html) defines a certifiable food-safety management system. [AWRI factsheet M8](https://www.awri.com.au/files/attachment/awissp-winery-factsheets/) links product, package and destination lots; its [packaging microbiology study](https://www.awri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/1704-tran-et-al-WVJ-30-2-2015.pdf) supports line investigation. | A contamination signal warrants a product hold, line investigation and lot reconstruction. A hypothetical defect is not automatically a food-safety CCP; the actual site analysis must determine hazard and control. Mentioning ISO does not assert a winery holds certification. Lot records narrow a withdrawal only if complete. |
| Packaging and transport | [AWRI's manufacturing guide](https://www.awri.com.au/files/attachment/wr163the/) discusses oxygen, format-specific life and bulk-transfer safeguards. [AWRI transport trials](https://www.awri.com.au/industry_support/winemaking_resources/storage-and-packaging/post-packaging/transport-and-storage/) tested particular wines/routes and found no consistent sensory preference between ISO and flexitank in that trial. [Wine Australia's packaging market bulletin](https://www.wineaustralia.com/news/market-bulletin/issue-297) documents the lower weight and potential freight cost of bulk shipping. | Compare glass, a suitable lighter pack and destination filling against the intended consumption period, oxygen, temperature, sensory condition, filling quality, traceability and full landed cost. The cited transport result is not a universal equivalence or a guarantee that bulk wine is cheaper or better in every market. |

Every item has a source-specific locator and remains `unverified`; generic MCQ generation is disabled. The five authored choices test constrained selections, while the four written cases expose action, reason, tradeoff and limitation points for learner self-review. The D1 scope selector now includes the quality-control nodes as well as the existing broad vineyard/cellar selectors. No Level 1–3 mapping, certification flag or passing threshold changes. This batch does not deliver a sustained timed 90-minute D1 answer, a complete unit, cellar cost accounting, or a qualified review of source claims.

The release baseline records 26 additional Diploma core study items (3,927 to 3,953) and five additional core items with useful choice practice (1,255 to 1,260); lower WSET and CMS track counts are unchanged. These are app coverage measures, not a WSET pass prediction. The corrected shared AWRI packaging citation now names the linked `Code of Good Wine Manufacturing Practice` rather than an inaccurate generic title. Curriculum lint reports zero errors and three existing curator-warning classes; static analysis is clean. The focused regression initially found only an obsolete exact Diploma item count in an older D4/D5 test; it was updated to the measured value, and that test file passed 9/9 on rerun. The other focused tests had passed. A subject expert still needs to verify every assertion.
