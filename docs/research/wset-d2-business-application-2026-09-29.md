# Diploma D2 applied business practice — source audit

The 0.24.17 batch adds 29 source-linked, item-specific choices to Diploma D2 study: 26 existing business assertions and three new core assertions. The three new areas are a conditional supply shock, an importer cost stack, and the five marketing Ps. The [WSET Level 4 specification, Issue 1.4](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/17609/wset_l4wines_specification_en_august-2025.pdf) sets the D2 scope; these app questions are formative practice, not WSET assessment.

| Decision in the question | Primary evidence | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |

Crop size does not determine a particular realised bottle price, margin or producer return. |

Actual duty, VAT accounting, responsibility for freight and other charges depend on product, origin, route, contract and date. No current rate is taught as a constant. |

A promotion alone cannot validate channel fit or demand. |

The other 26 questions refer to the citations already linked to their existing business facts. Their case figures and buyer terms are fictional premises, so the keyed answer applies only to the stated assumptions. The choices test fixed versus variable cost, contribution and break-even, cash timing, foreign-exchange exposure, producer models, routes and customer fit. Balanced alternatives include omitted costs, incorrect denominator or margin, and unsupported assumptions about demand. Generic MCQ generation remains disabled for these facts.

All new assertions remain `unverified` pending qualified subject review. The integrated baseline increases Diploma core useful practice from 1,260 to 1,289 of 2,682; Level 2 stays 552 of 748. Three existing shared facts gain Level 3 useful practice and eight gain CMS useful practice through their unchanged track mappings. Neither those counts nor this practice mark an official qualification.
