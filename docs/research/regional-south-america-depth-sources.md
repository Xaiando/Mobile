# Chile and Argentina regional depth comparisons — source note

Researched and authored 27 September 2026. This adds 44 original explanation/case points to the existing Americas comparisons: 14 paired principles (28 foundation points) and four hypothetical cases (16 action/reason/tradeoff/limitation points). It adds no map geometry, geographical nodes or containment relationships. All points remain **unverified**, with MCQ disabled: reading sources is not qualified curriculum verification.

The existing Americas assertions were read before authoring. This continuation adds Elqui, Leyda production, Peumo/Cachapoal seasonal management, Itata cultivation and tinaja production, Maule Carignan collaboration and a site trial, Bío-Bío, Salta canopy/grape context, Torrontés cultivar distinctions, Tulum/Pedernal, controlled Mendoza fermentation and harvest-capacity evidence, and Río Negro/Neuquén distinctions. It does not re-author the existing coastal Aconcagua, Maipo, Casablanca, Limarí, Colchagua, Mendoza altitude/UV/irrigation or general Patagonia latitude/wind assertions.

## Scope and mapping

All 44 points map to WSET Level 4 as core at depth 3. All 28 foundations map to CMS Certified as core at depth 2. The four cases map only to Level 4. The 28 WSET Level 3 foundations have 18 core and 10 secondary depth-2 mappings. The eight Itata/Maule heritage-and-Carignan foundations are secondary supporting enrichment: the pinned Level 3 table names the Southern Region and Maule but its principal Chile grape list does not name País, Moscatel, Cinsault or Carignan. The broad Salta red-grape association including Tannat and the detailed three-cultivar Torrontés distinction are also secondary. This is an editorial enrichment designation, not a claim that these ten points are new official required outcomes. Other foundations illustrate the named countries/regions and the specification’s human and natural factors.

The official Level 3 specification was actually read at printed pp. 12–15 (PDF pp. 13–16): Chile includes Elqui, San Antonio/Leyda, Cachapoal, Maule and Southern Region; Argentina includes Salta/Cafayate, San Juan, Mendoza and Patagonia. The pinned Diploma scope names Salta, San Juan, Mendoza and Patagonia. Standalone La Rioja expansion was deliberately omitted. WSET country objectives remain separate; CMS uses South America. Official specifications constrain editorial scope and are **not** factual citations for the regional mechanisms below.

Scope sidecar `build/south_america_depth_scope_groups.json` assigns each of the 18 subjects once: ten Chile (eight principles plus two cases) and eight Argentina (six principles plus two cases). Every full case premise is exactly retained in its subject name and question-template prompt. No inherited climate fact silently supplies a case measurement, and each case has one edge for each of the four existing roles.

## Cases and source-inference boundary

The Maule case tests a shared private marketing proposition against supplied wine variation, cost and missing buyer commitment. The Itata case states both wines are dry and sound, supplies the measured delicacy/structure difference, and treats skin and vessel changes as confounded rather than proving a clay effect. The Mendoza capacity case supplies two ready lots, insufficient internal capacity, qualified confirmed outside capacity and payment timing; it asks for a cost/cash decision process, not an invented affordable commitment. The Mendoza yeast case supplies matched must/handling and repeated coded results, while recognising one-must generalisation and tank-capacity limits.

Expected answers are original conditional applications. Sources support mechanisms and historical context; they do not themselves prescribe the hypothetical action, confirm buyer acceptance, prove an optimal schedule or confer legal approval. Samples and commitments absent from a prompt remain limits rather than hidden expected facts.
