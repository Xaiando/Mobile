# Diploma practice checkpoint — 29 September 2026

Release 0.24.55 measures **2,782/2,782 WSET Level 4 core authored items with useful practice** in the generated coverage baseline. The domain counts are geography 1,110/1,110, winemaking 683/683, viticulture 465/465, business 236/236, tasting 178/178, and service 110/110. These are the app's authored-item denominators, not the number of points in the [WSET Level 4 Diploma specification, Issue 1.4](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/17609/wset_l4wines_specification_en_august-2025.pdf). The baseline was regenerated after the business, geography/service, vineyard, winemaking and D1 cellar-method batches were combined. Core practice coverage must not be displayed as an official examination result, a WSET qualification, expert-verified accuracy or full syllabus parity.

The practice-closure batch adds item-specific, cited decisions and complete four-role cases for the existing 4,288 assertions. The D1 cellar-method continuation adds 16 more cited core assertions with useful practice; all 4,304 now remain `unverified`. Business case pools also close 33 vineyard and 19 winemaking roles that belonged to cross-domain scenarios. The 24 new D3 geography choices and 51 new winemaking choices have balanced correct-answer length ranks; their tests check source links, distinct options, plausible distractors and runtime delivery. Learners can also use the existing timed D1/D2 written rehearsal, D6 learner-owned research workspace and physical three-wine D4/D5 tasting flights. Those tools provide practice and recorded self-assessment, not automatic grading to the examining body's standard.

The [specification](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/17609/wset_l4wines_specification_en_august-2025.pdf) still identifies content and assessment work beyond this numerator:

- D1 now has sourced teaching and practice for flash détente, hyperoxidation, flotation, micro-oxygenation and oak-piece versus barrel choices. No authored teaching assertion has yet been found for layering, mass selection, head grafting, smoke/fire-risk management, cryoextraction, or pouch/can/glass-stopper comparisons. A second sourced D1 gap batch is underway; nearby generic grafting, clonal selection and carbonic maceration content does not close these precise omissions.
- D3 requires regional style, quality and price analysis and accurate comparative tasting. The atlas has broad named coverage, but three Vino Nobile Pievi, most Soave and Alto Adige UGAs, Paardeberg South and other fine registers still lack defensible distinct markers or full legal geometry. A named map target is not a complete regional lesson.
- D4 and D5 require product-specific analytical tasting and wider regional, legal, production and commercial comparisons. Physical tasting flights have been added, but their evidence is learner-entered and cannot establish sensory accuracy without a qualified reviewer.
- D6 practice supports independent source appraisal and drafting; the live assignment title and official assessment remain external to the app.

Before calling the Diploma study companion syllabus-complete, check each learning outcome and named product group against primary sources, add missing teaching and useful assessment practice, complete device-level scanner and tasting checks where applicable, and obtain qualified factual/editorial review. All 4,304 assertions currently remain `unverified`. The local focused tests, analyzer and lint results belong with the eventual PR evidence; hosted CI has not yet validated this isolated combined branch.
