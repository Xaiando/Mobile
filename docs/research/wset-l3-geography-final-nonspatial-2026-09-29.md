# WSET Level 3 geography: final non-spatial core batch — 29 September 2026

The prior local release had **982/1,060** WSET_L3 geography core assertions with useful served practice. Eleven remained outside clickable-map `LOCATED_IN` and regional `CASE_*` roles: eight `PRINCIPLE_EXPLANATION`, one `AWARDED_BY`, one `PRIVATE_CLASSIFICATION_OF`, and one `RESERVED_FOR_REGION`. This batch adds 11 source-linked, item-specific objective choices without changing the underlying knowledge assertions, curriculum mappings, map questions, or map geometry. Its scope is guided by the [WSET Level 3 Award in Wines specification](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf); the specification is not the factual answer source.

The same-date `CoverageChecker` now measures **993/1,060** useful WSET_L3 geography core assertions, with **67** remaining: 35 `LOCATED_IN` facts already served spatially, plus 32 regional `CASE_*` roles (11 action, 10 limitation, 8 reason, 3 tradeoff). The other agent's spatial and case work is separate. The whole WSET_L3 core rises from **1,625 to 1,636 of 2,232**. A second served format family is a practice-coverage signal, not a learner score, expert fact verification, or a WSET qualification. These 11 assertions remain `unverified` pending qualified review.

The choice recommends comparing structural red and fresher rosato categories while tasting the actual wines; it does not rank their quality. |
| `ki_wset_role_castilla_styles` | The linked [Castilla y León IGP specification](https://www.itacyl.es/documents/20143/0/PPCC%2BIGP%2BVTCYL%2B%282%29.pdf/dc9869da-3147-fa5a-273a-592a5e734187), §§2 and 6, includes white, rosé and red and authorised white/black grapes. The question avoids numerical limits and treats cellar route and oak as choices, not IGP guarantees. |
| `ki_wset_role_dornfelder_vine` | The [German Wine Institute's Dornfelder profile](https://www.deutscheweine.de/rebsorte/87/dornfelder?newsletter-type=%2Fnewsletter%2Fnewsmailer) notes a high-yield tendency, growers' crop reduction, relatively loose bunches with lower rot risk, and concern at frost-prone or very sandy/stonier sites. The choice asks for a vineyard response to these conditions, not a quality prediction from yield alone. |
| `ki_wset_role_friuli_cellar_cost` | [La Viarte's Friulano technical page](https://laviarte.it/en/products/friulano-magnum) describes one Colli Orientali DOC wine: temperature-controlled steel fermentation, six months on fine lees and further bottle rest. [Wine Australia's planning guidance](https://www.wineaustralia.com/growing-making/winery-management/planning-your-wine) treats inputs, capital, margins and style as planning considerations. The question calls this a producer *example* and asks only whether lees/storage have resource implications; it does not derive a retail price or compulsory DOC oak treatment. The live product price is intentionally unused. |
| `ki_wset_role_saumur_champigny_cellar` | The [Saumur-Champigny Cabernet Franc body](https://saumur-champigny.com/en/appellation/cabernet-franc/) describes youth, fruit, ripeness-dependent expression and development potential; the [InterLoire appellation guide](https://www.vinsdeloire.fr/sites/interloire/files/2025-11/guide-appellations-2024-bd_compressed.pdf) distinguishes early-drinking and selected longer-lived cuvées. [Wine Australia](https://www.wineaustralia.com/growing-making/winery-management/planning-your-wine) supports costed business planning. The question tests a contingent extraction/storage decision without claiming that time or oak guarantees improvement. |
| `ki_grosses_gewaechs_awarder` | Germany's [Weinverordnung §30(2)(2)(b), (4)–(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/weinv_1995/__30.html) names the Komitee – Klassifikation Erster und Großer Lagen in Deutschland e. V. for the statutory “Großes Gewächs” *Gütezeichen*, subject to an approved examination/award framework. [§54(23)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/weinv_1995/__54.html) permits older-rule use through the 2030 vintage, or until the new rules become effective sooner, and compliant stocks may be sold until exhausted. The prompt therefore asks which body §30 *names*, not which organization awarded any particular current bottle. This is legislation effective after 1 September 2026 and should be rechecked for future releases. |
| `ki_vdp_grosses_gewaechs_private` | The [VDP classification page](https://www.vdp.de/en/the-wines/classification) calls dry wine from VDP.GROSSE LAGE “VDP.GROSSES GEWÄCHS” and explicitly describes its classification as private. The same [Weinverordnung §30](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/weinv_1995/__30.html) separately provides for the statutory quality mark. The question keeps the private site hierarchy and legal mark distinct; it does not imply identical eligibility or that VDP awards the statutory mark. |
| `ki_wset_geo_valpolicella_ripasso_origin` | Italy's [MASAF Valpolicella Ripasso DOC production specification](https://www.masaf.gov.it/flex/cm/pages/ServeBLOB.php/L/IT/IDPagina/20313) identifies the protected wine name and its delimited grape area in the Verona/Valpolicella zone; the [consortium's area page](https://www.consorziovalpolicella.it/en/the-area/) corroborates the geographic setting. “Ripasso” is part of the wine designation, not a separately plotted town.
