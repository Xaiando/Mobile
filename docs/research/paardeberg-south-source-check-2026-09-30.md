## Existing evidence remains unresolved

The [SAWIS February 2026 register](https://www.sawis.co.za/cert/download/Production_areas_-_Eng_%26_Afr_-_Feb26.pdf) and [Board Notice 65 of 2018](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201805/41632bn65.pdf) establish legal identity and the ward's broad relationship to Paardeberg and Voor-Paardeberg. The [29 September source audit](fine-map-roadblocks-2026-09-29.md) records why Staart van Paardeberg and Slent are unsuitable substitutes.

The previous checkpoint established that the [SAWIS production-area portal](https://www.sawis.co.za/cert/productionareas.php) exposes a public [January 2026 ward overview PDF](https://www.sawis.co.za/cert/download/Wards_-__Jan2026.pdf), despite an industry-login banner. Its precision and reuse permission remain unresolved. In this pass the direct portal fetch timed out and the [SAWIS terms page](https://www.sawis.co.za/terms.php) could not be retrieved by the browser research tool. Those failures are not evidence of missing data, an authentication requirement, or a newly changed licence. No fresh SAWIS terms reading is claimed.

Public WOSA regional descriptions and winery listings found in the bounded search distinguish the broader Swartland/Paardeberg context. They do not establish a precise property's membership in the South ward. A producer address or winery coordinate cannot bridge that legal-identity gap on its own.
