## Access record and qualifications

The Wine Australia pages and OSU four-page PDF were read through the browser tool. Direct live HTTP 200 retrieval also recovered complete Wine Australia bodies, including the service-model and partner-evaluation tables and ordering/performance passages. Initial browser requests for both Penn State pages failed. Targeted searches then returned their complete indexed primary bodies; subsequent direct live HTTP 200 retrieval independently recovered the customer-service/fulfilment and restaurant-buyer/tasting passages. The browser failures remain recorded and are not described as successful browser fetches.

The Wine Australia routes page includes generalized control statements, old payment defaults, country-specific legal descriptions and an optimistic DTC profitability claim. None is turned into a curriculum rule. Services differ by the actual agreement. A distributor or retailer does not universally control every sales channel, and a logistics service is not assumed to supply active selling. The cases state their own allocation of consumer-order handling and data access; these are hypotheses, not laws or measured facts about all partners.

The page's term **depletion** is not authored as a universal consumer point-of-sale definition. Winery sell-in, a distributor's sales to accounts, account reorders and consumers' purchases are kept separate. Initial placement alone cannot establish the later stages. Reports should be requested where available; the pack does not imply that detailed customer-level data must be shared.

## Original cases and evidence alignment

- **DTC capacity:** supplied repeat online orders exceed the team's packing and customer-support capacity. One option outsources packing/delivery while support remains with the winery; another retailer handles its own consumer orders without sharing individual contacts. A trial of DTC, retail supply or a justified mix may work. The sourced functions explain the resource comparison; the specified customer-access terms explain the trade-off. Missing full costs and demand prevent a claim about the most profitable route.
- **Downstream review:** the winery sees only shipments to its partner, despite retail placement. The partner has competing brands and possible restaurant access; the restaurant's interest expressly depends on menu fit, a staff tasting and replenishment. Requesting downstream evidence, reviewing the retail route or a resourced restaurant trial is defensible. Limited promotional resources and uncertain reporting/purchase response prevent an automatic preference for more listings or another channel.

Prompts, capacity constraints and commercial terms are invented controlled conditions. Citations support the business mechanisms and evaluation methods, not an assertion that a source observed these particular wineries. The answer points leave room for alternatives that address the conditions. No cost arithmetic, sales threshold or official numeric rubric is supplied.
