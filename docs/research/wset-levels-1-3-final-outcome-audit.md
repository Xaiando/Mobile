I independently reviewed the other authors' general outcomes/production, European applications, regional grape roles and sparkling/fortified closure earlier, and now checked their current catalog selections. This audit checks assembly and recorded semantic acceptance without presenting internal review as qualified expert verification. Original lessons retain their actual `unverified` status.

## High-risk semantic closure checks

For all thirty `l2_grape_*` profiles, the selected `ki_wset_structure_<grape>_structure` point now gives actual finished-colour/acid/sugar-potential-alcohol/aroma context and black-grape tannin context. The eight principal varieties additionally select their vineyard/harvest point, specific cellar examples and appropriate development/ageing lessons. For example, Pinot Noir's acidity and relatively low tannin, Cabernet's acid tendency, Syrah's colour/tannin/qualified acid and Grenache's lighter-colour/lower-acid/high-potential-alcohol tendencies are explicit. Barbera, Cortese, Garganega and Verdicchio no longer rely on incomplete old profiles; Montepulciano and Zinfandel have bounded documented acidity examples. Generic cellar lessons explain choices, not a rule that every producer uses MLF/new oak or ages every bottle.

The exact grape-origin rows retain `ki_wset_origin_condrieu_viognier`, `_touraine_sauvignon`, `_provence_grenache`, `_priorat_grenache`, `_stellenbosch_merlot`, `_western_cape_chardonnay` and `_carneros_pinot` where broad climate/permission evidence was insufficient. `ki_wset_found_familiar_chablis`, `ki_wset_nw_napa_cabernet` and `ki_wset_ofinal_recioto_corvina` are now selected in the three final repaired pairs. Named producer examples remain bounded to the documented style rather than universal regional claims.

The earlier sparkling/fortified review's concrete repairs are present: counter-pressure has the required Level 3 mapping; Prosecco, Asti, Cava and Manzanilla legal sources have actual IT/ES jurisdictions; three country examples have Level 2 mappings; and advanced-method correct answers are Level 3-only in rehearsal version `.4`. Current Asti routes, Sherry fortification qualifications, Cava category ages and blend-age Port distinctions remain qualified. Champagne's total pre-release minimum is not confused with an all-lees period.
