## Content and evidence

The producer-model module compares integrated growing/winemaking, vineyard asset commitments, custom-production capacity and scope, cooperative resources and control, purchased grapes and existing bulk wine. Its cases use a confirmed custom-production proposal for a first lot and growers who explicitly prefer shared resources over individual brand control.

The routes module compares direct-sales resources and customer records, upstream shipments and downstream sales, logistics versus selling services, retailer access, restaurant fit, partner evaluation and reviewable roles. Its cases ask learners to compare fulfilment-supported direct sales with retailer supply and assess a distribution relationship using downstream evidence and a conditional restaurant trial. More than one approach can be defensible where the premise permits it.

Each case supplies its commercial conditions. Sources support the underlying mechanisms; they do not document the fictional wineries, quote their costs or promise their outcomes. The [producer-model source note](business-producer-models-sources.md) and [routes source note](business-routes-to-market-sources.md) record exact primary passages, retrieval limits and exclusions. Primary references include USDA, Wine Australia, Penn State and Oklahoma State extension material, plus attributed winery/cooperative examples. Some Penn State browser requests returned 403; successful indexed original bodies or separately recorded direct HTTP retrieval supplied the relevant passages. The historical vineyard and shipping publications support qualitative mechanisms only: numerical prices, carrier rules, taxes and legal assertions are excluded.

All 32 items remain **unverified**, with generic MCQ disabled. Sixteen principles are WSET_L4 core depth 2 and sixteen case points are core depth 3. The addition contains 52 nodes, 32 relations, 32 aliases, 32 mappings, 15 canonical sources and 59 citation links. No new schema, relation type or question engine is needed.
