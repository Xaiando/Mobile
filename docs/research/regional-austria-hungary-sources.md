## Primary regional text actually checked

The following live first-party pages were read. Locators in item citations identify the relevant text; none of the records is used to import current appellation permission lists, ratios, release dates, must-weight thresholds or maturation minima.

| Source ID | Primary text and selected support |
| --- | --- |
| `src_reg_ah_wachau` | [Vinea Wachau: The Wachau](https://www.vinea-wachau.at/en/the-wine-region/the-wachau). Pannonian eastern air and cooler, wetter forest-valley downslope currents from Waldviertel/Dunkelsteinerwald. No fixed plot maturity, geological flavour transfer or promotional quality guarantees. |
| `src_reg_ah_wachau_walls` | [Vinea Wachau: dry-stone walls](https://www.vinea-wachau.at/en/the-wine-region/dry-stone-walls). Soil retention/erosion control, skilled construction, local/quarried stone supply and upkeep effort. No terrace-cost-to-quality inference. |
| `src_reg_ah_kremstal` | [Kremstal committee: Climate & Soil](https://kremstalwein.at/en/winegrowing-region/climate-soil/). Warm eastern and cooler northern air, day/night variation, Danube heat moderation; actual named Frechau–Sandgrube deep water-retentive loess and Grillenparz shallow stony drought-prone profiles. No mineral-flavour claims or freedom from frost. |
| `src_reg_ah_kamptal` | [Weinstraße Kamptal: Klima und Böden](https://www.weinkultur-kamptal.at/der-wein-im-kamptal/klima-und-boeden/). Pannonian/Waldviertel contrast; variable-depth brown earth over gneiss, loess and Kamp-side clay-rich loam. German text read; no direct geology-to-flavour inference. |
| `src_reg_ah_weinviertel_climate` | [Weinviertel committee: Das Klima im Weinviertel](https://www.weinvierteldac.at/en/wine-in-weinviertel/climate-geology/das-klima-im-weinviertel/). Retz rain shadow on Manhartsberg’s eastern flank and stronger southeastern Pannonian influence. Old station averages and the page’s imprecise regional-boundary sentence were not used. |
| `src_reg_ah_weinviertel_gv` | [Weinviertel committee: Grüner Veltliner](https://www.weinvierteldac.at/en/weinviertel-dac/gruener-veltliner/). Relatively late ripening and broad pepper/fruit-associated style range. Region, grape and DAC permissions are not treated as interchangeable. |
| `src_reg_ah_mittelburgenland` | [Verband Blaufränkisch Mittelburgenland: Klima & Boden](https://www.blaufraenkischland.at/blaufraenkischland/klima-boden/). Hill shelter/eastern opening, deep heavy loam water storage and local soil variation. No blanket drought resistance or clay-created tannin/quality claim. |
| `src_reg_ah_eisenberg` | [Eisenberg DAC: Home](https://www.eisenberg-dac.at/en/home/). “Vineyards” identifies steep, stony, narrow plots and inheritance subdivision with common hand work. Iron-flavour, hail-dispersal, universal weather protection, elevation rankings and guaranteed preserved acidity were excluded. |
| `src_reg_ah_leithaberg` | [Verein Leithaberg: 10 Dinge](https://www.leithaberg.at/10-dinge-die-man-ueber-leithaberg-wissen-muss/), published 20 August 2023. Lake heat storage and forest-ridge cooling plus selected white/Blaufränkisch associations. Legal geography/statistics, hierarchy/quality implications and “schist gives smoky reduction” style claims were excluded. |
| `src_reg_ah_neusiedler_climate` | [Neusiedlersee committee: Klima – Luft](https://neusiedlersee-dac.wine/klima-luft/). Autumn mist and humidity associated with the lake favour botrytis. The universal noble-rot guarantee, sensory transfer from salty air and origin-quality claims were excluded; the general requirement for useful fruit condition/drying is separately supported by the Tokaj vine text. |
| `src_reg_ah_neusiedler_wines` | [Neusiedlersee committee: Weine](https://neusiedlersee-dac.wine/weine/). Selected Zweigelt association and steel/wood cellar routes, distinct from sweet white production. No legal alcohol, harvest, category, release or minimum-ageing assertions were imported from the page. |
| `src_reg_ah_styria_climate` | [Wein Steiermark: Climate](https://steiermark.wine/en/original/terroir/climate/). West/south relative rainfall, fog/storm/hail context and microclimate variation. No statistical averages, immunity or altitude-only coolness claim. |
| `src_reg_ah_styria_work` | [Wein Steiermark: in the vineyard & cellar](https://steiermark.wine/en/artisinal/in-the-vineyard-cellar/). Steep-site manual work across vineyard operations and harvest passes, time and mechanisation constraints. No statutory slope definition or complete wood-use claim. |
| `src_reg_ah_south_styria` | [Wein Steiermark: Südsteiermark](https://steiermark.wine/en/original/winegrowing-regions/sudsteiermark/). Sauvignon Blanc association and steep vineyard context. Old areas/percentages, uniformly high quality and manual work guarantees of individuality were excluded. |
| `src_reg_ah_styria_sites` | [Wein Steiermark: Südsteiermark Ortswein districts](https://steiermark.wine/en/original/winegrowing-regions/sudsteiermark/ortswein-districts/). Kitzeck-Sausal Alpine exposure/longer season; Gamlitz sheltered basins, exposed hills and stream-influenced settings. Fixed later-harvest averages, elevation-derived flavour thresholds and soil-to-spice/minerality were excluded. |
| `src_reg_ah_vienna` | [Wiener Wein: Wiener Gemischter Satz](https://www.wienerwein.at/wienerwein/wienergemischtersatz). Mixed white planting, joint harvest/processing and differing flowering/development patterns. The historical absolute crop-failure insurance claim and current legal blend/grape thresholds were excluded. |
| `src_reg_ah_tokaj_climate` | [Tokaj Council: Climate](https://winesoftokaj.hu/en/tokaj/climate). Continental heat/cold, southern slope shelter, river/lowland humidity and sunny autumn drying. Quantitative weather averages/fixed dates and a simplistic unripe-fruit-only explanation of grey rot were excluded. |
| `src_reg_ah_tokaj_soils` | [Tokaj Council: Soils](https://winesoftokaj.hu/en/tokaj/soils). Varied volcanic-derived/weathered tuff brown forest/clay soils; Tokaj Hill and nearby Tokaj/Tarcal/Bodrogkeresztúr loess. No mineral-flavour transfer or soil-based quality ranking. |
| `src_reg_ah_tokaj_grapes` | [Tokaj Council: Grapes](https://winesoftokaj.hu/en/tokaj/grapes). Furmint/Hárslevelű relative profiles, dry sound-ripe harvest target, successive aszú development and selective passes. Historical genetic/origin claims, percentages, complete permissions, fixed dates and a fixed pass count were excluded. |
| `src_reg_ah_tokaj_vine` | [Tokaj Council: Aszú on the vine](https://winesoftokaj.hu/en/the-aszu/on-the-vine). Ripe healthy starting fruit, damp infection conditions and sufficiently long dry periods for useful shrinkage/concentration. No numerical composition recipe or claim that every infection is desirable. |
| `src_reg_ah_tokaj_maturing` | [Tokaj Council: Maturing](https://winesoftokaj.hu/en/tokaj/maturing). Oak effects on aroma/colour and cool, stable underground cellar storage. No mandatory duration, barrel dimension, ventilation recipe or guaranteed quality improvement. |

The [Council’s organisation page](https://winesoftokaj.hu/en/tbht/szervezeti-felepites) was checked to establish the first-party publisher: Tokaji Borvidék Hegyközségi Tanácsa. It is background provenance, not an extra item citation or duplicate record.

## Access and interpretation limits

Several Austrian Wine Marketing Board HTML pages and its Kremstal May 2024 brochure returned HTTP 403. Indexed brochure text was inspected during research, then **excluded from final citations** after the live Kremstal committee page supplied the selected mechanisms and named profiles. Final regional references are live first-party text rather than an unread blocked brochure. One Wiener Gemischter Satz producer-group route redirected to its homepage and was excluded in favour of the stable Wiener Wein page. The cash-flow browser tool timed out and the Ohio PDF browser tool returned 403; direct HTTP retrieval succeeded and the relevant text was read.

Current Austria/Hungary legal content was read to avoid repetition and stale legal import. In particular, the existing canonical Tokaj specification `src_hu_tokaj_2025` governs the current graph. The Council’s separate “Wines” page contains an older dry-Szamorodni ageing statement that differs from the current 2025 specification. That page and its regulatory figures are excluded from this module’s citations. The Council’s “Aszú in the cellar” page was inspected but not added as a source: the new points do not repeat existing legal steeping or free-run Eszencia assertions, use historic puttonyos measurements, or import its yield/alcohol/sugar figures and minimum ageing. The new region module does not revise any law file.

## Conditional-case boundaries

All case premises are original and retained in full, identically, in subject node names and scoped templates. Each case has its own four role edges; no generic shared case node or geographical edges were added.

- Vienna establishes a joint harvest and currently sound fruit, one sweeter component and missing combined-lot balance. It proposes assessment rather than an exact date, a composition threshold or a legally required production change.
- Südsteiermark gives actual block samples, one crew, steep access and a rain forecast. Priority follows the supplied ready lot and capacity constraint; regional later-harvest tendencies are context, not proof of readiness or safe waiting.
- Tokaj berry selection explicitly supplies a qualified distinction between desirable shrivelling and damaging decay. The response separates suitability/handling decisions without prescribing pesticide treatment or asserting guaranteed future yields.
- Tokaj maturation supplies an optional extension after release clearance, continuing costs and buyer payment after delivery. The cash mechanism is general business reasoning applied to a hypothetical producer; affordability, future taste, customer demand and legal release requirements are separate questions. More oak or time is not automatically better, and no real price or market return is asserted.
