### Feedback and assumption boundaries

1. **Contribution:** variable cost is `13 + 2 + 1 = 16`; contribution is `24 − 16 = 8`. Fixed cost, tax and interest are excluded from these supplied unit inputs. EUR 8 is contribution, not net profit or an automatically defined gross margin. Do not omit packaging or fulfilment because the wine-only cost appears first.
2. **Whole-bottle threshold:** `ceil(10001 / (24 − 16)) = ceil(1250.125) = 1251`. At 1,250 bottles contribution is EUR 10,000, which is insufficient; at 1,251 it is EUR 10,008. The discrete coverage threshold is not the fractional algebraic break-even point. State constant price and variable cost within the range, one-product sales mix and sufficient capacity. The inputs do not require a legal minimum flag.
3. **Receipt:** `10000 × 75 / 100 = 7500 EUR`. The quote direction is explicit: each invoiced USD buys EUR 0.75. Do not invert it or silently assume USD is the home currency. The exercise supplies a settlement rate; it makes no statement about current rates, fees, hedging availability or customer default.
4. **Reduction:** comparison receipt `10000 × 80 / 100 = 8000`; settlement receipt `7500`; positive reduction `8000 − 7500 = 500 EUR`. The question asks for the decrease amount, not the final receipt or a percentage. Independently verify with integer cents or decimal arithmetic. Binary floating-point evaluation of `10000 × (0.8 − 0.75)` may give `500.00000000000045`; do not widen the exact grading tolerance to accommodate the test's arithmetic.
5. **Landed cost:** `9000 + 1000 + 500 + 500 + 2000 = 13000`; divide by the explicitly saleable 1,000 bottles to obtain `13 EUR`. All charges and recoverability are fictional premises, not a jurisdiction's tariff or VAT ruling. Recoverability does not establish that no tax cash needs funding; the question asks the stated nonrecoverable cost target, not cash required or a selling price. Do not invent import duty rates, losses or retail markups.
6. **Cash gap:** `47 − 12 = 35` elapsed days. The prompt explicitly excludes inclusive calendar counting and does not ask for Day 47 as a duration. A profitable sale can still require financing over the supplied interval, but the scenario does not specify or recommend an interest rate or funding amount.

All six results were checked independently using the integer arithmetic and boundary calculations above. The authored test repeats those calculations without reading back a quantity as its derivation. The first runtime attempt stopped at ingestion because of a duplicate template; those D2 assertions were not reached. After the bounded registration and widget-fixture repairs, all five D2 tests executed and passed in the final 26-test focused group. The exact FX reduction uses integer cents; no grader tolerance was widened.
