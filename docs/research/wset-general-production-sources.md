## Source and legal safeguards

- Conditional mechanisms are distinguished from mandatory recipes. UK, EU and Australian guidance does not establish worldwide permission. OIV recommendations are not binding law everywhere.
- The 2001 closure trial identifies closure families tested; the later AWRI oxygen discussion supplies performance context. Neither cork nor screwcap guarantees quality or successful ageing.
- The historical Productivity Commission report supports harvest labour-saving mechanisms. Its old adoption percentages and dollar estimates are not presented as current figures.
- Organic and biodynamic requirements depend on standards and jurisdiction. The 2026 Demeter standard describes its own certification; philosophical claims are not presented as demonstrated sensory superiority. EU organic wine can contain permitted sulphites.
- Yield, vine density, hand harvest, new oak, more maturation and higher price do not universally establish better sensory quality. Cost explanations condition on usable output, operations and intended markets.
- Chemical dosing prescriptions and universal temperature or closure limits are omitted.
