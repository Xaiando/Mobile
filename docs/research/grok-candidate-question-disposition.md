## Six-case evidence and rubric

`ShortAnswerFormat` pools by subject and relation. A fixed prompt needs exactly one `scope_node_id`. It presents the planned item plus **one** other unstudied item on a first encounter, even if the pool has four. The new cases therefore contain exactly two facts; any split of an existing four-fact case must ensure both new two-point views keep all four original item IDs in pools. No candidate answer is automatically graded, and distractor explanations are editorial notes only.

1. **Roman texts versus modern controls.** `n_hcourse_case_rome_limit` has two unverified/mcq_disabled knowledge items, WSET_L4/CMS_CERTIFIED secondary mappings, and per-item citations. `CASE_ACTION`/`ki_hcourse_case_rome_action` rubric: “Pliny's Falernian ranking and Columella's vine/site husbandry are not a controlled-appellation rule.” Evidence: `ki_hcourse_rome_rank` (`src_hist_pliny_falernian`) and `ki_hcourse_rome_site` (`src_hcourse_columella`). `CASE_REASON`/`ki_hcourse_case_rome_reason` rubric: “The opened Roman passages do not specify a delimited production area, permitted-grape list, yield ceiling, or minimum strength; the cited later French code does.” Evidence: `ki_hcourse_rome_not_law` (`src_hcourse_columella`, `src_hist_code_vin_1935`) and `ki_hcourse_modern_aoc` (`src_hist_code_vin_1935`). The wording avoids a universal claim that Roman law never had origin controls.

2. **1933, 1935, 1976 are different kinds of evidence.** `n_hcourse_case_scales` has two items and citations. `CASE_ACTION`/`ki_hcourse_case_scales_action` rubric: “The Twenty-first Amendment repealed US Prohibition on 5 December 1933; the cited French code ties AOC conditions to the 30 July 1935 decree.” Evidence: `ki_hcourse_modern_ban` (`src_hist_archives_amendments`, `src_hist_archives_repeal`) and `ki_hcourse_modern_aoc` (`src_hist_code_vin_1935`). `CASE_REASON`/`ki_hcourse_case_scales_reason` rubric: “The 24 May 1976 Paris blind tasting was one comparison, neither legislation nor a permanent world ranking.” Evidence: `ki_hcourse_modern_tasting` (`src_hist_paris_1976`). The French code is qualified **as published for 25 November 1967**, without claiming its 1935 formulation is current law.

3. **1963 Italian Articles 2/3 and repeal.** `n_hcourse_case_repeal` reuses its four fact IDs: `ki_hcourse_case_repeal_action` now gives the three historical names and absence of DOC/DOCG abbreviations (`ki_hcourse_doc_three`, `src_hcourse_dpr_930_compilation`); `ki_hcourse_case_repeal_tradeoff` gives Article 3's traditional-variety/local fair constant use/default municipal zone (`ki_hcourse_doc_semplice`, same source); `ki_hcourse_case_repeal_reason` retains the current Article 2 view's 2010 abrogation (`ki_hcourse_doc_repeal`, `src_hcourse_dpr_930_art2_current`); `ki_hcourse_case_repeal_limit` says the compilation is not the Gazzetta, Article 4 is blank, 2010 Article 1 was opened separately (`src_hcourse_dlgs_61_art1`), the 2010 decree was itself marked abrogated (`src_hcourse_dlgs_61_current`), and current DOC/DOCG comes from 2016 Article 28 (`src_hcourse_l238_art28`). The case now has two complementary two-point views: historical names/repeal (`CASE_ACTION` + `CASE_REASON`) and semplice definition/source boundary (`CASE_TRADEOFF` + `CASE_LIMITATION`). `n_hcourse_case_doc` remains the separate Article 1 source-view exercise.

4. **Astringency versus a guest's bitter fish match.** `n_somm_case_pair` keeps its four IDs. `ki_somm_case_pair_action`/`tradeoff` cover acknowledging the failed pairing and offering another wine without claiming one correct pour. `ki_somm_case_pair_reason` covers the review's provisional tannin–salivary-protein account and unsettled mechanism. `ki_somm_case_pair_limit` now keeps bitterness distinct from drying/puckering astringency, and says the review does not establish tannin binding fish protein or test this dish. Evidence: `ki_somm_astr_mechanism`, `ki_somm_astr_not_food`, `ki_somm_case_two_limit`, `src_somm_mcrae_astringency` (McRae & Kennedy 2011, abstract and seed-tannin discussion). The alcohol/service caveat remains. This subject was removed from the broad sommelier template and given two fixed two-point templates: response/alternative (`CASE_ACTION` + `CASE_TRADEOFF`) and mechanism/limit (`CASE_REASON` + `CASE_LIMITATION`). The guest's reported bitterness is not treated as if the paper established its mechanism.

5. **Calvados versus cider and INAO area counts.** No new CASE item. `ki_somm_case_cider_action/reason/limit` establish that Calvados is distilled from cider/perry and, on the INAO product note, aged at least two years in oak; the note states no minimum strength. `ki_somm_case_count_action/reason/limit` explain the note's contradictory commune counts and why the unopened cahier, not the note, would be legal boundary authority. Evidence: `ki_somm_calvados_distillate/area`, `src_somm_inao_calvados`; `src_bev_uk_cider_2023` defines only a UK duty category, not Calvados. Keep as a two-case learning sequence, not a new composite lesson.

6. **Armagnac still, oak, strength.** No new CASE item. `ki_somm_case_plates_action/limit` teach the BNIA page's **about 95%** continuous copper Armagnac alambic and reject a universal statutory claim or plate-by-plate/off-still instructions. `ki_somm_case_oakwood_action/reason/limit` teach 400-litre oak pièces mostly Gascony **or Limousin** and the stated 40% sale minimum; bottled spirit no longer ages in `ki_somm_armagnac_identity`. Evidence: `ki_somm_armagnac_identity/oak`, `src_somm_bnia_distillation`. Keep as two existing cases. If an exact combined one-prompt rehearsal becomes essential, the two principle facts under `n_somm_armagnac` can support a new two-point template, but that would repeat the cases and is not recommended here.
