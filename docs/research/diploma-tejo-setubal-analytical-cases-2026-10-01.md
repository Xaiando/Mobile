## Source distinctions

The [commission terroir page](https://www.cvrtejo.pt/terroir) describes four contexts. The [January 2026 IG Tejo specification](https://www.ivv.gov.pt/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/IG_Tejo.pdf), section 7, describes three soil settings. The [DoTejo PDO file](https://www.ivv.gov.pt/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/DO_Do-Tejo.pdf), section 5, lists six formal geographic sub-regions. They are different classifications, not conflicting claims for one statutory count. Source edition and locators are retained. Promotional soil-to-flavour and universal quality language is excluded.

The [commission cultivar profiles](https://www.cvrtejo.pt/castas) support Fernão Pires/Arinto roles without a mandatory recipe, universal harvest date, planted-share percentage or price claim. Matched parcel and harvest evidence is a decision constraint; the invented trials change more than one variable.

The [IVV regional overview](https://www.ivv.gov.pt/regioes-vitivinicolas/mapa-das-regioes/peninsula-de-setubal/), updated 24 March 2026, and [IGP product file](https://www.ivv.gov.pt/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/IG_Peninsula-de-Setubal.pdf), sections 1/2, 5–7, support the regional/cultivar/product boundary. The IGP file is not a Setúbal DO or Palmela DO specification. The differing climate-amplitude descriptions are not turned into a fixed rule; only qualitative maritime, river, relief and soil context is taught.

Independent primary-text research found [Portaria 118/2014](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/portaria/118-2014-25345927) Article 5 distinguishes general Setúbal composition from traditional Moscatel names. [Portaria 346/2015](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/portaria/346-2015-70508974), [Aviso 13722/2024/2](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/aviso/13722-2024-870941977) and [Aviso 19991/2025/2](https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/2s/2025/08/152000000/0021300213.pdf) concern Article 12/age indications in the retrieved texts. The official Palmela download route timed out; that does not prove absence.
