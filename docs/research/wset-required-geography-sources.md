# Required WSET geography closure: provenance and scope

Checked 27 September 2026. Original study facts remain unverified and require qualified review. The WSET specifications define which topics to study; they are not the factual or coordinate sources. This pack does not certify a learner’s qualification.

Scope: Level 2 2026 Issue 2.1 named grape/origin list (80 European and 69 New World pairs), Level 3 May 2022 Issue 2 named places, plus required sparkling and fortified origin contexts identified by the independent review. New World explanatory groups include contextual subareas; these are mapped at Level 3 unless present in the exact Level 2 grape/origin list.

The evidence JSON records 410 contextual requirement rows and their canonical identities, exact location-item IDs, lowest study level and integration recipes. These are references to the same places across grape/style requirements, not 410 independent places. Existing geographical facts receive only missing required-level mappings. Existing secondary mappings requiring core treatment are listed for the root integrator; they are not duplicated.

## Coordinates and boundaries

GeoNames: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt documents CC BY 4.0 and WGS84 coordinates. Curated rows retain original exact GeoNames IDs, feature classes/codes, country-download URLs, raw archive SHA-256, modification dates and source coordinates. Point metadata explicitly identifies a named locality or administrative orientation reference, never an official wine centroid or legal boundary. No offset or invented polygon distinguishes overlapping designations.

St. Helena: UC Davis Library/DataLab AVA project, pinned commit 7af5b29d45aee5e6c6ce3889e9b1de19085d7107, aggregate SHA-256 c90068f7154764519f0a7c6fb2788e597b7c56bf7581e8c0faa979554d984926, CC0. A deterministic horizontal scan of 63 interior scanlines selects the midpoint of the widest verified interior interval, accounting for polygon holes. The point is a community-polygon reference; the current TTB legal identity is cited separately.

French area recipes select exact IDA values from the existing licensed INAO 2025-10-09 commune-area snapshot and IGN 2026 commune boundaries. These unions locate appellation communes; they are not cadastral vineyard polygons. Anjou’s shared area recipe is explicitly shared for Rosé d’Anjou and Cabernet d’Anjou.

World framing is an aggregate of licensed Natural Earth land polygons, not a drawn rectangular boundary. Country-level location facts provide actual click-location practice without a false country-to-country relation. China is not promoted into Levels 1–3.

## Protected names and genuine places

Burgundy is a wine region; Bourgogne is also a protected regional appellation. Bourgogne Côte d’Or, Hautes Côtes and Côte Chalonnaise are geographical denominations within that regional appellation. County names differ from California AVAs. Navarra DO, Catalunya DO, Valencia DO, La Mancha DO and Castilla y León IGP differ from similarly named administrative regions. Chile’s wine-zoning regions are identified from Decreto 464; Aconcagua’s broad region differs from Aconcagua Valley.

Alsace Grand Cru is recorded as a category of named protected sites, with the existing Alsace region and named cru geometry supplying orientation. Amarone, Recioto and Ripasso use their actual Valpolicella or Soave origin context; wine-production labels do not justify artificial geographical separation. Port/Porto is tied to demarcated Douro, rather than Porto city. Cap Classique is not created as a place.

## Containment correction after real geometry build

The actual licensed area unions expose four differences from generic regional reference shapes: Bourgogne includes 15 Rhône département communes outside the existing Burgundy frame; Crémant de Bourgogne includes 88 outside that frame; Crémant de Loire includes 30 outside the existing Loire frame; Muscat de Beaumes-de-Venise includes Aubignan outside the existing Southern Rhône reference. Their new LOCATED_IN facts now use France, a true containing country, while preserving the proper named wine context in original assertions. The existing shapes and source coordinates are unchanged. INAO commune-register citations supply the exact provenance.
