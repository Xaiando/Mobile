# Diploma D3 regional theory application — integrated 0.24.18

This batch adds **23 original study prompts** for WSET Level 4 D3: 19 four-option applications of existing cited regional facts and four written decisions with 16 new cited rubric points. It does not add 19 new facts. Every fact is pending qualified expert review; the written format self-checks selected points and cannot assess a Diploma essay or tasting answer.

The [2025 WSET Level 4 specification, Issue 1.4](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/17609/wset_l4wines_specification_en_august-2025.pdf) is used only to set scope. D3 asks learners to explain how growing environment, vineyard and winery choices, regulation and business affect the style, quality and price of principal still wines. The material here addresses selected causal and commercial decisions in Spain, Portugal, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, Chile and Argentina. It is a discrete extension of D3, not a claim of complete regional study. The existing [gap audit](wset-level-4-gap-audit.md) remains open.

Every wrong answer is written to test a distinct overreach (uniform regional climate, guaranteed quality/price, or unsupported cellar rule). Some sources contain promotional quality claims; those are not used as evidence of a particular wine's quality. The cases require a wine-specific sensory or financial check where the premise lacks it. All case costs and purchase details are explicit hypotheticals.
