## Interpretation limits

The Stellenbosch and Carneros producer records establish actual local variety/style associations, while their exact barrel regimes and body descriptions are not universal regional rules. The Provence La Londe point provides a real Grenache rosé example within Côtes de Provence. WOSA supports the Cape Chardonnay spectrum; the De Wetshof example demonstrates that lees handling need not mean barrel ageing. Appellation grape identity facts are reused beside profile and cellar explanations rather than being treated as style evidence alone. Full exact replacement IDs and existing-item mapping promotions are recorded in the accompanying JSON.
