# WSET Level 2 geography non-location practice closure — 29 September 2026

This batch adds 33 original four-option, item-specific questions to existing core
geography facts. It does not add map points or change the WSET syllabus mapping.
The questions are source linked and remain **unverified pending qualified review**.

## Coverage and scope

The bundled `CoverageChecker`, measured on 2026-09-29, reports Level 2 geography
core useful practice rising from **220/276 to 253/276**. The 33 changed facts
previously had recall-only flashcard/typed/short-answer practice. The new choice
adds recognition with a second practice family. The remaining non-`LOCATED_IN`
gaps are `ki_chablis_grape`, `ki_champagne_chardonnay`, and
`ki_champagne_pinot_noir`: they already have recognition but lack another
family, so another multiple-choice question would not close them. The other 20
remaining geography gaps are location relations and need a separate map audit.

The 33 questions span 25 regional principles, three German protected-category
relations, and five reserved-wine origins. Answer positions are 8/8/8/9 across
indices 0–3, and no keyed answer is the unique shortest or longest option.
Correct answers preserve the stated qualifications. Every keyed
question cites a public primary source already attached to its knowledge item;
the Bordeaux Supérieur item gains a direct Bordeaux wine-body citation for its
red/sweet-white distinction. Coverage measures available practice, not learner
mastery, qualification, or expert approval.
