## Concrete closure work

1. **Complete process order**. `assets/curriculum/areas/wset_foundations.yaml:783`, `:793`, `:803` teach when white/red/rosé skins are separated; `assets/progress/wset_scope.json:67` links only those fragments plus fermentation. They do not teach a complete route through crushing, draining/pressing where appropriate, maturation and bottling, or what those stages mean. Add original ordered routes plus elementary step definitions rather than change the already useful skin-contact lessons. Root is authoring a disjoint L1 closure pack.

2. **Umami/intensity missing at L1**. `wset_wine_service.yaml:1644`, `:1652`, `:1700`, `:1708` map `ki_wset_srv_food_umami_bitter`, `_umami_choice`, `_intensity_match`, `_intensity_preference` to L2 only. L1 requires both topics. Promote these beginner principles by explicit L1 mappings and add both groups to L1 required catalog; leave decanting at its appropriate level. Update service source documentation, whose L2-only statement was the mistaken scope decision.

3. **Named examples need characteristics**. `wset_foundations.yaml:1023` (Côtes du Rhône), `:1033` (Chianti), `:1053` (Châteauneuf-du-Pape) currently teach identity, principal grapes and wine colour. L1 has no richer Sangiovese/Grenache lessons and these assertions give no aroma, body or structural example. Add beginner conditional style examples. Rioja has fruit-focused/matured variation but would benefit from an explicit red-fruit/oak and structural example too. This is educational sufficiency, not a claim that every bottle must share one profile.

4. **Climate definition/effects**. `wset_foundations.yaml:743` mentions cooler ripening/acidity and cooling exceptions, but does not define cool versus warm growing conditions or explain the usual sugar/ripeness direction. Add a simple conditional comparison (same variety/appropriate site assumptions, no universal guarantee) linking ripeness/sugar to potential alcohol. Existing ripening and heat/light principles provide context.

5. **Tasted flavour description**. `wset_guided_tasting_grids.yaml:16` onward supplies Level 1 colour/aroma and structural sensations; tasted flavour associations have no L1 field. `assets/study/guided_tasting.json:11` asks aromas and structure but not tasted flavours. Add original flavour observation/evidence at L1; append a new descriptor attribute/value vocabulary rather than redefine saved keys. No official SAT copying is needed. Source teaching already tells users to describe the palate (`ki_wset_taste_sequence_look`), so the main weakness is recording/calibration delivery.

6. **Access distinction**. `wset_scope.json` currently requires `physicalWines:1` for L1 app milestones. The official specification permits learners who do not sample alcohol to complete the qualification. Consider optional physical practice or a transparent non-alcohol/fictional observation alternative for early levels. Do not imply either app pathway replaces an official course or examination.
