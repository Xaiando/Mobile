# WSET Level 3 tasting and service application practice, 29 September 2026

This batch adds 40 original, item-specific four-option questions to existing cited core facts: 23 tasting observations and 17 service/storage observations. It adds no factual claim or certification mapping. Each choice is linked to a source already cited by its knowledge item. The questions require interpreting a concrete sample, service decision or limitation rather than recognizing an isolated keyword. Correct positions are balanced 10/10/10/10, and no keyed answer is uniquely the longest or shortest option. The underlying 4,125 knowledge items, including all 40 targets, remain unverified until qualified educator review.

The tasting questions distinguish clarity from colour intensity, aroma intensity from identity, primary/secondary/tertiary development, palate finish from lingering heat, tannin texture from amount, preference from quality, and evidence for drinking now or future development. The service questions cover temperature and glass controls, light and heat exposure, retained-sample comparison, sediment versus aeration, and handling fragile corks. Conditional prompts explicitly require assessment of the actual wine; no universal decanting time or certain future peak date is asserted.

Primary-source anchors already recorded in the dataset include:

- [WSET guidance on tasting and quality](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2019/november/how-to-build-your-wine-tasting-skills), [WSET guidance on ageing](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2023/march/21/why-do-we-age-wine), and the [WSET Diploma tasting assessment guide](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/6714/wset_l4diploma_cag_tasting_en_aug2018.pdf). The latter supports concepts but this app does not reproduce WSET's proprietary tasting grid or claim official marking.
- [OIV sensory-analysis conditions](https://www.oiv.int/sites/default/files/2022-09/review-on-sensory-analysis-of-wine_en_3.pdf), especially comparable sample temperatures, odour-free glasses, and neutral surroundings.
- [AWRI storage and transport guidance](https://www.awri.com.au/industry_support/winemaking_resources/storage-and-packaging/post-packaging/transport-and-storage/) and its [technical note on heat and light](https://www.awri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/TN09.pdf), including the need for physical, chemical and sensory evaluation of suspected heat damage.
- [Château Margaux's service guidance](https://chateau-margaux.com/en/mon-vin/service) on conditional aeration, sediment and fragile older wines, and [Taylor's Port service guidance](https://www.taylor.pt/en/enjoy-port-wine/how-to-serve-port) on gentle cork extraction.
- [AWRI's wine fault overview](https://www.awri.com.au/industry_support/winemaking_resources/sensory_assessment/recognition-of-wine-faults-and-taints/wine_faults/) for the context-dependent fault checks.
