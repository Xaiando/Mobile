# WSET Level 3 tasting and service core practice closure, 29 September 2026

This release adds 36 original four-option scenarios against **existing** cited and mapped facts: 12 tasting-fault observations, eight service principles, and four distinct decision parts for each of four service cases. The four case parts ask for an action, reason, tradeoff and limitation. The existing written cases remain available for self-review; objective choices do not mark free-form tasting or service performance.

The previous release's WSET Level 3 core practice was 1,546/2,232. The new baseline is 1,582/2,232. Tasting moves 166/178 to **178/178**, and service moves 78/102 to **102/102**, for useful app practice coverage in those two domains. CMS Certified rises 966/1,242 to 994/1,242 and WSET Level 4 rises 1,636/2,766 to 1,672/2,766 through existing shared mappings. WSET Level 2 stays 692/748. These counts describe served app formats, not official WSET or CMS completion, exam marking or professional competency.

The tasting scenarios distinguish 4-EP from 4-EG, explain why smoke or mustiness alone cannot diagnose one cause, keep detection separate from rejection, and assess delayed mousiness, matrix-dependent thresholds and the VA/ethyl acetate measurement distinction. Service prompts test food bitterness, fruitiness versus complexity, guest context, faults, flight order and table sequence. Each case decision remains conditional on the wine, dish and guest; no fixed aeration time or guaranteed pairing is keyed.

Every choice uses a source citation already attached to its knowledge item. Primary references checked for this batch include [AWRI's Brettanomyces FAQ](https://www.awri.com.au/industry_support/winemaking_resources/frequently_asked_questions/brettanomyces-faq/), [AWRI's mousiness technical article](https://www.awri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/s1694.pdf), [AWRI's volatile-acidity technical note](https://www.awri.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/TR-143.TECHNOTE.03.pdf), [WSET's food-matching guidance](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2020/december/21/winter-wine-and-food-matching), [WSET's serving and decanting guidance](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2024/july/21/frequently-asked-questions-about-serving-and-decanting-wine), [Bordeaux's service guidance](https://www.bordeaux.com/en/wine-tasting-basics/serve/) and [Château Margaux's service advice](https://chateau-margaux.com/en/mon-vin/service). The item-level citations remain in the existing area files. All 4,288 knowledge facts remain unverified pending qualified educator review.

Curriculum lint found zero errors and the three known curator-warning categories. The strict five-track baseline was regenerated for version 0.24.30. The focused closure test checks unique item coverage, attached HTTPS citations, balanced correct positions (9/9/9/9), non-extreme answer lengths and 178/178 tasting plus 102/102 service served core facts. Hosted CI and physical tasting/service evaluation remain independent release checks.
