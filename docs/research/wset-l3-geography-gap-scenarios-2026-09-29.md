# WSET Level 3 geography practice audit — 29 September 2026

The [WSET Level 3 Award in Wines specification, Issue 2 (2022)](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf), Unit 1 Learning Outcome 2 and its regional and law ranges (pp. 10–16), define the broad study context. The specification is not an answer source. These are original app practice questions, not WSET examination questions or a substitute for the written paper or tasting examination.

At PR31 head `74accc0`, WSET_L3 geography had **818/1,060 core items with useful practice**. There were 242 core geography gaps; 54 `LOCATED_IN` items already had a spatial format recorded and were deliberately excluded. This batch supplies 36 four-option, item-specific scenarios for distinct existing `PRINCIPLE_EXPLANATION` geography items, selected from the other 188 gaps: 25 regional site and variety comparisons and 11 origin or classification decisions. Of these 36, 28 have a direct Level 3 core mapping and eight enter Level 3 as inherited Level 2 core knowledge; seven of the 28 also have a Level 2 mapping. It adds no knowledge item, mapping, map geometry, or progress requirement. The answers test a regional or origin inference and explicitly avoid claiming a guaranteed bottle style or quality from the place name alone. The keyed positions are balanced 9/9/9/9; each option set is distinct, with no keyed answer uniquely longest or shortest. Mechanical balance does not prove educational difficulty or complete distractor validity.
