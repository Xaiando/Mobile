# General outcome closure: sources and delivery

Authored 27 September 2026 after the [independent production review](wset-general-production-independent-review.md). Scope comes from the current [Level 2 2026 Issue 2.1 specification](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/19132/wset_l2wines_specification_en_april2026_issue21.pdf), Outcomes 1–2, and [Level 3 May 2022 Issue 2 specification](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf), Unit 1 Outcome 1. The original explanations are educational editorial material, not official course text or copied examination material.

`assets/curriculum/areas/wset_outcome_closure.yaml` contains 20 original explanatory items. Fifteen have direct Level 2 mappings and all 20 have Level 3 mappings. The detailed OIV vineyard-proportion definition, two vine-structure explanations and two cap-operation definitions remain Level 3. Seven existing assertions gain Level 2 mappings without alteration or duplication:

- `ki_landwein_protection`, `ki_qualitaetswein_protection`, `ki_praedikatswein_protection`.
- `ki_wset_eu_alsace_vt_sgn`.
- `ki_wset_taste_ageing_not_all`, `ki_wset_taste_ageing_evolution`, `ki_wset_taste_ageing_uncertain`.

All new assertions retain `unverified` status, disable automatic MCQs, and use forward descriptive recall at minimum depth 2. The date records the source check, not an expert endorsement. No fact-memory state, review event or official result is created by ingestion. The machine-readable [evidence ledger](wset-outcome-closure-evidence.json) gives exact IDs, statements, levels, outcome dimension and source locators.

## Primary references checked

| Subject | Primary reference and exact support | Limits retained |
|---|---|---|
| Vintage, GI and old-vine wording | [WSET label explanation](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2019/how-to-read-wine-labels-part-one-common-features-on-wine-labels), GI/grape/vintage/other terms headings; [TTB 2025 label guidance](https://www.ttb.gov/system/files/2025-12/Part_1.5_Labeling_--_FINAL.pdf), PDF page 34 vintage conditions. | Harvest year differs from bottling; origin differs from grape. Vine age alone is not a quality promise. Different allowed harvest proportions are illustrated by primary rules without inventing a worldwide percentage. |
| OIV old-vine definitions | [OIV-VITI 703-2024](https://www.oiv.int/fr/node/3806), adopted definitions and harmonisation recommendation. | Individual vine, graft union and parcel criteria are distinguished from each other and from enforceable national label rules. |
| EU wine PDO/PGI | [European Commission schemes explanation](https://agriculture.ec.europa.eu/farming/geographical-indications-and-quality-schemes/geographical-indications-and-quality-schemes-explained_en), wine-specific clauses. | EU wine origin rules are not applied to every country or confused with the food-product one-stage explanation. Sensory preference is separate from protected status. |
| Late harvest | [CIVA AOC explanation](https://www.vinsalsace.com/fr/gouts-et-couleurs/aoc/vendanges-tardives/) supplies delayed harvesting of overripe fruit; the public [WSET session plan](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/13022/wset_l2wines_sessionplans_en_may2023_issue2.pdf), PDF pages 29–31 and 44, supplies elementary harvest/sweetness distinctions and French vocabulary. Existing Alsace explanation is reused. | The 2023 plan is not used to define current 2026 course scope. Generic vocabulary does not grant one worldwide named-origin permission or assume all late-harvest wine is botrytised. No claim that every Alsace VT may be dry is made. |
| Ripeness and flavour | [WSET comparative style guidance](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2025/how-to-find-a-wine-youll-like), fruit-character sections; [WSET geography explanation](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2021/august/24/how-does-geography-affect-a-wine-s-style), ripeness comparison. | Descriptors are conditional on variety and site, not a universal colour-of-fruit maturity test. Blanket tannin/price/old-vine-law statements in the lifestyle article are not adopted. |
| Phenolic maturity | [BC Wine Grape Council](https://bpg.bcwgc.org/harvest-quality/fruit-sampling-and-maturity/), Grape Seeds and Aroma/Flavour and Phenolic Compounds. | Different processes can mature at different rates. No universal sugar index, seed-colour threshold, extraction dose or quality score is adopted. |
| Vine structure | [Iowa State Extension](https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/how-to/how-prune-grapevines-home-garden), shoot/cane/trunk/arm/cordon terminology; existing [NMSU H-338](https://pubs.nmsu.edu/_h/H338/), growth, photosynthesis, buds, flowers and tendrils. | One-year wood is retained previous growth, distinct from the perennial framework and current green growth. The home-garden pruning recipe is not transplanted into every commercial vineyard. |
| Oak size and toast | Original [Seguin Moreau technical brochure](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5ce302f4858d840001a7e524/t/5d1402e83cc1ff0001e996c7/1561592554884/french-oak-range.pdf), PDF page 4 volume effect; [cooperage R&D explanation](https://www.seguin-moreau.fr/en/wine-ranges/barrels-wine/), toast levels; existing Iowa oak reference. | The physical contact mechanism is conditioned on comparable shape/wood/age/toast/time. No product endorsement, commercial quality claim or automatic heavy-toast ranking. |
| Blending complexity | [WSET blending explanation](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2024/why-do-we-blend-wine/), How are wines blended? and Enhance complexity. | Complementary layers differ from consistency and balance; more components are not automatically better. Regional law and questionable Sherry examples elsewhere in this article are not adopted. |
| Pump over and punch down | [Winemakers Research Exchange](https://winemakersresearchexchange.com/library/fermentation/cap-management-punch-downs-and-pump-overs/), definitions and original research context. | Methods, timing and intensity matter. No universal aeration schedule or assumption that either method is always gentler. |
| Bottle colour | Existing [WSET 2018 explanatory tasting guide](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/6714/wset_l4diploma_cag_tasting_en_aug2018.pdf), printed page 11 general colour-development discussion. Existing [WSET ageing explanation](https://www.wsetglobal.com/knowledge-centre/blog/2023/march/21/why-do-we-age-wine) supports reused development/limits. | Only original elementary observations are taught. No proprietary grid, descriptor matrix, marking scheme or worked example is reproduced. This older reference is not presented as current examination guidance. Colour cannot date a wine precisely. |

## Verification and remaining integration

The pack's IDs, source references, reused item IDs and mapping levels were checked statically against the local authored dataset. New citation URLs were checked for canonical duplicates; existing H-338, oak, ageing and colour sources are reused, and the stronger CIVA AOC page is added. The meaningful regression test at `test/core/curriculum/wset_outcome_closure_test.dart` loads the actual registered bundled curriculum, ingests it into the app database and checks currentness, level membership and the actual served recall modes. It also checks that ingestion does not manufacture learner reviews. The parent task owns registration, the required-outcome catalog and SDK execution; this author ran no SDK or Git commands.

These lessons close the explicit general gaps found by this review. They do not certify that every named regional style/quality/price application is complete; that remains the integrated catalog and regional evidence review. App study coverage and learner practice remain separate from an officially awarded WSET qualification.
