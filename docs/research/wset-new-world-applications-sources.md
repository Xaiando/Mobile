# New World regional application source ledger

Authored and sources checked 27 September 2026. Original prose; all items remain unverified and MCQ-disabled pending qualified review. WSET specifications define educational scope, not factual authority or certification. This supplement augments the frozen regional matrix with actual named production/style consequences, Western Cape environment, conditional cost/quality comparisons, and common Argentina/New Zealand origin mechanisms.

A supplied vineyard or lot condition is part of an original thought experiment. Regional sources establish the named growing/grape context; technical sources explain transferable mechanisms. No generic equation or barrel choice is evidence that an entire region routinely uses that practice, earns a premium, or has a particular quality score. Numeric prices and transient acreage rankings are not taught.

Argentina Law 25.163 provides the qualitative IP/IG/DOC framework. INV’s current origin department and public register were cross-checked; the revised 37/2025 digesto entered into force on 1 January 2026. We do not teach obsolete label thresholds or copy old typography rules. Luján IG and DOC remain separate claims. New Zealand ordinary single variety/vintage/area claims use regulation 43(2); specified export exceptions are acknowledged. Enduring country/island GIs differ from smaller registered origins and from applications. Protected origin is not a required variety or sensory-quality grade.

## New public sources

- **src_wset_nwa_za_climate** — [Western Cape climate and site influences](https://www.wosa.co.za/The-Industry/Terroir/Climate/), Wines of South Africa. Locator: Climate; Sea Breezes; qualitative seasonal and site mechanisms only.
- **src_wset_nwa_ar_origin_law** — [Law 25.163: Argentina wine-origin designations](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-60510/texto), Honorable Congreso de la Nación Argentina. Locator: Articles 1–6, 13–20 and 29–34; qualitative IP/IG/DOC distinctions, not label percentages.
- **src_wset_nwa_ar_origin_register** — [Recognised and protected Argentine geographical indications and DOCs](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/i.g._y_d.o.c._de_la_republica_argentina.pdf), Instituto Nacional de Vitivinicultura. Locator: Five-page register, named IG/DOC rows; no claim that the list is exhaustive forever.
- **src_wset_nwa_ar_origin_department** — [Protection of origin: IG and DOC](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/inv/proteccion-del-origen), Instituto Nacional de Vitivinicultura. Locator: Current INV responsibility and register links; checked 27 September 2026.
- **src_wset_nwa_nz_gi_guide** — [Using the New Zealand geographical indications register](https://www.iponz.govt.nz/get-ip/geographical-indications/using-the-geographical-indications-register/), Intellectual Property Office of New Zealand. Locator: Enduring GIs and separate application/registration status.
- **src_wset_nwa_nz_marlborough_gi** — [Marlborough wine geographical indication, 1004](https://www.iponz.govt.nz/get-ip/geographical-indications/register/marlborough/), Intellectual Property Office of New Zealand. Locator: Registered status, IP 1004; no premium-price or compulsory-variety conclusion.
- **src_wset_nwa_nz_martinborough_gi** — [Martinborough wine geographical indication, 1011](https://www.iponz.govt.nz/get-ip/geographical-indications/register/martinborough/), Intellectual Property Office of New Zealand. Locator: Registered status, IP 1011.
- **src_wset_nwa_nz_label_rules** — [Wine Regulations 2021: New Zealand grape-wine label statements](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/regulation/public/2021/0401/41.0/whole.html), New Zealand Parliamentary Counsel Office. Locator: Regulation 43(2), ordinary single variety/vintage/area statement; specified export exceptions in 43(7) retained.
