# WSET Level 3 viticulture case practice — source and coverage audit

This release adds 40 item-specific authored choices to the app's 40 core Level 3 viticulture `CASE_*` mappings. Each case part has a separate question: 10 actions, 13 reasons, 7 tradeoffs and 10 limitations. The answer stays within the existing mapped assertion; an item-linked primary citation supports the mechanism or regional context. The supplied case conditions, not an external source, establish the particular vineyard observations. All mapped facts remain `unverified` pending qualified expert review.

The choices avoid unsupported final harvest dates, fixed irrigation doses, promised fruit or wine quality, guaranteed disease control and blanket training conversions. The limitation prompts make missing measurements explicit. Answer positions are balanced 10/10/10/10, all four options are distinct, and no correct option is uniquely longest.
