# WSET application practice, release 0.24.9

This release adds 104 original, item-specific four-option questions to facts already in the curriculum: 67 European origin and style decisions, 16 New World decisions, and 21 winemaking decisions. Every answer cites a public primary source attached to its existing knowledge item. All 4,125 knowledge items remain `unverified` pending qualified educator review; registration and automated checks do not confer factual verification.

The European set covers French origin categories; Burgundy, Chablis and Bordeaux labels; Loire, Rhône and Alsace styles; Italian denominations; Iberian labels; and Central and Eastern European examples. The New World set tests broad named GI, grape, climate and style associations without requiring finer legal or subregional detail at Level 2. The winemaking set tests source-bounded effects of fermentation, pressing, vessels, rosé routes, sweetening, blending, fining, malolactic conversion and stability. Choice alternatives were reviewed for a single supported answer and balanced wording. The original claim and its caveats remain in each item's assertion and linked source.

Source repairs made with the questions: the Portuguese DOC/IGP record now points to ViniPortugal's certified-wines page and the IVV region map; Collio uses its own denomination page rather than a Colli Orientali citation; Terre Siciliane style points to the EU legal notice; Chablis Premier Cru grouping points to BIVB's appellation sheet; Recioto di Soave uses the consortium's production rules; and the red-pressing, blending-objective and vintage-consistency references now use direct AWRI, OIV and Wine Australia pages. Existing identical source URLs were reused rather than duplicated. The linked sources support the bounded answers; a conditional cost, quality or flavour outcome is not presented as a legal guarantee.

Level 2 syllabus depth was checked against the [April 2026 WSET Level 2 specification](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/19132/wset_l2wines_specification_en_april2026_issue21.pdf); Level 3 depth against the [2022 Level 3 specification](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/11731/wset_l3wines_specification_en_highres_may2022_issue2.pdf). Thirteen granular European questions, nine New World questions and four winemaking questions remain unregistered while their track mappings, simpler prerequisite facts or sources are corrected. In particular, a Greek three-PDO comparison needs three primary citations, which the single-citation authored-choice cue cannot express. The detailed European Level 2 remap and New World mapping plans remain research drafts outside the bundled release.

Measured useful **core** practice rose from **440/744 to 490/744** at WSET Level 2 and from **1,045/2,228 to 1,149/2,228** at Level 3. Level 1 remains **132/132**. These are app item counts, not official WSET completion or exam-readiness results. Core causal reasoning is still zero at Levels 2 and 3, so the broader application requirement remains open.

Validation at registration: curriculum lint passed 157 included files with zero errors and three existing curator warnings; `flutter analyze --no-pub` passed; the focused Europe, New World, production and authored-choice tests passed after the latter's release-count assertions were updated. Exact-head full regression and browser smoke remain pending for this release.
