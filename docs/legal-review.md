# Legal and Licensing Review Register

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Open. Every item must be reviewed by qualified counsel **before any public release**. |
| **Decision** | D10: development does not wait for legal review. This register tracks what must be cleared later. |
| **Nature** | Engineering flags, not legal advice. |

## Rules in force now

- No proprietary WSET or CMS exam questions, syllabus text or tasting-grid artwork is reproduced (D10).
- Curriculum facts cite authoritative public sources and stay `unverified` until reviewed. Generated content is never treated as authoritative without sources (D3).
- The product specification PDF stays out of the repository, and the project is proprietary with no open-source license (D8).

Côte de Fontenay is explicitly a Fontenay-près-Chablis reference, not a precise vineyard marker. US interior reference points derive from the pinned UC Davis AVA Project CC0 polygons; they do not replace the TTB legal descriptions. Thirteen further New World locality/terrain markers derive from GeoNames national gazetteers under CC BY 4.0. Their layer credits GeoNames, links the licence, records the source edition/hash and discloses the selected reference-point derivation. These snapshots are kept separate from CC0 sources. See the [France](../tool/geography/france_atlas_completion_sources.md) and [New World](../tool/geography/new_world_atlas_completion_sources.md) notes.

Diploma scope is pinned separately from curriculum fact sources. Its short objectives and six workstreams use original editorial descriptions. Track activation inherits lower-level study; it does not reproduce the proprietary Diploma tasting form or establish assessment readiness. The gap audit keeps unfinished analytical, tasting, business and research content visible.

## Additional 0.11.0 source handling

Wine Australia's ArcGIS item owned by `WineAustralia` expressly licenses its GI spatial data CC BY (Attribution) 4.0. The authoring tool rejects other licence classes. Derived interior reference points carry attribution and per-feature source geometry hashes; the publisher's legal textual definitions prevail over its GIS interpretation. New GeoNames markers retain separate CC BY 4.0 snapshots; Wikidata/UC Davis markers remain CC0. [Australian/South African source notes](../tool/geography/new_world_oceania_africa_sources.md) record editions, hashes and derivations.

The NZ planting questions author twelve bounded factual ranks from observed 2025 planted hectares in NZ Winegrowers' 2026 report. The app does not bundle the report, its tables or illustrations. Prompts preserve the observation year and measure, independently of legal grape permissions. [Research notes](../tool/geography/new_world_plantings_sources.md).

Learner progress uses original topic labels and expressly incomplete app coverage judgments. Self-reported WSET exam passes are learner settings, independently reversible and backed up; they are not verified credentials or an automatic result derived from app reviews. No examiner questions, assessment rubric or tasting form is reproduced.
