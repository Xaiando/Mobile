# Learner progress

`WsetProgressRepository` derives progress from current knowledge items, the study planner's cumulative mappings, available question formats, shared review states and the append-only review log. It creates no curriculum facts and requires no schema migration.

A fact is studied after its first review. For current mastery it must be in the FSRS Review state, have stability of at least seven days and package-calculated retrievability of at least 0.90. It must also have Good/Easy reviews on at least three distinct UTC calendar dates spanning seven days, all after its most recent Again. Repeated taps or changes of question format cannot satisfy the date requirement. A reverse-question failure and subsequent memory decay can reduce mastery. The screen refreshes after database changes and once a minute while visible. Its stable stream retains the previous result during refresh, serializes calculations and coalesces overlapping requests. The minute timer starts only after the first successful result; Home uses a static initial loading message, so optional progress calculations do not keep the rest of the interface animating.

Available-material bars use the available fact count and say so explicitly. All current mapped facts are counted separately; mapped facts without a served question remain visible as unavailable coverage gaps. Expired and superseded facts leave current denominators without deleting their review history. Empty material has no percentage and cannot complete any app milestone. Each current fact counts once within a level; its same review state counts in every cumulative level that includes it.

The app-owned scope asset `assets/progress/wset_scope.json` marks all four levels incomplete. The integrated 0.20.5 audit on 27 September 2026 has no Level 1 mappings, 103 cumulative Level 2 facts, 2,381 Level 3 facts and 2,885 Level 4 facts. Vineyard/winery, fault-control and 41 causal-reasoning points across eight exercises support D1; general business, including 32 additional producer-model/channel points, supports D2; diagnostic tasting foundations support D3. Explicit editorial selectors route 704 regional comparison points to D3, 58 sparkling points to D4 and 57 fortified points to D5, while service foundations remain supporting material. Regional vineyard, winery and commercial points stay with D3 ahead of broad domain matching. Advanced cases retain depth-3 mappings; lower levels inherit only their authored foundation mappings. These are available app material, not full syllabus denominators. The runtime queries the installed curriculum rather than freezing these numbers. An app-level study milestone requires a nonempty scope explicitly marked complete, no unavailable mapped facts, and all mapped facts currently mastered. Mastering an incomplete available pack is a separate material milestone.

Diploma D1–D6 topic groups assign each mapped fact to at most one group. Optional explicit `itemIds` take precedence over selected regional facts, which take precedence over broad domains. This allows a specialist production or business point to support D4/D5 without pretending its subject is a location. Rutherglen's regional selector contributes location context only, because the region also makes other wine styles; Douro is not automatically classified as Port. Dedicated Rutherglen/Port production items are explicitly assigned to D5. A named grape statistic remains in the geography domain. Unassigned material is displayed separately. Topic groups measure supporting facts, not official unit completion. The parser rejects contradictory complete/gap declarations, invalid/duplicate unit IDs and malformed or repeated item IDs across groups. Runtime checks reject unknown region/item selectors. Referenced retired items remain valid scope references but leave current denominators.

Exam pass declarations are independent, optional and labelled self-reported. `exam_pass_wset_l1` through `exam_pass_wset_l4` use the existing user_settings table. They can be checked or unchecked independently; a higher-level declaration does not silently set lower levels. The existing backup format exports and imports these arbitrary setting keys. Resetting study reviews retains settings; erasing all learner data removes declarations. No pass is inferred from app scores or fact mastery.

Coverage notes are original editorial judgments informed by current public qualification outlines: [Level 1](https://www.wsetglobal.com/qualifications/wset-level-1-award-in-wines), [Level 2](https://www.wsetglobal.com/qualifications/wset-level-2-award-in-wines), [Level 3](https://www.wsetglobal.com/qualifications/wset-level-3-award-in-wines), and the [Diploma specification, August 2025 issue 1.4](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/17609/wset_l4wines_specification_en_august-2025.pdf). These sources establish broad topics and six mandatory Diploma units; they do not endorse this app's authored mappings or mastery threshold. Fact reviews do not assess the full tasting, written or independent research requirements.

Tests exercise empty scopes, inheritance, shared formats, spaced dates, reverse lapses, decay, unservable facts, expiry, unit grouping, invalid scope metadata, app milestones, self-reported pass export/import/reset/erase and narrow-screen navigation. A controlled slow calculation checks that initial Home loading settles, no timer interrupts the initial result, refresh retains visible data, overlapping ticks do not start parallel queries and unmounting cancels future calculations.
