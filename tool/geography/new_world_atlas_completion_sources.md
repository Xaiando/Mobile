## United States

The addition and the existing world atlas together cover the 17 Napa study areas, the Sonoma list in `docs/content/subregion-atlas.md`, all 11 Willamette Valley nested AVAs, all 11 Paso Robles districts, the Santa Ynez nested areas, and the listed Washington areas. The addition also supplies key Lodi districts, Monterey and Arroyo Seco, Anderson Valley, Santa Cruz Mountains, Southern Oregon and additional Washington AVAs.

The authoring script matches every US AVA to its TTB register row through its exact CFR section. When a parent is another AVA, it accepts only a fully containing entry. It rejects entries marked as partial overlaps. This matters for Los Carneros and Wild Horse Valley: neither is asserted wholly inside Napa Valley. The former retains its authored California parent; the latter is located within North Coast. Petaluma Gap, Rockpile and Pine Mountain-Cloverdale Peak likewise use fully containing North Coast rather than incorrectly treating partial overlaps as full containment. Fort Ross-Seaview uses West Sonoma Coast, as listed in the current TTB register. Multistate Columbia Gorge uses United States, rather than an exclusive Washington or Oregon parent.

US markers are derived from the **CC0** community polygon dataset maintained by the [UC Davis Library and DataLab American Viticultural Areas Project](https://github.com/UCDavisLibrary/ava). They are not official TTB boundary shapes. The pinned revision is `7af5b29d45aee5e6c6ce3889e9b1de19085d7107`; the raw [aggregate file](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/UCDavisLibrary/ava/7af5b29d45aee5e6c6ce3889e9b1de19085d7107/avas_aggregated_files/avas.geojson) has SHA-256 `c90068f7154764519f0a7c6fb2788e597b7c56bf7581e8c0faa979554d984926`. The project [licence](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/UCDavisLibrary/ava/7af5b29d45aee5e6c6ce3889e9b1de19085d7107/LICENSE) is CC0 1.0.

The algorithm takes 63 evenly spaced horizontal scanlines through each source polygon, computes ring intersections, rejects holes, and chooses the midpoint of the widest verified interior interval. It then checks the result against the original polygon. This produces an actual source-derived interior reference marker without tracing a boundary, extrapolating a centroid or shifting a municipality to a guessed vineyard. Every feature records the source commit, raw-file hash, AVA ID, CFR index and derivation. The marker shows location only; it must not be presented as a legal border or an official centroid.

## Southern Hemisphere coverage

Australia now includes every registered subregion appearing in the Wine Australia hierarchy: High Eden was already present; the addition supplies Lenswood, Piccadilly Valley, Broke Fordwich, Pokolbin, Upper Hunter Valley, Swan Valley, Albany, Denmark, Frankland River, Mount Barker, Porongurup, Nagambie Lakes and Great Western. The Great Western item asserts geographic nesting only, not unrestricted rights to use its protected name. It also supplies the missing Fleurieu and Hunter Valley zones, and important additional regions such as Langhorne Creek, Padthaway, Wrattonbully, Riverland, Rutherglen, Heathcote, Macedon Ranges, Geelong, King Valley, Grampians, Goulburn Valley, Mudgee, Orange, Riverina, Tumbarumba, Canberra District and Pemberton. Some of these use the fully containing state instead of adding every intermediate zone. The addition does not claim coverage of every Australian registered region or zone.

The combined NZ geography covers all 19 currently registered regional/local wine GIs in the IPONZ list, plus the seven listed Central Otago informal study areas and Marlborough's three informal valleys. The statutory country/North Island/South Island enduring GIs are not introduced as additional wine-region questions. Informal subregions are typed `informal_area`, and their location items cite [New Zealand Winegrowers' Central Otago](https://www.nzwine.com/en/regions/centralotago/) and [Marlborough](https://www.nzwine.com/en/regions/marlborough/) descriptions. Cromwell Basin cites the [Central Otago District Council's tourism account](https://www.centralotagonz.com/discover/our-stories/the-cromwell-story/). Lowburn, Pisa and Bendigo have Central Otago as their containing region; the source does not establish a legally nested informal hierarchy, so none is invented.

South Africa includes all eight Stellenbosch wards in the current authority list, and all three Hemel-en-Aarde wards. It also adds key Cape Town, Durbanville, Franschhoek, Wellington, Tulbagh, Darling, Breede River Valley, Breedekloof, Robertson, Worcester, Klein Karoo, Calitzdorp, Cape Agulhas, Elim, Bot River and Overberg areas. The current formal district for Constantia is Cape Town; the existing broader Coastal Region relationship remains geographically true. Adding a geography node does not imply a complete register of every South African ward.

Argentina adds the listed Los Chacayes, San Pablo and Pampa El Cepillo GIs, plus Agrelo, Las Compuertas, Vista Flores, San Rafael, San Juan, Valle del Pedernal, Patagonia and the Calchaquí Valleys. Gualtallary is an **informal area**, not an asserted registered GI: it is absent from the current INV protected-origin list. Its Tupungato location is corroborated by [SENASA's locality record](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/disposicion_dnpv_472-2019_anexo_i.pdf), and wine-growing geography by [Zuccardi's primary Gualtallary origin sheet](https://zuccardiwines.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/FT-ESP-ALUVIONAL-GUALTALLARY-2021.pdf). Los Chacayes uses the sourced El Manzano Histórico locality marker, whose location in that district is described by the [municipal tourism authority](https://tunuyan.gov.ar/vivitunuyan/atractivos/tierra-de-chacayes/); it does not incorrectly use the homonymous Los Chacayes settlement in San Juan.

## Marker licences and qualifications

`new_world_atlas_completion_points.geojson` contains **161 CC0 markers**: 83 UC Davis polygon-derived points and 78 Wikidata gazetteer points. Each Wikidata feature records the entity ID, exact nondeprecated Earth `P625` statement ID, precision, entity download URL, title and description. Structured Wikidata data are [CC0](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing). A gazetteer municipality, settlement or landform remains exactly at its published coordinate and is labelled as a reference point, not an official GI centroid. References at different hierarchy levels do not imply coextensive legal areas.

`new_world_atlas_completion_gazetteer_points.geojson` contains **13 CC BY 4.0 markers** selected from [GeoNames national WGS84 gazetteer exports](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/). Their [licence and coordinate system](https://www.geonames.org/about.html) allow commercial redistribution with attribution. Each feature contains its exact GeoNames ID, feature class/code, modification date, source file hash, attribution and qualification. The checked-in point snapshot, rather than the mutable daily national export, is the build input.

## Reproduction and verification

Run `node tool/geography/new_world_atlas_completion.mjs --author` from the repository root with the authoring caches present. The script preserves the exact pinned UC Davis revision, checks US legal nesting, checks all source markers and authors only its owned new file. Without `--author`, it regenerates only the two point snapshots. Wikidata entity lookups are cached and paced; `--refresh` requests current statements for an intentional source update. GeoNames selections require the exact cached national TXT snapshots; never replace an edition silently without updating provenance and reviewing the points.

The ordinary geography pipeline consumes the checked-in GeoJSON snapshots offline after the root agent registers their sources and recipes. It does not depend on authoring caches or a live Wikidata/GeoNames endpoint. Basic checks confirmed 174 unique node IDs, 174 location items, 348 both-track mappings, 174 finite WGS84 points, one point per new node, and no duplicate coordinates among same-type new nodes or between them and the existing world atlas. The source hierarchy types require one new `LOCATED_IN` signature, `informal_area → appellation`, included in the owned curriculum file.

Remaining atlas-plan work includes exact open legal boundary shapes, rivers and landforms as study nodes, neighbour/order/river-bank drills, other US AVAs and the remaining Australian zones/regions, additional South African wards and broader country registers. Expert validation and production release checks remain separate from successful source authoring.
