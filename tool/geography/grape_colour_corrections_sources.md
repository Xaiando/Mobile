# Pinot Gris physical berry colour

The primary [Pl@ntGrape Pinot gris G record, number 217](https://www.plantgrape.fr/en/varieties/fruit-varieties/217/export), maintained by INRAE, IFV and Institut Agro Montpellier, explicitly describes ripe berry skin as grey (PDF page 1, Description elements). It also identifies the variety as a grey mutation of Pinot noir. Retrieved 26 September 2026; the retrieved export displays an edit date of 25 September 2026.

The inherited `germany.yaml` white classification was incorrect physical metadata. Its edge is preserved with `valid_until: 2026-09-26`; the corrected grey edge starts that day in `grape_colour_corrections.yaml`. These dates record the app's correction, not a claim that the variety changed colour in 2026. No historical white-colour knowledge item is added.

The new colour item cites the primary cultivar description, stays unverified pending qualified review, and serves typed/flashcard recall from WSET Level 2. Gray is an alternative English spelling of the existing Grey answer node. Current colour recall must reject the retired erroneous White value; the focused regression checks this separately from legal grape unions.

Pinot Gris and Sauvignon Gris are the two currently curated grey-skinned varieties. They cannot provide three defensible wrong grey-grape choices. Multiple choice is disabled for four existing French/Austrian Pinot Gris permission items and the dated Central Otago planting item; typed recall, flashcards and eligible map questions remain. The colour fact itself is also MCQ-disabled because the small berry-colour answer set cannot provide three rivals.

Only original factual paraphrases and a source link are distributed. No publisher text extracts, photographs, PDF or geometries are bundled. The correction introduces no coordinate or geometry licensing requirements.
